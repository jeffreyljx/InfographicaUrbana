## Conventions settled in Phase B

- One continuous state in several Cliopatria rows (Roman Empire, Eastern Roman Empire, Byzantine Empire; Georgia 567-576 = Lazica) is handed over between dossiers with a `dissolve`/`found` pair at the row boundary and a note; the colour agent gives the rows one colour (rule 2).
- Stateless land (the Sclaveni of the Balkan interior from 630) goes to `nobody`.
- A page budget per state: up to 3 shapes 1-2 pages, up to 12 shapes up to 5, up to 30 up to 9, more up to 14; unverified keyframes get verdict ok with the note "unverified: page budget". Reviewers judge `thin` against the budget.

## Gap round (1 Nov 2026)

A cross-dossier audit after the main round found states that two or more dossiers cite (as a counterpart, predecessor or successor) but that nobody wrote. Eight gap batches (`g-eu-a`, `g-eu-b`, `g-eu-c`, `g-sa-a`, `g-sa-b`, `g-sa-c`, `g-af`, `g-ea`; briefs in `briefs/g-*.md`, made from the `-x` brief template) ran 08:35-11:20 UTC with the same research, adversarial review and fix pipeline (workflow `cartahistoria-gaps-v6`). The round wrote 44 new dossiers with 295 events, and all of them validate and resolve. `syrian-city-states` was not written: it is a collective name, not a state. `first-deva-dynasty` was split from `deva-dynasty`. Reviewer verdicts were 12 pass, 5 fixed by the reviewer, 12 thin (minor states, within their page budget) and 15 failed, which went back to their authors for repair. 47 other cited slugs were misspellings of existing dossiers. A Sonnet agent matched 33 of them, and the orchestrator 2 more; the referring dossiers were corrected, as listed in `notes/phase-c/aliases-2026-11-01.csv`.
