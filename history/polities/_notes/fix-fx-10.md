# fix-fx-10 notes (1 Nov 2026)

One line per finding. All keyframe and ledger edits are in mughal-empire.json unless named; `without X` means the Mughal keyframe (or event region) is now `minus [shape, X]`, X being the other state's own shape spec. Pages were opened in this run (en.wikipedia.org/wiki/...).

- F055 kalachuris-of-ratnapura / mughal-empire (fixed): Chhattisgarh page says the Ratnapur-Raipur rulers only "acknowledged the nominal overlordship of the Mughals when they arrived"; Kalachuris_of_Ratnapura has no Mughal rule and "accepted the suzerainty of the Maratha Nagpur Kingdom in 1740". Nominal overlordship is not Mughal land, so the Mughal keyframes 1659-1741 (21, plus the new 1740 split; 257 cells) are now without Ratnapura's 1114 shape; hierarchy.csv has no Mughal row for it. Pages: Chhattisgarh, Kalachuris_of_Ratnapura.
- F104 garhwal-kingdom / mughal-empire (fixed): Garhwal_Kingdom page: infobox "Tributary of the Mughal Empire (1655 onwards)", Rani Karnavati "repelled an attack of Mughal army led by Najabat Khan", no Mughal occupation; Najib-ud-daula took Dehradun in 1757, Garhwal regained the Dun in 1770. Mughal keyframes 1588-1769 are without Garhwal's shape (the 1757 shape without the Dun for 1757-1768, the full 1770 shape for the 1769 keyframe, which runs into 1771). Page: Garhwal_Kingdom.
- F110 kumaon-kingdom / mughal-empire (fixed): Kumaon_Kingdom page: "mostly remained independent and sovereign but maintained a tributary relationship", "sovereign from the river Tons to the river Karnali", nothing on Mughal garrisons in the hills. Mughal keyframes 1588-1769 are without Kumaon's own shape of that year (hills and Doti; with the Terai in 1638-1664 and 1673-1740, as the Kumaon ledger has it). 21 Terai cells stay overlapped in the 1626 and 1662 keyframes (Kumaon's Terai gain of 1638 and loss of 1665 are dated only in its own ledger) and 7 in 1741; the size rule keeps them with Kumaon. Page: Kumaon_Kingdom.
- F112 cutch-state / mughal-empire (fixed): Cutch_State page: the only Mughal act is the 1719 invasion by the Mughal viceroy of Gujarat, which "was defeated"; Kutch_district: Khengarji's line "had a good relationship with the Gujarat Sultanate and Mughals". No page shows Mughal rule, so the 31 Mughal keyframes 1579-1741 are without Cutch's 1549 shape. The Gujarat events of 1535 and 1572-73 still name Gujarat as a whole (ledger 6, 35-37); keyframe snaps release Kutch at each keyframe year. Pages: Cutch_State, Kutch_district.
- F153 afsharid-iran / mughal-empire (fixed for 1741 and 1744, partly for 1738): Nader_Shah%27s_invasion_of_India: "annexes all lands west of the Indus river" (infobox), "ceded back all territories to the east of the Indus, which he had overrun, to Muhammad Shah", left Delhi 16 May 1739; Nader "captured Kandahar" from the Ghilji. Mughal 1738 keyframe split: 1738-1739 without Kandahar, new 1740 keyframe also without everything west of the Indus; 1741 and 1744 without the land west of the Indus, 1741 also restores the Punjab, Islamabad and Azad Kashmir east of it. afsharid-iran 1741 and 1744 keyframes now also without that east-of-Indus land (340 and 4 cells were in both shapes). Left: 51 Ghazni/Peshawar-side cells of the 1738 keyframe are Mughal until the June and November 1738 events; the 1738 conflict also counts 330 Afghan highland cells (Bamyan, Baghlan, Samangan...) that the Mughal keyframes 1545-1737 carry from Cliopatria and that no page I opened dates, so they are not touched. Page: Nader_Shah%27s_invasion_of_India.
