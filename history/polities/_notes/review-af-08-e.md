# Review of batch af-08-e (2026-10-24)

Adversarial review of the nine dossiers of af-08-e (six owned, three counterparts). Validator: 0 errors on all nine; warnings only for the four bantustan regions and counterparts in south-africa and the open-ended contests of republic-of-sudan and rapid-support-forces (ongoing at 2024). Pages were re-opened by the reviewer through WebFetch; quotations below are as the relay gave them.

Verdicts: italian-africa **fail**; tangier-international-zone **fix** (fixed by the reviewer); republic-of-the-rif **pass**; south-africa **thin**; kingdom-of-libya **pass**; republic-of-sudan **fail**; emirate-of-cyrenaica **pass**; sudan-people-s-liberation-movement **fail**; rapid-support-forces **fail**.

## italian-africa: fail
1. **Libya 1912-1929 is drawn as land held, the pages say it was not.** K3 (1912) keeps Cliopatria's Libya, about 0.88 M km2 of the 1.47 M km2 shape (Tripolitania, northern Cyrenaica, western Fezzan) and calls it 'the coastal strip'; /ledger/11 gains the same land. Italo-Turkish War page: 'The Italian soldiers were in effect besieged in seven enclaves on the coasts of Tripolitania and Cyrenaica', Tripoli's 'extended barely 15 kilometers'; 'After the Treaty of Ouchy, the Senussi order controlled most of Libya.' Fix: cut K3-K5 (and the Libyan part of K6) to the coastal enclaves; make /ledger/11 gain only them (the rest as a contest against the Senussi Order or nobody); add the interior as the Pacification page dates it (1923-24, Sirtica 1924, Fezzan 1930, Kufra 1931, Cyrenaica January 1932). The author's resume notes already describe this.
2. **/ledger/12 (1920 western Fezzan, confidence 1) contradicts the pages.** Its note says the Italian Tripolitania page has the Fezzan lost in 1914 and retaken 1929-30; the Pacification page: 'In 1930, Italian forces conquered Fezzan'. Delete it and remove the Fezzan from K4-K6.
3. **Tigray annexation missing.** First Italo-Ethiopian War page: Baratieri 'occupied Adwa on April 2' (1895) and issued a proclamation 'annexing Tigray province into Italian Eritrea'; Adwa 1 March 1896. Add gain 1895-4 and loss 1896-3 against ethiopian-empire.
4. **1914-1922 collapse to the coast** not on the ledger (Pacification page: in the First World War the Libyans 'collaborated again with the Ottomans to expel Italian troops'). Add contest zones or the 1914 Fezzan gain/loss.
5. **Campaign of the Sultanates**: no contest pair (majeerteen-sultanate has 1925-10 to 1927); Hobyo gain 1927 against the Hobyo dossier's 1 October 1925 occupation (confidence 3). Agree a date.
6. **1940-1943**: Kassala, Moyale, the 1940 invasion of Egypt, Operation Compass and the Cyrenaica swings, and the Free French Fezzan (french-africa 1943-1) are missing.
7. /ledger/27 (British Somaliland recaptured, 1941, confidence 1): british-africa cites 'British recapture: 8 April 1941' (British Somaliland infobox).
Confirmed on the pages: /ledger/3-5, 6-7, 13, 14, 17, 20-22, 24, 26, 28, 30, 31. Area: Italian Empire infobox 3,798,000 km2 (1938) against Cliopatria 1936 3,329,655 (0.88), acknowledged in K10.

## republic-of-sudan: fail
1. K5 (2014) and K6 (2024) are 'ok' and keep Darfur and Gezira although /ledger/16-17 lose them to the RSF (October and December 2023, about a quarter of the state); K5 calls them 'overlays'. The worked example cuts rebel land from the government keyframes (tang-dynasty_755). Add a 2023 keyframe and adjust K6, or recast the losses as contests.
2. /ledger/7 (1997, Central and Western Equatoria to the SPLM) leaves no trace in K3 (1992-2010), and nothing returns the land in 2005.
3. Missing: July 1992 government offensive ('seized many parts of southern Sudan, and captured the SPLA headquarters in Torit'), already quoted in the dossier's war note.
4. Missing: the RSF's 2024 advances on the cited page (Al-Fulah, West Kordofan, 20 June 2024; Singa 29 June 2024 and Dinder, Mazmoum, Wad an-Nail; Babanusa January 2024; the army's late-2024 recovery of Singa).
5. The Darfur war (February 2003, in the notes) has no /wars entry.
Confirmed: Halaib February 1958, 1994, January 2000; 5 June 1983; 1997; Nyala 26 October 2023; Gezira 15 December 2023; El Fasher from April 2024; 25 May 1969; 6 April 1985. Area 1,886,068 km2 against 1,861,884 (0.987), acknowledged.

5. Keyframes overlap Sudan's 1983-2005 keyframes; fix together with the Sudan dossier.
