- Boundaries between my dossiers use the same year for end and next start (-2686, -2181, -2055, -1650). Dates are the Wikipedia infobox "c." values, not Cliopatria's 100-year block edges: Early Dynastic -3150..-2686 (Cliopatria -3000..-2501), Old Kingdom -2686..-2181 (-2500..-2201), Lower Egypt -2181..-2033 (-2200..-2001), Upper Egypt -2134..-2055 (-2200..-2001), Middle Kingdom -2055..-1650 (-2000..-1601), Hyksos -1650..-1550 (-1800..-1401).
- Upper Egypt (Thebes, Eleventh Dynasty) and the Middle Kingdom are one continuous state (Mentuhotep II); Cliopatria's two rows split it. Merge candidate for the colour agent. Likewise Early Dynastic, Old Kingdom and the Middle Kingdom are periods of one Egyptian state, family egypt.
- lower-egypt IS the rival Herakleopolis (Memphite 7th-8th Dynasties then Heracleopolis Magna) and upper-egypt IS the rival Thebes that the group scope says Cliopatria lacks; me-03-x need not write them.
- Hyksos and Cliopatria's Fifteenth Dynasty of Egypt (slug fifteenth-dynasty-of-egypt, another batch) are the same dynasty (both infoboxes: Avaris, 1650 to c. 1550 BC). The Hyksos dossier uses the Fifteenth Dynasty shape as its Egyptian core. One should be merged into or demoted under the other.
- Cliopatria puts the Sixteenth Dynasty shape in the Delta/Faiyum (lat 29.2-31.5) while the Second Intermediate Period page puts it at Thebes; the owner of sixteenth-dynasty-of-egypt should check. My Middle Kingdom ledger hands Thebes to it at -1650.

## lower-egypt (2 shapes) and upper-egypt (2 shapes)
- Source: the First Intermediate Period page, read twice with different questions. The page is inconsistent: period ends c. 2055 BC, Heracleopolis to c. 2050, Merykare last Herakleopolitan c. 2069-2061, yet the defeat is "around 2033 BC"; Intef III (2069-2061) is the successor of Intef II, who beat Nekhen "by around 2060". The dossiers take the explicit dates (Thebes c. 2134-c. 2061 then the Middle Kingdom from 2055; the defeat of the north around 2033) and place the undated Abydos conquest at -2061 (confidence 1).
- Territory is the weak point: the page names no extent for the Memphite phase or the early Theban kingdom. Lower Egypt keeps Cliopatria's shape (edge at Siut, which the page calls the buffer in the south of the Heracleopolitan kingdom) plus a 60 km radius at Abydos for the Heracleopolitan years; Upper Egypt is a 50 km radius at Thebes until Nekhen and Abydos are won, then Cliopatria's strip minus the Asyut governorate. All confidence 1 or 2.
- Natural Earth's Aswan governorate stands for "toward Elephantine"; it also covers desert to the Red Sea.

## middle-kingdom-of-egypt (3 shapes, 6 keyframe entries)
- Pages: Middle Kingdom, Twelfth Dynasty, First and Second Intermediate Period, Fifteenth Dynasty. Cliopatria's second shape (433,182 km2) is the valley plus Lower Nubia (18k), Sinai (28k) and 163k km2 of desert; the pages support only Lower Nubia (Senusret I; the formal boundary at Semna under Senusret III) and the Sinai mining camps, so the desert hinterland is a confidence 1 event ("page silent").
- Nubian dates: Senusret I's campaign is undated (placed mid-reign, -1944, confidence 1); Senusret III's campaigns are in his regnal years 6, 8, 10 and 16, converted with the Twelfth Dynasty page's reign (c. 1878-c. 1839) to -1873..-1863. The Twelfth Dynasty page says Senusret I reached the Third Cataract; the Middle Kingdom page says the Second. Second used.
- The end c. 1650 BC includes the Thirteenth Dynasty (the page also gives c. 1700 for a narrower definition). Losses at -1650 to hyksos, sixteenth-dynasty-of-egypt and Kerma/Kush are bridged (Kush's reoccupation of the forts is undated).
- Capitals Thebes, Itjtawy (year of the move unknown, set at -1991), Thebes again from c. 1677. Ruler strip is the Twelfth Dynasty only (gaps stated).

## hyksos (4 shapes: 2 wrong, 2 adjust)
- Cliopatria's Hyksos row (-1800..-1401; Egypt, Israel, Lebanon, Jordan, Syria) is far larger than the pages support. Hyksos infobox: fl. c. 1650-c. 1550 BC, Avaris, "Lower Egypt and Middle Egypt, up to Cusae", direct control "probably limited to the eastern Nile delta". The -1800 shape (Asiatic settlement under the Middle Kingdom, then the Fourteenth Dynasty) and the -1500 shape (a century after the Hyksos) are wrong; the middle two are cut to the Egyptian part plus Sharuhen near Gaza.
- Date conflicts left in the notes of the dossier: end c. 1550 (infobox) vs Ahmose I's conquest in his year 18 (c. 1532 BC) vs Khamudi c. 1550-1540; Kamose's campaign c. 1553 (his page), c. 1542 (Hyksos page), c. 1545-1540 (Second Intermediate Period page). The infobox end is used so the seam with the New Kingdom is at -1550; the real fall of Avaris may be up to two decades later.
- Not drawn: Canaan-wide or Syrian power ("may have included Canaan itself"), Thebes as vassal (Ryholt: "a baseless assumption"), Khyan's conquest of "the whole of Egypt" (undated).
