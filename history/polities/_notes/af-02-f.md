# af-02-f notes (Regency of Algiers, Karamanli dynasty, Ottoman Tripolitania; plus two counterparts)

Written 2026-10-19. Dossiers: regency-of-algiers, karamanli-dynasty, ottoman-tripolitania (owned), emirate-of-abdelkader (state Cliopatria lacks, direct successor of the Regency) and ali-burghul (type rebel, usurper at Tripoli 1793-1795). All validate with `--resolve` (0 errors, 0 warnings).

## regency-of-algiers (1706-1830)
- Start: the Regency dates from 1516 (page lead "1516 to 1830"), but Cliopatria's own row starts 1706 and the same land (793 Algerian cells, 1560-1705) sits in the Cliopatria Ottoman Empire row. The dossier starts in 1706 to avoid double drawing; the found event is confidence 1. The ottoman-empire dossier (me-10) should record the Regency as a de jure vassal/eyalet ("De jure province and vassal state (eyalet)", "De facto independent since mid-17th century") and decide whether 1516-1705 is drawn as a state of its own.
- Page failure: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regency_of_Algiers is cut off by the fetch tool in the middle of the 1710 sentence ("who strangled seventeen hundre"); a second fetch with an offset returned nothing further. 1710-1830 rests on the Oran, Invasion of Algiers in 1830 and French conquest of Algeria pages. Not on any page opened: Tunisian wars (1756, 1807, 1813-1814), Tuareg south, Kabylia, Constantine and Titteri beys, any border move with Morocco after the Moulouya settlement.
- Oran: Cliopatria leaves Oran out in 1709-1791 and adds it in 1792; the Oran page has Algiers taking it in 1708, Spain retaking it in 1732, Algiers winning it in 1790-1792. New keyframe 1708-1731 adds the Oran cells (carried by the Cliopatria 1792 shape); the 1709 shape is kept for 1732-1791 only. The year of the Spain-Algiers agreements is not on the page ("12 September in Algiers, and on 12 December in Madrid"); 1792 is the siege heading's.
- 1830-1845: the Regency fell on 5 July 1830. Cliopatria keeps drawing the not-yet-conquered remainder of Algeria as "Regency of Algiers" to 1845 (French Algeria is the conquered part; 1846 French Algeria = the whole 1792 shape). Verdicts: 1830 shape adjust (kept for 1830 only), 1834/1836/1840/1842 shapes wrong. The remainder is taken over by emirate-of-abdelkader (Cliopatria remainder minus the Constantinois); NO DOSSIER OWNS the Beylik of Constantine (Ahmed Bey, repulsed Clauzel Nov 1836, Constantine fell 13 Oct 1837), Kabylia or the Saharan oases (Touggourt, Ouargla, Laghouat). Until someone writes them, that land is stateless in 1831-1845.
- Rulers omitted (page gives four, with partial years). Wikipedia infobox has no area, so the area sanity check could not be made.

## karamanli-dynasty (1711-1835)
- Start 29 July 1711 (coup; ruler list), end 26 May 1835 (end of Ali II's reign; the infobox "Deposition: 1832" is Yusuf's abdication). Cliopatria runs 1713-1839; its Ottoman Empire row holds the Libyan coast (544 cells) and a 30-cell strip of south-east Tunisia up to 1712 and 87 Tripolitanian cells in 1836-1839 (the ottoman-empire dossier should drop them for 1711-1712 and 1835-1839).
- The Cliopatria Karamanli shape is only a coastal strip (Fezzan 0 cells). New keyframes 1807 (adds Cyrenaica) and 1811 (adds Fezzan) widen it on the page's "covering most of Libya"; the Cyrenaican and Fezzan parts are read from the Ottoman Tripolitania shape of 1840. Fezzan: Karamanli page says "brought the Fezzan under direct control" by 1807, Fezzan page says occupied "1811/1812" (occupier unnamed); both kept (vassal 1807, gain 1811).
- 30-cell Tunisian strip in the 1713-1793 shape and its loss to Tunis in 1794 are on no page (civil-war page: Burghul occupied Djerba 30 Sept 1794, nothing on a frontier strip); confidence 1, matching the Tunis dossier's gain (also confidence 1).
- Dates in conflict: Karamanli page puts the restoration in January 1794, the civil-war page and the ruler list in January 1795 (used). First Barbary War treaty 10 June 1805 in the body, 4 June in the infobox.
- 1836-1839 Cliopatria shape marked wrong: no page opened has a Karamanli remnant after 1835.
- Not on any page opened: year of the Ottoman recognition (Cliopatria 1713), relations with Ghadames, Wadai and Bornu (the page has the 1824 dropped Bornu plan), what happened to Derna after the 1805 treaty.

## ottoman-tripolitania (1835-1912)
- Cliopatria runs 1840-1911; the page dates direct Ottoman control from 1835 and the Italian cession from the Treaty of Ouchy (18 October 1912). New keyframes 1835-1839 and 1912; Italy's 5 November 1911 decree is in the loss note. Italian Africa's Cliopatria 1912 shape covers Libya (2,946 cells, the same land), so 1912 is a contest blend with Italy; the italian-africa dossier should mirror the contest from October 1911.
- Cliopatria 1840-1884 shapes include a stray patch of 47 cells in Somaliland (10 N, 44-45 E) plus 11 cells on the Tunisian, Niger and Chad edges; the page lists five sanjaks, all in Libya. Marked adjust (cut to Libya). The patch is probably the Red Sea coast held by Egypt/Ottomans 1870-1884 (khedivate-of-egypt owner should check).
- Pages silent: Kufra, Tibesti, Borku, the Anglo-French Sahara declarations (1890, 1898-99, 1902), the Egyptian frontier, Sudan; the 1895 and 1905 shape changes (a few Saharan-edge cells) are unverified. Tunisian frontier is dated: coast 1886, "down to the vicinity of Ghadames" 1892, treaty 19 May 1910, pillars 1910-11 (Libya-Tunisia border page); the Algeria-Libya border page is silent on the Ottoman era.
- Cyrenaica: independent sanjak 1863, under Tripoli 1871, directly under Istanbul 1888: no shape change, kept in notes. Senussi (Jaghbub, Kufra from the 1880s) have no dossier.
- Infobox gives no area. No rulers list.

## emirate-of-abdelkader (1832-1847) and ali-burghul (1793-1795)
- Written because the Regency's and Karamanli's own events needed a counterpart dossier. Pages are thin: Emirate page gives 27 Nov 1832, capitals (Mascara to 1835, Tagdemt), two treaty dates, surrender 23 Dec 1847, no territory. Keyframes are read from Cliopatria's Regency remainder minus the Constantinois ("approximately two thirds of Algeria", Treaty of Tafna page). Setif/Biskra (Emirate provinces) are not added: no page dates them. The 1846 loss/contest end are confidence 1.
- Gazetteer names lacking: Djerba (ali-burghul uses 10.85 E, 33.8 N, 25 km).
- Counterparts still without dossiers: beylik-of-constantine, the Fezzan Awlad Muhammad dynasty, the Senussi order, Kabylia.
