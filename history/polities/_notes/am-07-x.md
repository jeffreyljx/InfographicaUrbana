## Simplifications the reviewer should know
- THIRTEEN COLONIES. The pages give charters and founding years, not settled areas. Each colony enters as its modern state in its founding year (Virginia as the Jamestown disc to 1623 and the state from the 1624 royal charter, at confidence 1; Plymouth a disc). The shapes before 1763 overstate the settled area; the 1763 Proclamation Line (a 10-point polygon along the Appalachian watershed, the researcher's approximation) cuts the west, and 1768 adds West Virginia and Kentucky. Maine and Vermont are left out (the pages say nothing about their attachment). The dossier overlaps the Cliopatria aggregates (English Colonial Empire 1609-1779, British Colonial Empire 1706-1999); the render should draw one or the other (see english-colonial-empire notes).
- LABRADOR. The coastal strip is a researcher's polygon (the Labrador boundary dispute page says only that Labrador was 'limited to a coastal strip' in 1763). It is used in province-of-quebec (1774-1791), lower-canada (1791-1809), dominion-of-newfoundland (1763-1773, 1809-1926). The interior goes to Newfoundland in 1927.
- VANCOUVER ISLAND. The gazetteer has no region, so the island is a 10-point polygon intersected with British Columbia. The pre-1863 northern limit of the BC colony is read as the Nass-Finlay line (about 56 N), taken from the Stikine Territory page's southern limit.
- RUPERT'S LAND. A chartered claim, drawn soft; the company's actual posts were coastal. The gazetteer region overshoots into Alberta and Nunavut (ratio 1.30-1.37 to the infobox 3.86 M km2).
- CAPITALS. Several capital spans are the researcher's readings and flagged in each entry's note (Cuper's Cove, Charles Fort, Kodiak before 1808, Newark 1791-1795, Province of Canada stays ended one year early so spans do not overlap).

## Disagreements between pages
- Detroit surrendered 16 August 1812 (War of 1812) vs 'captured by the British on 6 August 1812' (Upper Canada). Fort Niagara 18 December 1813 (War of 1812).
- Sitka as capital 1804 (timeline) vs 1808 (text) on the Russian America page; Russian America payment 9 April 1867 vs Senate ratification 15 May 1867 (Alaska Purchase).
- Labrador boundary decision 1 March 1927 (Labrador boundary dispute) vs 1 April 1927 (Dominion of Newfoundland).
- Newfoundland Crown colony 1824 (intro) vs 1825 (body).
- Union of Vancouver Island and British Columbia: proclaimed 6 August 1866 vs 19 November 1866 (infobox); Pig War arbitration 1871 (Pig War page) vs 1872 elsewhere.
- Kingston as capital of the Province of Canada 1841-1843 (text) vs 1841-1844 (infobox).
- Georgia a royal colony 1751/1752/1755; New York captured 27 August (text) vs 28 August (infobox), surrendered 24/29 September; New York a royal colony 1685/1686/1689.
- Quebec Act: royal assent 22 June 1774 vs infobox commencement 13 January 1774. The 1774 shape is 4.2 times the 1763 shape (the page says 'roughly tripling'); the 1763 shape may be small.
