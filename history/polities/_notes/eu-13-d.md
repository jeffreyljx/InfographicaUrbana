WebFetch returned summaries of each page, with quotes under about 125 characters; the notes quote only what the tool returned in quotation marks.

ONE STATE, NOT SPLIT. Cliopatria's single row 755-1870 is one continuous state in the Wikipedia article (infobox: 'under the direct sovereign rule of the pope from 756 to 1870'). The two breaks (Roman Republic February 1798 to June 1800; annexation to the Kingdom of Italy and the French Empire 1808-1814) are drawn as loss/gain events, not as new states; the colour and dossier continue. The state starts in 756 (Cliopatria 755) and ends 20 September 1870 (Capture of Rome).

CLIOPATRIA ROWS TO CORRECT (all in keyframe_review): 755 shape drawn from 756 only; 1147 and 1202-1272 shapes lack the March of Ancona acquired in 1198; 1507-1555 shapes lack Parma and Piacenza (1512-1545, new keyframe 1512 and verdicts on 1507 and 1529); 1600-1796 shapes lack the Duchy of Castro (1649, new keyframe 1649 and adjust verdicts); 1800-1802 shape wrongly includes the Legations ceded in 1797; 1797 shape drawn for 1797 only (Roman Republic from 15 February 1798, no papal state until June 1800); 1807 shape for 1807 only with a new keyframe for 1808 (Marche annexed 2 April 1808; rest 17 May 1809); 1859 shape without the Legations. Relation rows: the allegiance of the Papal States to the Byzantine Empire 755-869 is contradicted by the Donation of Pepin page (the Empire's envoys offered money to restore the lands, Pepin refused, 'the imperial presence in central Italy' was at an end); the alliance with the Spanish Empire 1564-1611 is not territory. Ledger events override the shape in their year; the vassal tints (Ferrara 1208-1597, Comtat Venaissin 1274-1791, Romagna under Cesare Borgia 1500-1503) sit inside or beside the Cliopatria outline and the renderer should let the tint win.
