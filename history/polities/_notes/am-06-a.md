## portuguese-empire: Portuguese Empire (1415..1910)

ONE STATE, TWO CLIOPATRIA ROWS. Cliopatria's "Portuguese Empire" (1502-1823, 48 shapes: Brazil, Africa and the Atlantic) and "Portuguese Colonies" (1564-1910, 32 shapes: Goa, Macau, Timor, Ceylon until 1658) share Wikidata Q200464 and the Wikipedia article "Portuguese Empire", and both are members of the umbrella (Portuguese Empire). They are the Atlantic and the Asian halves of one state, so this is one dossier under the earlier slug with both Names in cliopatria_names and all 80 shapes reviewed. The group sa-06 owns the slug portuguese-colonies: it should not write a second dossier (or should make it a pointer), and the colour agent should give portuguese-colonies the colour of portuguese-empire. The other components of the umbrella, Portuguese Africa (1507-1581, af groups) and Portuguese Ceylon (1507-1686, sa-06), are also parts of this state in Cliopatria's own model; they are not mine to edit.

LIFE SPAN. The infobox says "c. 1415 to 1999" (S1). The dossier starts in 1415 at Ceuta (as the Tang example starts in 618 where Cliopatria starts in 623) with new keyframes for 1415-1501, and ends in 1910, where Cliopatria's Colonies row ends and its Portuguese Republic row (1911-1925, eu-20; then Estado Novo 1926-2024, eu-21) takes over the same overseas territories. The empire did not end in 1910: the dissolve event is a regime change, so the colour agent may prefer to treat the Republic and Estado Novo as continuations (rule 2). Neither of the two rows draws Angola or Mozambique in 1824-1910 (the Colonies row has Asia only, the empire row ends in 1823); four new keyframes (1824, 1885, 1891, 1895) restore them from S1, S8 and S9.

OVERLAPS TO RESOLVE IN THE COMPILE STEP. (1) Cliopatria's Kingdom of Portugal shapes already hold Ceuta (1415 shape), Madeira and the Azores (1450 shape) and, in 1502, also Cape Verde, Tangier, Safi, São Tomé and Fernando Po, which its Portuguese Empire 1502 shape holds too; the pre-1502 events and keyframes here repeat Ceuta, Madeira and the Azores because S1 dates them (1415, 1419, 1427) and they are the empire's birth. eu-12's kingdom-of-portugal dossier should decide which row draws them; the Portuguese Empire is the lighter green of the same family. (2) During the Iberian Union Cliopatria draws the Portuguese overseas lands (Brazil, Angola, Mozambique, Goa, Ceylon, Timor, Malacca) inside its Spanish Empire row from 1582 to 1639 and Madeira and the Azores inside the Iberian Union row; the empire has no shape of its own in those years except the 3-cell Northern Province patch, a stray 1619-1621 shape that is not Portuguese, and Portuguese Ceylon. The ledger records a loss to spanish-empire in 1581 and a gain in 1640 (a personal union with Portuguese autonomy, S11): spanish-empire (am-06-b/c) needs the matching events or a personal-union tint. Losses of the union years (Amboina 1605, Hormuz 1622, Salvador 1624-1625, Pernambuco 1630, Elmina 1637) are therefore not in this ledger, only in the Dutch-Portuguese War entry.
