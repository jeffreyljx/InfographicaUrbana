## haudenosaunee: thin (fixes applied by the reviewer)

Fixes applied (indices are those after the edit):

1. /ledger/9, the 1651 Neutral contest_end: confidence 3 changed to 2. The page names the people, not the land.
2. /ledger/16 note and /keyframe_review/6 note: removed 'they claimed the land by right of conquest as a hunting ground' and 'The English acknowledged this claim in 1674'. On the Beaver Wars page both sentences are about the Mannahoac land of the northern Virginia Piedmont, not the Ohio watershed. They now sit in the 1670 Mannahoac note (/ledger/17). The Ohio year stands: 'from about 1670 onward'.
3. Old /ledger/23, the 14 September 1726 'gain' from british-colonial-empire: deleted. The Nanfan page describes the 60-mile strip as reserved for Six Nations use 'with the permission of its owner under the 1701 agreement, the King of Great Britain'. That is a use right, not a gain. The keyframes do not draw it, and the event would have asked eu-11 for a false British loss. The amendment is still described in /keyframe_review/12 and /13.
4. The Wendat occupation was missing its mirror. Added /ledger/3, occupation_start 1649-03 (confidence 3), and /ledger/4, occupation_end 1649-05 (confidence 2), both with counterpart wendat-confederacy and source Huron_people, which is added to sources[]. Month 5 is set on the 1649 contest_end and gain to keep the (year, month) order.

Thin, for a gap-filler:

- 1659 gain of 166,000 km2 at confidence 1: try Erie_people.
- 1683 gain and 1687 loss at confidence 1: try Seneca_people.
- 1734 loss of the Champlain shore to New France at confidence 1. Fort_St._Frederic dates it, since the reviewer read 'Construction was started in 1734 by Gaspard-Joseph Chaussegros de Léry' (Crown Point). Set that source and confidence 2.
