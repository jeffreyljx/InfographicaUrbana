## The Turks / Türgesh label swap (turks, turgesh)

- Cliopatria "Türgesh" 692-749 is two things: 692-704 is only the Mongolian Plateau (75% of the plateau, the whole Orkhon valley), i.e. the Second Turkic Khaganate (682-744, Orkhon; Second Turkic Khaganate page); 705-749 is that plateau plus the real Turgesh lands (Semirechye, Dzungaria, Chach, Turfan) plus Transoxiana, 4.03-4.04 M km2. The Türgesh page makes the Turgesh a state only from 699 and gives "from Chach to Turfan and Beshbaliq" (703); the turgesh dossier uses that span (about 0.9 M km2) and marks the Mongolian 692 shape wrong.
- Cliopatria "Turks" 666-756 is not a state with an article: its Wikipedia title "Turk" is a disambiguation page, and its Wikidata Q2118293 is shared with "Oghuz Turks". Its 692-717 shape lies inside the 705-717 Türgesh shape (all but 4 of 3,206 cells), so the two rows double-draw Zhetysu in 705-717. What the pages support as a distinct "Turks" state is only the Western Turk remnant under Tang puppets, Ashina claimants and Tibetan pressure, 666-698 (Western Turkic Khaganate page); the turks dossier keeps that (12 events after the repair below, 3 wars, soft) and ends in 698, handing the Taraz core to the Turgesh and the rest to the Tang. The 750-756 "Turks" shapes (Pamir, Ferghana, Transoxiana, Zhetysu) are marked wrong: Tang protectorate to 756, Karluks from 751, Turgesh in Zhetysu to 766.
- Turks and Oghuz Turks are different states (Chu and Ili versus Aral and lower Syr Darya).
- The Second Turkic Khaganate (682-744) has no Cliopatria row under its name and no dossier in any batch I could find (ia-01-c asked for it; ia-01-x owns nothing). It needs one: its land is the Mongolian shape of Cliopatria's Türgesh 692-749 (then the Uyghur Khaganate from 744/750).
- Turgesh end moved to 766 (Cliopatria 749; Türgesh page infobox and "In 766 the Karluks conquered Zhetysu"). Zhetysu 745-765 is drawn alone (page silent on 745-765); Cliopatria draws the Uyghur Khaganate over Zhetysu in 760, which the Karluks page contradicts.
- Disagreements between pages: Suyab (Türgesh: captured in 703; Western Turkic Khaganate: Tang Anxi garrison 679-719); Saqal/Suluk reign dates (list 716-738 versus text "elected in 714"); the Samarkand battle (Türgesh 720, Muslim conquest page 721).
- Transoxiana is a vassal tint 719-732 (the page's "Turgesh overlords") and a contest zone 721-743 against the Umayyads, not direct land.
- Events other dossiers should carry: tang-dynasty (ea-04): loss of Suyab and the 703 span, gain of Beshbalik 736, suzerainty over the Turgesh 740-744 (Kül Chor submits 740, executed 744), suzerainty over the Western Turks 666-677 and 685-690 (vassal_start/vassal_end with counterpart turks; WTK infobox: Kunling and Mengchi protectorates 657-736 and 657-742, vassal 657-742), loss of the Western Turks' land in 698; umayyad-caliphate (me-07): the 728 Bukhara loss, the 732 recovery, the contest 721-743; qarlugs (ia-01-f): the 766 gain of Zhetysu (its row starts 793; the Karluk Yabghu State of 756-940 has no dossier); second-turkic-khaganate: contest 711-715 over Dzungaria.
