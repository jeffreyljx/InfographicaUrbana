## imamate-of-oman (repaired after review; pages: Imamate of Oman, Treaty of Seeb, Jebel Akhdar War; budget 1-2)
- Life 1913-January 1959 (Cliopatria's row is 1913-1919). The earlier version dissolved the Imamate at the Treaty of Seeb; that was wrong: the Imamate page's infobox reads 'End of the Jebel Akhdar War: 1959' and the Treaty of Seeb 'granted autonomy to the imamate in the interior of Oman but recognized the sovereignty of the' Sultanate of Muscat. Now: vassal_start 25 September 1920 (confidence 2); vassal_end and contest_start in October 1954 (Treaty of Seeb page: the Muscat Infantry entered and occupied Fahud on 25 October 1954, seen by the Imamate as a breach); loss of Nizwa on 15 December 1955 (Jebel Akhdar War page; the Treaty of Seeb page also has 1955) and a 1957 regain of the Nizwa district (that page: the Imamate's forces 'freed Nizwa (capital), Firq, Izki, Tanuf, Bahla and Jabal Akhdar' in the 1957 uprising; Ibri stayed occupied); contest_end and dissolve in January 1959 (30 January: SAS at Saiq and Shuraijah; 'the autonomous Imamate of Oman was abolished'). New keyframe imamate-of-oman_1920 (1920-1959, the 1919 polygon, adjust), the Jebel Akhdar War in wars, certainty to 1959, successor land dated January 1959, and rulers from the Treaty of Seeb and Jebel Akhdar War pages (Salim ibn Rashid al-Kharusi to July 1924, Muhammad bin Abdullah al-Khalili, Ghalib al-Hinai elected 3 May 1954).
- Disagreements and choices: the war begins on 10 October 1954 in the Jebel Akhdar War infobox and on 25 October 1954 (Fahud) in the Treaty of Seeb page; I dated the contest to the occupation of Fahud (first ground held) and cite the other date in the note. The Jebel Akhdar War page's 15 December 1955 for Nizwa sits oddly with its own timeline (Adam occupied 13 December 1954) but the Treaty of Seeb page also has 1955. The vassal_start/vassal_end counterpart is sultanate-of-oman, as the review asked; the schema defines the counterpart of a vassal event as the vassal itself, so this reads the other way round from sultanate-of-oman /ledger/19 (counterpart imamate-of-oman, the vassal): the renderer should draw the tint from the suzerain's dossier only. The 1955 loss of Nizwa and the 1957 regain have no mirror in sultanate-of-oman (me-10-c): that dossier covers 1954-1959 as one contest and gains the interior in 1959. I did not cut the Nizwa district from the 1920-1959 shape: from 1954 the whole interior is a contested blend and no page says what the Imamate held in 1956 ('the red flag of the Sultanate flew over the interior for the first time in half a century') or dates the Muscat Regiment's suppression of the rising at Nizwa; the government-in-exile at Dammam is undated, so the capital dot stays at Nizwa. Rulers: the Salim ibn Rashid al-Kharusi article named by the review returned url_not_found; the Treaty of Seeb page gives his murder (July 1924) and al-Khalili's election without a year, and al-Khalili's death is undated, so his last year (1954) overlaps Ghalib's first.
- Still unsettled: no area on any page (the Cliopatria polygon is 103.9 k km2 and includes a coastal cell at Sur the page does not support); the 'Ghalib's election' is 1954 on the Treaty of Seeb page, 3 May 1954 on the Jebel Akhdar War page; the Imamate page gives 749 in the infobox and 750 in the text for the first Imamate. Counterpart: sultanate-of-muscat-and-oman is merged into slug sultanate-of-oman (me-10-c).
