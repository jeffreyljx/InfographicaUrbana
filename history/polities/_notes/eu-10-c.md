## Pattern repeated in several dossiers: gaps in Cliopatria are held by other states
Aquitaine (1138/1139-1203), Maine (1056-1063 and 1111-1449), Vermandois (1169-1190) and Toulouse (1216-1219, 1227-1235) have no Cliopatria shape for years in which the pages show another state holding the land. The ledgers record the hand-overs against the holder (kingdom-of-france, angevin-empire, county-of-anjou, county-of-flanders) and do not invent a shape. The holders' dossiers (eu-10 kingdom-of-france, county-of-anjou, county-of-flanders; eu-11 angevin-empire, kingdom-of-england) should carry the matching gain/loss: Aquitaine to France 1137 and to the Angevin Empire 18 May 1152 (Eleanor and Henry), back 1204; Maine to Anjou 1051 (Mans, shortly after 26 March) and 1110; Vermandois to Flanders 1167 and back 1191.
