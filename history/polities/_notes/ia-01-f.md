# ia-01-f notes (Xiongnu to the Türks, part f): uyghur-khaganate, qarlugs, kara-khanids, ganzhou-kingdom, qocho-kingdom, kingdom-of-khotan

Six dossiers written (kara-khanids and qocho-kingdom repaired after review, see their sections), all validate with 0 errors (warnings only for counterpart slugs with no dossier yet: second-turkic-khaganate, guiyi-circuit, xiliangfu; rulers and one war outside the life span in two dossiers). 13 distinct pages opened: Uyghur Khaganate, Karluks, Karluk Yabghu (URL Karluk_Yabgu_State; Karluk_Yabghu_State returned 404), Tibetan Empire, Yenisei Kyrgyz, Kara-Khanid Khanate, Samanid Empire, Kingdom of Khotan, Tarim Basin, Ganzhou Uyghur Kingdom, Guiyi Circuit, Western Xia, Qocho. Every ledger event, verdict and war cites one of them; a few quotes are the page-summarising tool's paraphrase and are marked "paraphrase" where I knew it.

## Cross-cutting decisions

- Four of the six Cliopatria rows start or end in the wrong year against their infobox: Uyghur Khaganate 750-849 (infobox 744-840), Qarlugs 793-849 (Karluk Yabghu 742-840), Kara-Khanids 850-1045 (Established 840), Ganzhou 860-1039 (894-1036), Qocho 860-1138 (843-1128/1132), Khotan 888-1009 (independence 851, disestablished 1006). Each is handled by a new keyframe before the first shape and a "years only" verdict after the last.
- The Karluk Yabghu page says the yabghu's 840 title change "formed the Kara-Khanid Khanate"; Cliopatria has two rows (Qarlugs 793-849, Kara-Khanids 850-1045, the latter containing all 1,348 Qarlugs cells). I kept two dossiers split at 840 (qarlugs ends 840, kara-khanids begins 840 with the Qarlugs 840 shape as a new keyframe). The reviewer may prefer a single Karluk/Kara-Khanid state; if so, the colour agent should give both the same family and hue.
- Wikidata Q494354 (Kara-Khanids, Eastern Kara-Khanids, Western Karakhanid Khanate): the first row is the unified Khanate (840-1045); after the 1040s split the Eastern and Western Khanates are different states with different capitals and are written by other batches. The kara-khanids dossier hands its land to them with a split event at 1045 (the page gives only "the 1040s"; 1045 is Cliopatria's last year, confidence 1).
- Zhetysu (Semirechye): Cliopatria draws it as direct Uyghur land 755-792 and gives it to the Qarlugs row in 793; the Karluks page has the Karluk Yabghu State there from 766. The Uyghur shapes 755-783 are corrected to drop it (Türgesh vassal tint to 765, Karluks from 766) and the qarlugs dossier carries 766-840.
- The Tarim: Cliopatria draws Kashgar and the west Tarim in the Kara-Khanid shape 850-887, then in the Khotan row 888-1009, then in the Kara-Khanid shape from 1010. The pages put the Kara-Khanid capture of Kashgar in 934 (Satuq Bughra Khan), Khotan's independence in 851, a Khotan seizure of Kashgar in 970 and the Kara-Khanid conquest of Khotan in 1006. Both dossiers correct this the same way (Kashgar to the Kara-Khanids from 934, counterpart "nobody" because no page says who held it before; Khotan without Kashgar). Who held Kashgar in 851-933 is open.

## uyghur-khaganate (744-840; Cliopatria 750-849; 9 keyframes: 1 ok, 7 adjust, 1 wrong; 15 events, 6 wars, 5 pages)
Founding 744 and the 745 death of Kulun Beg rest on the Uyghur page alone (Second Turkic Khaganate page not opened). Kyrgyz vassals 758-840 (Kyrgyz page); Karluks vassals from 746 (Karluk Yabghu page), Türgesh vassals 755-765 (the Uyghur page dates only the reign, 747-759). Tibetan wars 790-808: 790 Tingzhou defeat, 791 Beiting, 792 Gaochang (Turfan-Hami added back to the 793 shape), 808 Liang. The 757 step into Dzungaria and the northern Tarim, and the 793 step out of it (-2,980 cells), are not dated by any page opened; the Futu valley (789) has no location on the page; the Kyrgyz page says the Kyrgyz stayed at home after 843 while the Uyghur page has them holding Baikal to the Irtysh. The 840-849 shape is wrong (remnants under Wujie and Enian held no land); their reigns are listed as rulers 841-848 and the 841-848 war with the Tang has no zone. Infobox 3.1 M km2 (800) against 3.03 M km2 (Cliopatria 793, ratio 0.98).

## kara-khanids (840-1045; Cliopatria 850-1045; 11 keyframes: 4 ok, 7 adjust; 11 events, 4 wars)

Not covered: the 1008/1025 Ghaznavid wars (no dates on the page), Uzgen as a capital, the Qocho and Khitan frontiers.
Repair pass: the 896-959 keyframe no longer adds Kashgar (it enters with the 934 gain, and the 960 keyframe carries it); rulers 840-1017 added from the page's list (Bilge Kul Qadir Khan to Ahmad Arslan Qara Khan; none for 958-970 and 1018-1045). The Kara-Khanid page, re-read, is silent on who held Kashgar before 934, on Khotan's 970 seizure and the return, and on a 1006 Khorasan invasion or 1008 Balkh defeat (not on this page; the Ghaznavids page was not opened, the 5-page budget being spent). Split year stays 1045 at confidence 1 (page: "the 1040s"; 1041 is only 'Ayn ad-Dawlah's accession).

## ganzhou-kingdom (894-1036; Cliopatria 860-1039; 7 keyframes: 5 ok, 2 adjust; 7 events, 5 wars, 3 pages)
