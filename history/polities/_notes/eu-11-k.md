## british-colonial-empire

WHAT THE ROW IS. Cliopatria's 'British Colonial Empire' (1706-1999, 90 shapes, max 13.69 M km2 in 1900) is an aggregate of British possessions drawn as effective control in coarse grid cells: the Thirteen Colonies, Newfoundland, Nova Scotia and Hudson Bay, the Caribbean, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, Africa outside the british-africa and british-gold-coast rows, then the remnants. It is NOT the whole British Empire: the Indian subcontinent is drawn by british-empire (1757-1858) and british-raj (1859-1947), Aden is in no Cliopatria shape, and what a shape draws is not always what a page says. Type is 'colony' because the territory is a dependency of the metropole (kingdom-of-great-britain, then united-kingdom).

Q8680 SHARED WITH british-empire. Both rows carry Wikidata Q8680 and Wikipedia 'British Empire'. They are different states in Cliopatria's drawing (this global aggregate 1706-1999 versus the East India Company's India 1757-1858) and are NOT merged: british-empire is the counterpart named by many Indian dossiers (gwalior-state, nawab-of-awadh, kingdom-of-mysore and others) and has its own dossier from sa-06-c. The 'British Empire' article draws no line between the two.

START AND END. identity.start is 1704 (TE: 'Gibraltar | 1704 | Captured by Britain'), two years before the first Cliopatria shape (new keyframe british-colonial-empire_1704 covers 1704-1705). BE gives no establishment date for the empire. The end is 1999, the last Cliopatria year; BE puts the symbolic end at the Hong Kong handover of 1 July 1997 (new keyframe _1997 drops Hong Kong). The remnants continue as british-overseas-territories (2000-).

INFOBOX AREA. BE: 'by 1920 it covered 35.5 million km2'. This row's 1920 shape is 12.03 M km2; the six British-family rows alive in 1920 (this row 12.03, british-africa 10.07, commonwealth-of-australia 7.78, british-raj 4.94, british-gold-coast 1.20, kingdom-of-great-britain 0.77) sum to 36.77 M km2, 3.6 percent above the infobox. The Cliopatria row's maximum (13.69 M km2, 1900) is thus not the empire's area; no keyframe was widened to reach 35.5 M km2.

OVERLAPS. (1) english-colonial-empire (1609-1779) covers 1709-1712 as well; 771 of its 777 cells in 1709 are this row's cells, and this dossier treats 1709 as a hand-over of rows (ledger 1709, confidence 1). (2) Canada: the Cliopatria shape keeps the Dominion's land in this row until 1928, while canada.json (state from 1 July 1867) lists this dossier as its predecessor and asks for a vassal link. The verdicts for 1868 to 1928 therefore remove the Dominion's provinces from the direct shape in step with the CAN transfers (four founding provinces 1868; Rupert's Land and the North-Western Territory 1870; British Columbia 1871; everything but Newfoundland and Labrador from 1873), and the ledger carries Canada as a vassal from 1867 to 11 December 1931 (SW). Newfoundland and Labrador (a separate colony; Labrador to Newfoundland on 11 March 1927, CAN) stay direct land. New Zealand is treated the same way (verdicts 1908-1947 remove it; vassal 1907 to November 1947) and Australia (vassal 1901 to 1942). (3) commonwealth-of-australia (Cliopatria 1905-) starts four years after federation; Cliopatria keeps Australia whole in this row through 1904, so new keyframe _1901 removes it and the Dominion lands from 1901. (4) Sub-colony dossiers (british-honduras, belize, guyana, jamaica, barbados and others) describe ground this row also draws: the same land at a finer scale, not rivals.

EFFECTIVE CONTROL VERSUS CLAIMS. Cliopatria draws effective control, so the interior claims (Western Australia, South Australia and the Northern Territory before settlement; New Zealand's South Island in 1840; the Canadian north) are drawn only to the extent the cells go. The keyframe corrections follow the shape and say where a page contradicts it; they do not paint nominal claims. Where a page lists a territory that no cell draws, it is a gap (see below), not a correction; where a page contradicts a cell the verdict says so.

Each is a few cells at most.

WHERE I DISAGREE OR CANNOT AGREE ALONE

The same events are named differently by the other authors: kingdom-of-iraq names kingdom-of-great-britain for the 1921-1932 and 1941-1947 occupations, burma names british-empire for Arakan and Tenasserim (and puts Arakan on 1 April 1825 and Tenasserim on 24 February 1826, as this row now does), new-france and the Guiana dossiers name british-colonial-empire.
