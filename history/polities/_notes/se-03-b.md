## Repair of 2026-10-19 (author, after the review in review-se-03-b.md)

Convention now used by the whole batch: both sides of a dependency carry the event. A suzerain's vassal_start/vassal_end names the vassal as counterpart; the vassal's own dossier names the suzerain. An occupation event names the occupier when the state is occupied (dutch-east-indies: british-colonial-empire, empire-of-japan; pontianak: empire-of-japan) and the occupied state when it occupies (dutch-east-indies: sultanate-of-tidore 1806-1811). Cliopatria's Dutch East Indies shapes include land that was only a vassal; that land stays in the shapes and is carried by the vassal events, and a gain marks the year the land passes to direct rule.
