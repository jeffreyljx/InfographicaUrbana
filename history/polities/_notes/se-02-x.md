# se-02-x notes: Maritime Southeast Asia, states Cliopatria lacks (additions batch)

Written 2026-10-19 by the Phase B research agent. 20 new dossiers, all with `cliopatria_names: []`, all validate with 0 errors; 133 ledger events, 48 sources. Every page was opened with WebFetch in this run. WebFetch returns a summary, not the page: quotes in the dossiers are the summariser's rendering and may differ from the Wikipedia text by a word. The reviewer should treat every `'quoted'` phrase as a close paraphrase.

Written (most eventful first): sultanate-of-ternate (16 events), sultanate-of-tidore (18), pagaruyung-kingdom (10), padri-movement (9), melayu-kingdom, kedah-kingdom, luwu, wajo (5 each), old-pahang-sultanate, peureulak-sultanate, kingdom-of-aru, sultanate-of-bacan, sultanate-of-buayan (7 each), negara-daha (6), langkasuka (4), sumedang-larang, kutai-martadipura, nan-sarunai, siak-sultanate, kertabhumi-majapahit (3 each). Rebel entries (type rebel): padri-movement, kertabhumi-majapahit.

## Common weakness: territory

Almost no page gives a frontier. Every shape is a disc, an admin1 stand-in or a gazetteer region, all with `certainty: soft` and a note saying so. Confidence 3 is used only where the page gives the year and the land. Events whose end year is a bridge (Luwu 1905, Wajo 1905, Bacan 1889, Kedah 1026-1135) are confidence 1 with "page silent". Infobox areas were not given on any page, so no Cliopatria-area comparison could be made for any state (there is no Cliopatria row to compare).

## Per state

- melayu-kingdom (671-1347). Pages: Melayu_Kingdom, Pamalayu, Adityawarman. Shapes are the researcher's discs on the Jambi lowlands and upper Batang Hari. No page dates the move from Jambi to Dharmasraya (capital_move 1286 at confidence 1). Disagreement: the Melayu page dates independence from Srivijaya to 1028; the srivijaya dossier merges Melayu in 692 and loses Jambi in 1202, so 1028-1201 is a conflict between the two dossiers. The Majapahit dossier carries "Sumatran principalities (Jambi-Melayu, Palembang, Minangkabau)" as one string, not the slug. Ends 1347 with Adityawarman's Pagaruyung.
- pagaruyung-kingdom (1347-1833). Pages: Pagaruyung_Kingdom, Adityawarman, Padri_War, Minangkabau_people, Melayu_Kingdom. Shape is the gazetteer's Minangkabau region; the rantau (Kampar, Siak, Jambi, Indragiri, Pasaman, Barus, Pariaman) are undated on the pages. The loss of the west coast to Aceh c.1609 is confidence 1; Aceh's dossier gives that coast to the VOC in 1666, so Pagaruyung does not regain it. The Padri occupation 1815-1822 and the contest 1803-1833 pair with padri-movement. The 1821 Dutch alliance (a Dutch vassal span) is missing from the dutch-east-indies dossier. No "Islamic sultanate" rename event: the page gives no year.
- sultanate-of-ternate (1257-1914). Pages: Sultanate_of_Ternate, Ternate, Maluku_Islands, Sultanate_of_Tidore. 1257 is the traditional start (infobox 1486 is the sultanate). The peak dependencies (1570-1583, Babullah) are "influence" on the page, drawn as a soft tint. Portuguese fort occupation 1522-1575, Spanish 1606-1663, British 1801-02 and 1810-17 are occupation pairs recorded by Ternate; the loss of the Ambon Quarter is 1656. The 1683 treaty cost unnamed lands; the Ternate page has no hongi or 1652/1667 treaty. The dutch-republic and dutch-east-indies dossiers lack the matching vassal spans. Ternate and Tidore discs overlap at grid resolution (Halmahera's west coast and the strait islands are shared on the pages).
- sultanate-of-tidore (1081-1905). Pages: Sultanate_of_Tidore, Nuku_of_Tidore, Maluku_Islands, Sultanate_of_Ternate. 1081 is the local-tradition date of the first king (the infobox also gives 1450); the infobox runs to 1967, 1905 is the bridge. The Papuan claim (by 1849) is an upper bound, a tint at confidence 1. Nuku's rebel Tidore (1780-1797) needs a dossier (nuku-rebellion, type rebel). Jailolo's fate is on the page without a clear date. The Dutch vassal span is for dutch-republic and dutch-east-indies.
- langkasuka (150-1400). One page. Start (150, mid-point of "second century") and end (1400, the patani-kingdom founding) are confidence 1. 718-1224 is held by srivijaya. 1225 gain from tambralinga at confidence 1. The 1139-1224 stretch and the Pattani basin are an open gap. Pasai Annals date the end c.1370, the page notes historians who run it to the 1470s.
- kedah-kingdom (330-1136). Pages: Kedah_Sultanate, Bujang_Valley, Srivijaya, Kedah_Tua. 685-1025 belongs to Srivijaya (loss 685). 1026-1135 is a bridging reading of the Srivijaya page (Kedah outside Sanfoqi's influence in the 11th century; the Cholas took the king of Kadaram in 1025 and restored him). It conflicts with srivijaya and sultanate-of-kedah, which hold Kedah under Srivijaya to 1136/1139. The sultanate-of-kedah dossier already lists this slug as predecessor.
- old-pahang-sultanate (1470-1623). Pages: Pahang_Sultanate, Johor_Sultanate. Slug differs from the `pahang-sultanate` string the sultanate-of-johor and sultanate-of-aceh dossiers use for the 1612-1623 events; the sultanate-of-malacca dossier uses `old-pahang-sultanate`. The 1853-1889 "Pahang Sultanate" (group se-03) is a different state with the same Wikipedia title. The Old Pahang Kingdom before 1454 (Ligor's vassal) has no dossier.
- padri-movement (1803-1837, rebel). Pages: Padri_War, Imam_Bonjol, Pagaruyung_Kingdom, Minangkabau_people. A movement of several leaders; the only dated land gain is the 1815 Pagaruyung occupation; the shape is the three stronghold areas. Expeditions into the Batak lands are undated and not drawn. Dutch contests 1821-24 and 1831-37; ends Aug 1837 with Bonjol's fall.
- peureulak-sultanate (840-1292). Pages: Peureulak_Sultanate, Samudera_Pasai_Sultanate. The 840 founding and the sultan list are the Hikayat tradition. The Shia-Sunni split 986-988 and the Srivijaya invasion 988-1006 have no geography and no srivijaya-side event (a gap-filler could ask the srivijaya dossier to carry it). Region is the Samudera Pasai dossier's.
- kingdom-of-aru (1225-1613). Pages: Aru_Kingdom, Deli_Sultanate. The shape is the east-coast block of Aceh's 1613 gain, so there is no independent evidence of the frontier. The sultanate-of-aceh dossier uses the string "Aru" (not this slug) and a separate 1612 "Deli" disc. The majapahit dossier lacks Aru as a vassal (page: Majapahit 1339 and 1365).
- sumedang-larang (1585-1620). Pages: Sumedang_Larang_Kingdom, Mataram_Sultanate. Shape: Sunda's last Cliopatria shape intersected with an 80 km disc on Kutamaya. The Mataram suppression of Sumedang and Ukur 1631-36 falls after the end and is in the notes, not in wars. The sunda-kingdom dossier lists this slug as successor.
- kutai-martadipura (399-1635). One page, thin, no infobox. The shape mirrors the kutai-kingdom dossier's 1635 gain. "Kutai Lama" is not in the gazetteer (6 resolve warnings, all this one name). Muara Kaman coordinates approximate. Page and the kutai-kingdom dossier disagree on the sultanate's start (page: end of the 13th century or 1300).
- nan-sarunai (1309-1362). One page. It contradicts itself: infobox ends 1389 with Majapahit vassalage 1354-1389, the text says the kingdom "fell in 1362". The dossier ends at 1362 so it does not overlap Negara Dipa. The majapahit dossier uses the slug `nan-sarunai` as counterpart (two events) but has no vassal span.
- negara-daha (1380-1526). Pages: Negara_Dipa, Negara_Daha, Nan_Sarunai. One state under two names: Negara Dipa to 1477, renamed Negara Daha 1478 (capital moved); ends 1526 with the sultanate-of-banjar's start. The Daha page has no end date. The gap 1362-1380/1387 is explained on neither page. The "majapahit" counterpart for the founding is my reading (confidence 2).
- luwu (1300-1905). Pages: Kingdom_of_Luwu, Wajo_Kingdom, South_Sulawesi_expeditions_of_1905. Start c.1300; rename Feb 1605 (Islamisation); capital_move c.1620. End 1905 is a bridge from the campaign page title (the page runs to 1954). Territory: only "northern South Sulawesi" and four regencies. Ruler strip omitted.
- wajo (1400-1905). Pages: Wajo_Kingdom, Makassar_War. The disc on Tosora is mine. Gowa's Islamisation war forced Wajo in 1610 (contest 1610 pair vs sultanate-of-gowa). The 1670 fall to Bone and the VOC, the 18th-century peak and La Maddukelleng are not dated by territory.
- siak-sultanate (1722-1946). One thin page, no territory, conflicting reign dates. The sultanate-of-johor dossier keeps mainland Riau until 1824, so the two overlap from 1722 (no loss recorded there). The sultanate-of-malacca dossier has "Siak" (a pre-1722 string) as counterpart: it is not this state.
- kertabhumi-majapahit (1468-1478, rebel). Only the Majapahit article was used; the Kertabhumi and Brawijaya_V pages failed. The majapahit dossier calls the counterpart "Kertabhumi (rebel Majapahit at Trowulan)", which does not equal this slug. Single Trowulan disc.
- sultanate-of-bacan (1322-1889). Pages: Sultanate_of_Bacan, Maluku_Islands. 1609 Dutch/Ternate invasion; loss of the Obi Islands 1682; Nuku's occupation 1797-1806 (its end is confidence 1). Tidore's dossier uses this slug as the counterpart of that occupation. The 1609-1889 vassal tint belongs to the dutch-republic and dutch-east-indies dossiers. The Raja Ampat legend and Gebe are undated.
- sultanate-of-buayan (1350-1905). One page (Buayan). The page gives 1350 and 1905, no frontier. The 1619-1671 vassal pair over Maguindanao is confidence 1 (the page says only "declines" in 1619 and "heavy influence" in 1648); it mirrors the Maguindanao dossier's span over "Buayan (rival sultanate)" in South Cotabato plus General Santos. The Spanish vassal pair 10 March 1887 to 1898: the end is page-silent. The infobox predecessor Kiliman has no dossier.
