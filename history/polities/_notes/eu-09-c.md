ONE STATE OR TWO. 'Holy Roman Empire Minor States' (1260..1806) and 'Holy Roman Empire' (962..1259, plus a stray 1619..1621 shape) share Wikidata Q12548 and the article 'Holy Roman Empire': they are one empire, split by Cliopatria at 1260. The orchestrator may merge them later; the keyframe ids here are all slug 'holy-roman-empire-minor-states_*'.

WHAT THE ROW IS. Not the whole Empire but its residual: the umbrella '(Holy Roman Empire)' (peak 1,213,036 km2) holds the great members as leaf shapes (Bavaria, Habsburg, Bohemia, Saxony, Brandenburg, Trier, Lorraine, the Swiss, Savoy, Milan, Venice, ...); this row is what is left, shrinking from 517,661 km2 in 1260 to 4,248 km2 in 1806. Most of its 95 shape changes are therefore a leaf shape being cut out or handed back, not an event of this state, and the pages opened date only a few of them. Each change of 15 or more cells has a ledger event naming the leaf shape that takes or returns the cells (confidence 1, 'page silent', where no page was opened); changes of fewer cells are described in the keyframe notes only. The HRE page gives an infobox area only for 1150 (1,100,000 km2), so no infobox figure could be set against the row's areas.
