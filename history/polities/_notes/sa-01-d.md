## magadha-shaishunaga-dynasty (-413..-345)
- Cliopatria dates are shifted: row -404..-334 against the infobox 413..345. New keyframe -413..-405 closes the gap left by magadha-haryanka-dynasty (ends -413); the last Cliopatria shape (-337..-334) is verdict wrong for this slug (its years are the Nandas').
- The three Cliopatria shapes are one identical 797,305 km2 polygon (3,069 cells). It holds the Haryanka land plus the Avanti, Vatsa and Cedi discs and about 1,800 cells (Madhya Pradesh, Bangladesh, Uttar Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Odisha, West Bengal, Rajasthan) that neither page supports; it has no Matsya. Corrected to the sibling hand-over regions (Haryanka -413 dissolve, Avanti successor land, Matsya dissolve): about 516,000 km2. The regions are copied, not referenced; if a sibling changes its region, re-copy. The corrected shape is a page-supported minimum and may be too small.
- Events at confidence 1 (page silent on the year): Avanti with Vatsa (-404; both pages give the conquest, no year; reign c. 413-395), capital to Pataliputra (-395, Kalashoka's accession), Matsya (-350; the page lists Matsya only in the infobox).
- Kosala: the Shishunaga page says he "conquered kingdoms of Kosala and Vatsa"; the Haryanka dossier already holds the Kosala land at -413, so no Kosala gain is written.
- Asmaka: janapada-of-asmaka dissolves to this state in -345, but neither page names Asmaka and neither Cliopatria shape contains the Asmaka disc, so there is no event. The Asmaka dossier's counterpart should probably be nanda-empire or nobody.

## nanda-empire (-345..-322)
- Cliopatria row -333..-319 against infobox c. 345..c. 322. New keyframe -345..-334 (the Shaishunaga state); the Maurya Empire row in Cliopatria starts -318, so the Maurya dossier should start -322 (gap -321..-319 otherwise).
- West edge: Cliopatria reaches 65.87E (Sind, Baluchistan, part of Pakistan's Punjab: 740 cells). The Nanda page says "from present-day Punjab in the west" and puts Alexander's army at the Beas with the Nanda territory beyond; the Alexander page has the Macedonian Empire annexing north-west India. All of Pakistan is removed in the corrected shape (a reading of mine, confidence 2; some Bahawalnagar-Cholistan cells east of the Sutlej may belong).
- The Cliopatria neighbours in the seed (ptolemaic-kingdom -326..-324, macedonian-empire -323..-319) are Alexander's empire, not Nanda losses: no loss/contest/occupation event is written. The `ptolemaic-kingdom` row is the known mislabel.
- Growth from the Shaishunaga shape to 1.56 M km2 in -333 is explained only by confidence-1 gains: Pañcāla (-340) and Surasena (-334) with the years the sibling dossiers use, then at -333 the Kuru disc, Odisha (Kalinga canal) and an unnamed remainder of about 775,000 km2 (Rajasthan 1,138 cells, Madhya Pradesh 550, Uttar Pradesh 372, Chhattisgarh 154, Haryana 154, Bangladesh about 370). The page's conquest sentences are undated and hedged. These are the dossier's largest gap; south (Chhattisgarh, 20N) and east (Bangladesh) edges are unverified.
- Not used: Asmaka (theory only), Haihaya (Narmada valley, undated), Mithila (already Magadha's), Jain tradition of coastal conquest, Kuntala inscriptions (page: not reliable). No ruler strip (page gives only Mahapadma c. 345-340 and Dhana Nanda c. 329-322).
