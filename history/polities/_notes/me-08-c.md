## Qarmatians (qarmatians)
- Cliopatria's four shapes are a coastal strip (Qatif, Bahrain, Qatar, the UAE coast) and leave out the al-Hasa oasis around Hofuf, where every page puts the capital; all are adjusted to add it (place Hofuf, 60 km).
- The drop from 64,680 km2 (970-979) to 6,700 km2 (980-1065) is not explained by any page opened ("after their defeat by the Abbasids in 976, the Qarmatians began to look inwards"): unverified within the budget. The Battle of Kufa (977/978), the loss of Hajar, a capital called Mu'miniya, Yamama (the Abu Sa'id page: "probably not placed under Qarmatian control"), Ray and Buyid-Qarmatian relations 973-978 are on no page opened. A gap-filler could open the Arabic-language or Seshat pages, or "Bahrayn (historical region)".
- End: 1067 (Agius, quoted on the Qarmatians page), 1076 (Uyunid infobox), 1077 (Qarmatians infobox, used), 1077-1078 (Uyunid text). Cliopatria ends the row in 1071 and starts uyunid-emirate in 1072, so both shapes cover 1072-1077 if both dossiers are drawn; the Uyunid dossier (another part of me-08) should start at 1077.
- Hajar: the Qarmatians page has Hajr taken in 899, the Abu Sa'id page in 903 (and the Abbasid governor Ibn Banu claimed Qatif in late 903). Both are written; the 899 found event is confidence 2.
- Bahrain island broke away in 1058 under Abu al-Bahlul al-Awwam (the page's spelling): a small rebel entity with no dossier; how long it held is not on any page opened.
- The Qarmatian ruler of Multan (Abdul Fateh Daud; Mahmud's expedition of 1005) is mentioned only in passing; the Zakarawayh revolts of 901-907 are named as uncoordinated with Bahrayn. Neither is written.
- 968: al-A'sam beat the Ikhshidid governor before Ramla (28 October 968) and the Qarmatians took Damascus for a tribute of 300,000 dinars; no ledger event (no page opened gives the end of that tie), mentioned in the 971 event note.
- Rulers: only Abu Sa'id, Sa'id and Abu Tahir (to 944); after 944 "his three surviving sons and nephews" ruled together and no list was opened.
