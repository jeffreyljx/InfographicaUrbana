## Per state
- **yayoi-period** (-300..300, type culture like jomon-period). Pages: Yayoi period, Kofun period, History of Japan. No area, no extent, no dated spread on any: region = the jomon-period dossier's handover (Japan without the Ryukyus and without Hokkaido, Aomori, Iwate, Akita), confidence 2. After 300 the land outside Nara is nobody's (confidence 1) until yamato's vassal events at 350; 300-350 is blank unless a Kofun/Wa dossier is added. Dates: lead c. 300 BC - AD 300; History of Japan says onset around 400 BC or 1,000-800 BC by radiocarbon. Epi-Jomon (Hokkaido, Aomori, Iwate, Akita; jomon-period loss to 'epi-jomon' at -300) has no dossier and was not in the owned list. Chinese records (57 AD gold seal to Na, Wei zhi 257, Himiko after a 'major civil war') are not territory events; no war is listed (no year for the civil war).
- **yueshi-culture** (-1900..-1500, type culture). Pages: Yueshi culture, Erligang culture. Shandong (868 cells, from the Longshan handover) plus a Shangqiu disc (Panmiao, eastern Henan) and Xuzhou and Lianyungang discs (north Jiangsu); about 300,000 km2. The page's 'retreated to the Shandong Peninsula' is undated and not drawn (Panmiao component dated c. 1900-1450 BC). The north Jiangsu gain is confidence 1. Successor shang-dynasty (Erligang culture, dated 'approximately 1600 to 1400 BC' on its page, which says nothing about Shandong).
- **shijiahe-culture** (-2500..-2000, type culture). Pages: Shijiahe culture, Longshan culture (checked; it does not mention Shijiahe). Shape = the qujialing-culture handover copied cell for cell (916 cells). No successor on the page ('Followed by' empty); the land is nobody's at -2000. 'Warfare with the expanding Longshan culture' is only a possible cause of decline, with no date: no war listed.
- **xiaoheyan-culture** (-3000..-2000, type culture). Pages: Xiaoheyan culture, Hongshan culture, Lower Xiajiadian culture, Longshan culture. The Xiaoheyan page contradicts itself (infobox c. 3000-2500 BC; lead and body about 3500-2000 BC); 3000-2000 follows the Hongshan and Lower Xiajiadian infoboxes. Its own geography is Yanshan north and south, early stage to Zhangjiakou, Datong and Shijiazhuang (undated; drawn from -3000 at confidence 1), later only north of Yanshan after Hougang II replaced it at the southern foot ('later Longshan period'; bridged to -2600 from the Longshan page's 2600-2000 late period, confidence 1). Hongshan's West Liao basin is gained at -2900 (mirrors the hongshan-culture dissolve). Lower Xiajiadian (2200-1600 BCE) has no dossier and starts 200 years before the Xiaoheyan ends.
- **michelsberg-culture** (-4400..-3500, type culture). Pages: Michelsberg culture, Rossen culture, Wartberg culture. Emerges in northeastern France c. 4400; the spread over central Germany, eastern Belgium and the southwestern Netherlands is undated and is drawn at -4300 (the Rossen's end, confidence 1). 'Central Germany' read as Hesse and Thuringia with the Rhine states; Bavaria, Switzerland and Bohemia not on the page. Successor wartberg-culture (3,600-2,800 BC, no dossier); Pfyn and Funnelbeaker are infobox followers without land.
- **ertebolle-culture** (-5400..-3950, type culture). Pages: Ertebolle culture, Funnelbeaker culture, Kongemose culture. Denmark, Schleswig-Holstein, Skane, Halland and Blekinge from the start (the page says only 'concentrated in Southern Scandinavia'; Scania named once); Baltic coast to Rugen after 4,100 BCE (discs at Wismar, Rostock, Stralsund). Slug: tools/slug.py would give 'erteb-lle-culture' (validator warns); the brief's slug is kept. Kongemose (circa 6,000-5,200 BCE) and the related Swifterbant (5,300-3,400 BCE) have no dossier.
- **rossen-culture** (-4600..-4300, type culture). Pages: Rossen culture, Michelsberg culture, Linear Pottery culture. Germany except the north (9 Lander plus a Hildesheim disc for southern Lower Saxony), the southeast Low Countries, Lorraine, Alsace, Champagne, northern Switzerland (Basel to Thurgau); the Austrian part is not located on the page and is not drawn. The infobox line 'Northern and Central Hesse, Westphalia, South Lower Saxony, West Thuringia' is identical to the Wartberg culture's and is treated as a copy, not a core. At -4300 the Rhine overlap goes to michelsberg-culture and the rest to nobody (confidence 1; post-Rossen groups Bischheim and Gatersleben, Lengyel, Pfyn and Baalberge have no dossier).
- **shakkanakku-kingdom-of-mari** (-2266..-1830, type state). Pages: Mari, Syria; Shakkanakku (a stub); Akkadian Empire; Third Dynasty of Ur. The Mari page gives Ididish c. 2266, Akkad's direct control, independence under Shar-kali-sharri, nominal Ur hegemony, the end before the Lim dynasty c. 1830, but no territory: the 60 km Mari disc until independence (-2217, the accession year of the Akkadian Empire page, confidence 1), then the mari dossier's middle Euphrates reading. No rulers list. Vassal relations (Akkad, Ur III) belong in those suzerains' ledgers.
