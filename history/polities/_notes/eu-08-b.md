Written 2026-10-25 by the Phase B agent for batch eu-08-b (one state, nine pages opened of the nine allowed for 26 shapes).

ONE STATE OR TWO. Cliopatria's 'Holy Roman Empire' (962..1259, plus a stray 1619..1621 shape) and 'Holy Roman Empire Minor States' (1260..1806) share Wikidata Q12548 and the Wikipedia article 'Holy Roman Empire'; they are one empire, split by Cliopatria at 1260 for the directly held land. eu-09 owns holy-roman-empire-minor-states and writes it separately, so this dossier lists only the Name 'Holy Roman Empire', ends at 1259 and names the second row as successor (a row boundary, not a collapse; the dissolve event is at confidence 1 and its note says so). The colour agent should treat the two as one colour (family germany); the state page should link them. The article's infobox starts the Empire at 'Charlemagne crowned Emperor: 25 December 800'; this dossier starts at 962 with Cliopatria and the Kingdom of Germany dossier (eu-08-a), whose successor it is.

THE 1619..1621 SHAPE is not the Empire: 604 cells in Bohemia, Moravia, Silesia and Lusatia (the Lands of the Bohemian Crown during the Bohemian Revolt). Marked wrong, correction: use the Minor States shape for the Empire in 1619-1621. The Kingdom of Bohemia / Habsburg groups should decide whom the fragment belongs to (the Bohemian Revolt page was not opened: outside the group's scope to 1250).

WHAT THE SHAPE IS. The leaf 'Holy Roman Empire' draws the directly held Empire: the German kingdom, Austria, the Italian kingdom north of the Papal States (Corsica in some years), Bohemia and Moravia (1010-1201), and from 1034 the Kingdom of Burgundy (Arles; 480 of its 543 new cells are the Arles shape of 936-1033). Kingdom of Germany and Kingdom of Italy are not separate shapes after 969; the Arles shell is inside this shape (eu-08-a's request: record Arles as a component: done in the 1034 gain). The Kingdom of Bohemia is a vassal 1198/1202-1259 (Cliopatria relation 1202..1259), the personal union with the Kingdom of Sicily (Cliopatria 1216..1259) is not territory and has no ledger type; both are for the state page.
