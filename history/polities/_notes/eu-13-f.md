## giudicato-of-gallura (866-1287)

- Cliopatria ends the row in 1209. The judicate ran on under the Visconti judges (from 1207) until Nino Visconti was deposed in 1287 (Gallura text), 1288 (History of Sardinia) or 1296 (Gallura infobox "Disestablished"). 1287 is used. Pisa then held Gallura until the Aragonese conquest of 1323-1324 (Gallura and Pisa pages): a matching gain for republic-of-pisa in 1287.
- The Gallura page says of Logudoro "in 1236 it was acquired through marriage to Adelasia"; the Adelasia page has her acclaimed judge in her own right in 1236 and holding the titular judgeship of Gallura 1238-1257 while the Gallura page has Giovanni Visconti succeeding Ubaldo in 1238. Not resolved; no territory event.
- 1211-1217: Torres and Genoa against Gallura, Pisa struck back in 1212, Pope Honorius III forced a settlement on 1 December 1217. No ground recorded, so the war is listed with no zone.

- Row name "Republic of Amalfi"; Wikipedia article "Duchy of Amalfi"; the dossier is named "Duchy of Amalfi". Cliopatria draws 960-1033 only (96 km2, against the 5.7 km2 of the modern comune on the Amalfi page; the pages give no territory).
- The two pages disagree. Duchy page: freed from Lombard domination 839 (after Sicard of Benevento's capture in 838), first duke 958, ruled Salerno 981-983, under Capua 1034, under Salerno 1039, Normans 1073, revolt 1096 put down 1101, revolt 1130, subdued 1131, sacked by Pisa 1135 and 1137. The ledger follows the Duchy page's dated list and writes the 1034-1073 subjection as foreign control (occupation_start/end, counterparts principality-of-capua, principality-of-salerno, norman-italy); the end of the Salernitan control is page-silent and bridged at 1073 with confidence 1.
