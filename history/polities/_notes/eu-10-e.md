- Cliopatria draws the duchy's land as other states for most of its life: West Franks 918, Upper Burgundy c. 950, Kingdom of France 990-1002, Kingdom of Arles 1018-1384 (71-77 of the 137 cells of the gazetteer's Burgundy region, Dijon included). New keyframes 918-1002 and 1018-1384 carry the duchy through; the 1018-1384 shape is a bridge (carried from the 1010 shape; no page gives a boundary). The eu-08 owner of kingdom-of-arles should check the Burgundy part of its shapes.
- The 1385-1481 shapes are the whole Valois composite. eu-10-x wrote burgundian-state (1363-1482, predecessor this slug) over the same row; both carry the 1384-1482 gains. The compile must choose; both end December 1482 (Treaty of Arras, 23 December 1482), one year after Cliopatria's row, to match the Habsburg row of 1482.
- Cliopatria dates the 1432 (Hainaut, Holland, Zeeland) and 1443 (Luxembourg) acquisitions to 1450 and the Somme towns (1435) to 1440: shapes 1431 and 1440 are adjusted. Lorraine (1475-Oct 1476) is in the 1475 shape to 1481: new keyframes 1477 and 1482.
- 1477-1482 is a contest, not an end: the assembly of the duchy acknowledged reincorporation into France on 29 January 1477, French forces seized Artois, Boulogne, Ponthieu and Vermandois, and Franche-Comté was occupied; Maximilian recognised the annexation only in December 1482.
- Pages disagree: Nevers/Rethel leave in 1407 (Burgundian Netherlands) vs Nevers 1404 and Rethel 1406 (Philip II page); Namur bought 1421, taken 1429; Hainaut-Holland-Zeeland 1432 vs 1433; Robert I duke 1032 vs 1025 (French article); the Duchy page's infobox says absorbed 1790, its text 1477; Auxerre 1005 (Robert II page: Landry gave it up; Auxerre page: Landry count to 1028).
- Not done / page silent (updated in the repair, see the section at the end): no page gives an area; the 1395 and 1402 shape changes beyond the Charolais purchase; Treaty of Péronne 1468; Tonnerre "returned" in 1435 (to whom is not on the page); the 1463 redemption of the Somme towns and their 1465 return (no page dates or places it).
- Counterparts without a dossier: County of Namur, County of Auxerre, County of Rethel, County of Chalon (the Bishop of Langres is no state: Dijon 1016 is a caption inside the 1010 shape).
