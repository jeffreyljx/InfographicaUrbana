WHAT CLIOPATRIA DRAWS. Until about 1889 the row draws settled and organised land (the 1890 shape is the first that is whole states), not the sovereignty or treaty extent. The dossier takes the treaty extent as the state's land: each acquisition (Louisiana, Florida, Texas, Oregon, Mexican Cession, Gadsden, Alaska) enters as a whole region on its treaty date, and the 'adjust' corrections add what the row lacks and remove what it draws wrongly. Interior Native-held land inside the treaty lines is therefore drawn as United States land with the caveat that it belonged to nations that am-05 should carry (northwestern-confederacy, the southern nations, the plains and Great Basin peoples); the ledger models the Northwest Indian War as a contest zone, not as a loss. Areas were compared with the infobox 9,833,520 km2 (undated): the row's 2024 shape is 9.44 M km2, ratio 0.96, and the 1890 shape 9.40 M km2; cell-based areas run about 3.5 percent below the polygon areas, so corrected areas were rescaled to Cliopatria's basis.
