## Per state

### persis (-150 to 224, family persia)
- One dossier for the Fratarakas and the Kings of Persis (dynasty change inside one state); begins at the independence the Fratarakas page dates 'circa 150 BC'. Earlier Frataraka decades (c. 280/295 BC on, disputed) are Seleucid land.
- OVERLAP: the pages end the kingdom in 224 (Ardashir V reigns 211/2 to 224), but the sasanian-empire and parthian-empire dossiers treat Persis as Sasanian from 215 (Cliopatria's row). Both claim Fars for 215-224. Suggested render rule: from 215 the sasanian-empire dossier wins, or truncate persis at 214.
- Unsettled: the Fratarakas' seat (Persepolis traditionally, Susa per Kritt); the Kings' dates are by half-century except Vahshir II (c. 206-210), Pabag (205/6) and Ardashir V (211/2-224); no area on any page, so the gazetteer's Persis (about 157,000 km2) is the reading.

### elymais (-147 to 222)
- 140/139 to about 82 BC the Elymais page has direct Parthian control; the dossier therefore has loss -140 and gain -82 against parthian-empire and no shape in between.
- Unsettled: capital (infobox Susa, text says Susa lay east of Elymais); provinces Massabatice, Corbiane and Gabiane are not placed; Luristan not claimed; the end is 221/222 on the infobox, 224 in the parthian-empire dossier.

### characene (-141 to 222)
- Disagreements: founding 141 BC (infobox, Hyspaosines page), 129 BC (Tylos page), 127 BC (Charax Spasinu page, renaming of the city); Hyspaosines held Babylon in 127 and Timarchus retook it in November 127 (Hyspaosines page) against 'forced out in 122' (Parthian Empire page, though Hyspaosines died on 11 June 124 per his page); Trajan 115 (Characene page) against 115-116.
- Left out for want of a dated page: Bahrain (Tylos) and Oman (Meredates, 'king of the Omani', about 131); Failaka lies in the Kuwait country shape. The Tylos page says Characene ruled Bahrain 'until the second century AD', later the Sasanian province of Mazun; the Mazun page was not opened.
- The king list (28 reigns, c. 127 BC to 222 AD) is on the Characene page; no rulers strip entered.

### hatra (116 to 240)
- THE LAND IS A PLACEHOLDER (a 60 km disc round the city; the pages say only 'Upper Mesopotamia'); found event confidence 1. Start 116 is the first dated event (Trajan's siege), not a founding: the infobox says 'Established: 2nd century CE', the table starts the lords (mry') in 128/29, the title of king (malka) is of the 170s.
- Unsettled: Nasru's reign (128 to 140 on the Sanatruq I page, to 176/77 on the Hatra tables); end 240 (Ardashir I page) or 241 (infobox); Sanatruq II as 'a vassal of the Romans under Gordian III' (r. 238-244) against his table dates 207/08-229/230 and a Sasanian fall in 240/41.
- Tanukhids and Salihids (Arab foederati of the same region) were opened and NOT entered: the Tanukh page calls the Tanukh a tribal confederation, not a kingdom, and names no capital; the Salihids page gives a phylarchy with no territory and 'nothing further is heard' after about 529.

### kingdom-of-kinda (450 to 528)
- Start 450 is the Kinda page's 'mid-5th century'; the Kingdom of Kindah infobox says c. 3rd century to c. 528. Land is the gazetteer's Nejd (about 551,000 km2) as a soft tribal hegemony. Capital Qaryat al-Faw is the Kingdom page's, but the Qaryat al-Faw page has the Kinda abandoning it about 300. No successor named: after 528 the Kinda flee to Hadramawt and lose the Yamama about 550-580 (Shi'b Jabala). al-Harith's capture of al-Hira has no year (a window 498-528).
- The Hejaz towns (Mecca, Medina, Ta'if) of the scope: no page opened presents them as states with land before Islam, so no dossier. The Kinda's original homeland Ghamr Dhi Kinda is 90 km north-west of Mecca.

### osroes-i (110 to 129, type rebel)
- A rival Parthian king with the west, including Mesopotamia, against Vologases III in the east (Vologases III page). The shape is Babylonia minus Al-Basrah and Maysan (Characene's land), one shape 110-129 (the page names no other land; Media is not mentioned). Roman occupation of Seleucia and Ctesiphon 116-117 is an occupation_start/occupation_end pair with roman-empire (the land stays Osroes', after the repair). Mithridates V (129/130 to 140) is named as a contender with no land; not entered.

### vologases-vi (213 to 222, type rebel; was 213 to 228 before the review repair)
- Vologases VI keeps Seleucia while his brother Artabanus IV holds most of the empire 'by 216'. The enclave is a 60 km placeholder disc. End 222: both the Vologases VI page ('continued to rule Seleucia until 221/2') and the Artabanus IV page give 221/2 for Seleucia; 228 is his infobox death and his coin mints (see the repair section).

### bahram-chobin (590 to 591, type rebel)
- Summer 590 to August 591 (Blarathon page). Shape: Babylonia minus the Lakhmid shape of 590, Media, Iranian Khorasan in 590; Media and Khorasan in 591 after Khosrow II retakes Mesopotamia (February 591 Dara and Mardin, 'later' Ctesiphon). Atropatene not drawn.

### vistahm (591 to 596, type rebel)
- Dates 'ca. 590-596' (590/1-596 or 594/5-600 on the infobox); he 'soon held sway in the entire eastern and northern quadrants of the Iranian realm', 'from the Oxus River to Ardabil'; the shape is Khorasan, Tabaristan and a 120 km disc round Ardabil. No capital on the page (empty list). No year for his death at Qumis; Smbat Bagratuni's second expedition 'the next year' ended it.
