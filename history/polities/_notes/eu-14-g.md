## What could not be settled

1. Start year. The Denmark-Norway page dates the dual monarchy 1537-1814 ("Norwegian riksråd abolished: 1537"); Cliopatria starts 1526, the year after its Kalmar Union row ends. The dossier starts 1526 (found event, confidence 1) to avoid an 11-year hole; 1537 is a rename event with no territory.
2. Wikidata Q62651 is shared with norway-denmark (1046-1055, one shape). The page dates this union 1537-1814, so that row is a different state (the 11th-century union) and is not merged. The norway-denmark dossier (another part) should not use this dossier.
3. Greenland. The page lists Greenland (2,166,086 km2 in 1800) as a Norwegian overseas possession and 2,655,567 km2 as the 1800 total; no Cliopatria shape includes Greenland (no cell west of -24.4 E) and no page opened dates a Danish-Norwegian acquisition. It is undrawn. Without Greenland the page's total is 489,481 km2 against Cliopatria's 526,384 km2 (ratio 1.08).
4. Pomerania. Article VII of the Treaty of Kiel promised Swedish Pomerania to Denmark, but Charles XIII released his Pomeranian subjects on 1 October 1815 and Prussia took it on 23 October: Denmark never held it. Cliopatria draws it Danish in 1814 (14 cells); corrected in the 1814 keyframe of the second dossier. Danish-held Swedish Pomerania of the Great Northern War ("returned to Sweden", Frederiksborg page, no dates) is not drawn.
5. Holstein and Jutland 1629-1631. Cliopatria hands 107 of 127 removed cells to its Habsburg Monarchy row; the Treaty of Lübeck page says the treaty "restored to Christian his pre-war possessions" (22 May 1629). Verdict "wrong", use the 1632 shape. The 55 Lower Saxon cells added in 1626 are treated as a contest zone (the page: "claims to Lower Saxon bishoprics"), not Danish land.
6. Gottorp Schleswig. Cliopatria gives the ducal part of Schleswig (33-35 cells) to Holy Roman Empire Minor States from 1662 to 1712 (eu-09-c already doubts it). The Holstein-Gottorp page has the dukes' sovereignty from the Treaties of Roskilde (1658) and Copenhagen (1660), Danish troops in the Great Northern War, "full control over Schleswig" in 1720. I used holy-roman-empire-minor-states as the counterpart (the row that carries the cells) and named the duchy of Holstein-Gottorp in the notes; it deserves its own dossier. The County of Oldenburg (Danish 1667-1773) likewise.
7. Sweden's two rows. The Danish neighbour is kingdom-of-sweden to 1611 and swedish-empire from 1612. Pairs that straddle 1611 (Kalmar War) use swedish-empire. The Swedish dossiers should mirror: 1645 Jämtland, Härjedalen, Idre-Särna, Gotland, Ösel, Halland gained; 1658 Scania, Blekinge, Bohuslän, Trøndelag, Bornholm gained; 1660 Trøndelag and Bornholm lost; 1563-1570 and 1658 contests.
8. Denmark 1940-1945. Cliopatria keeps the Faroes only in 1940 and nothing in 1941-1944; Nazi Germany's shape contains 197 of Denmark's 202 cells in 1940-1944. The Danish state went on under occupation to 5 May 1945 (the pages say the government "remained functioning de jure", dissolved by the Germans on 29 August 1943). A gap-filler should decide whether 1940-1945 is drawn as a hatched occupation of Denmark. The second dossier ends in April 1940 (a dissolve event) to match the row.
