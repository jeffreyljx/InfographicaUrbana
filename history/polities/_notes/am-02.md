## Disagreements between pages

- Naranjo 626-680: the Naranjo page has two Caracol attacks in 626, Calakmul retaking Naranjo in 631 and Caracol eliminating K'ahk' Xiiw by 680; the Caracol page (caracol dossier) has Caracol winning a star war in 631 and Naranjo independent in 680. The Calakmul dossier has Naranjo free of Calakmul by 626. The vassal_end of Naranjo from Calakmul in 695 is a bridge.
- Quirigua: installation of K'ak' Tiliw is 724 and 725 on the same page; the beheading of Copan's king is 3 May 738 there and April 738 in the copan dossier.
- Tecpan Guatemala: 25 July 1524 (Kaqchikel people page) or 27 July 1524 (conquest and Iximche pages).
- Tz'utujil defeat: 1523 (Tz'utujil people) or 18 April 1524 (conquest page).
- Ceh Pech capital conquered 1538 (Ceh Pech page) against permanent Spanish settlement of Yucatan 1541-42 (conquest page).
- Chichen Itza: archaeology has it declining by 1100; the chronicles and the Mayapan page have Hunac Ceel conquering it in the 13th century (some sources 1221). The Chichen dossier ends 1100; the 1221 war is a no-zone war in the mayapan dossier.
- Mayapan: abandoned 1441 (written records) or about 1461 (archaeology); the Xiu page's infobox puts the kuchkabals "after 1461", other province infoboxes say "Established: 1441".
- Piedras Negras: infobox "Built c. 200 AD" and "Classic-Postclassic", text says abandoned by 930; the page calls the 808 captor "Skull III" and "Skull IV".
- Coba: infobox coordinates 20°29′24″N 87°43′55″W against 19°29.6' in the body.
