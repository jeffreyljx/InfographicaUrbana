# Notes for batch af-08-x (additions: states Cliopatria lacks, Africa 1830-2026)

Written 2026-10-24/25. This batch owns no Cliopatria row: every dossier is a new state (cliopatria_names empty, every keyframe new with verdict adjust). The Wikipedia articles were opened with WebFetch, which summarises long pages and often returned 'not on the page' for dates asked; those silences are recorded rather than filled from memory. No page failed to load. Sections below are each dossier's own notes field.

Cross-cutting points for the reviewer and gap-fillers:

- The colony dossiers (French West Africa, French Equatorial Africa, Anglo-Egyptian Sudan, Italian Libya, German East Africa, the Nigerias) sit inside Cliopatria's continental aggregates (French Africa, British Africa, German Africa, Italian Africa). The renderer should decide whether the aggregate row or the colony dossier draws the land; they overlap by design.
- The gazetteer's cell counts undercount deserts (Libya resolves to 0.78 of its infobox area, Western Sahara's country polygon to the controlled zone only), so area comparisons in keyframe notes are rough.
- Counterparts used as slugs but without a dossier yet: french-togoland, maakhir, senussi-order, ssc-khatumo, uganda-protectorate (af-05). The dossiers british-cameroons, tanganyika-territory, tripolitanian-republic and puntland were added by this batch.
- Region names the gazetteer lacks: Halaib Triangle, Lado Enclave, Sarra Triangle, Suakin (place), Neukamerun, Dar al-Kuti, British Cameroons, Kionga Triangle, Maakhir.
- The Federal Republic of Somalia and Morocco dossiers (other groups) need the mirror events: a 1991 loss of the north to Somaliland; the occupation of Western Sahara from 1976.

## French West Africa (french-west-africa, 1895-1958)

French West Africa is a colony-type federation inside Cliopatria's French Africa row (1846-1957), which draws every French territory in Africa as one shape; this dossier gives the federation its own outline and dates. Cliopatria has no row for it, so every keyframe is new and built from modern country outlines, which overstate the 1895-1900 French position inland.
Disagreements between pages: Mauritania joins in 1904 (History of Mauritania) or 1920 (federation page); Upper Volta is made in 1919 from Ivory Coast provinces (History of Burkina Faso) or in 1921 by splitting French Sudan (federation page); Niger is a constituent in the infobox but 'out of the federation until the 1940s' in the text. The dossier follows the country pages for the dates and lists the other reading in each note.
Pages that were thin: the federation article has no section on the conquest and gives no Governors-General; the three 'History of' pages and the Wassoulou and Toucouleur pages give years but not boundaries. The 1895-1900 shape therefore does not show the unfinished conquest of the hinterland; the contest zones for Samory (1895-98) and the Mossi (1896-1901) mark it. The Franco-Dahomean wars and Archinard's Soudan campaigns (Nioro 1891, the Toucouleur empire ended 1893) precede the founding and are carried by the French Africa dossier.
Not covered for want of a page in the budget: the Cote d'Ivoire and Guinea conquest details, the 1932 Upper Volta partition by colony, the 1947 restoration as an outline change (internal), the Kaocen and Tuareg wars in Niger, the 1912 Adrar conquest, Vichy-era loyalties (1940-42, Dakar), and the exact autonomy dates of 1958-60. Counterparts needing dossiers: french-togoland (1916-1957, League of Nations mandate). The Mali Federation (1959-60) has its own dossier (mali-federation).
Gazetteer: no new names needed (Sikasso admin1 carries 'Wassoulou' as an alias).

## Anglo-Egyptian Sudan (anglo-egyptian-sudan, 1899-1956)

Cliopatria has no Anglo-Egyptian Sudan row (it lies inside the aggregate 'British Africa' 1885-1960), so every keyframe is new and built from modern outlines. Dates: the Halaib Triangle page gives the Condominium agreement as 19 January 1899, the Anglo-Egyptian Sudan infobox 19 June 1899; the dossier follows the page that quotes the agreement. The dossier ends on 1 January 1956, the day the Republic of Sudan row starts.
Not settled for want of a page: the southern frontier and how much of Equatoria and Bahr el Ghazal the Condominium actually held in 1899 (the keyframes draw the modern South Sudan outline from 1899, which is generous for the first years); the Sudan-Uganda exchange of 1914 (area unknown); the Egypt-Sudan boundary change at Wadi Halfa; the Ilemi triangle and Kafia Kingi; the 1924 events (the page only says Britain edged Egypt out 'almost completely by 1924' and divided the country into two 'from 1924 onwards'); Bahr el Ghazal's 1948 split from Equatoria is internal. Egypt as co-ruler is not drawn as a separate layer.
Counterparts needing dossiers: italian-libya (1911-1943) and uganda-protectorate (1894-1962, af-05 lists it). Gazetteer: 'Halaib Triangle', 'Lado Enclave', 'Sarra Triangle' (regions) and 'Suakin' (place) are real names the gazetteer lacks; the Darfur outline is the five Darfur admin-1 states.

## French Equatorial Africa (french-equatorial-africa, 1910-1958)

Cliopatria has no French Equatorial Africa row; the colonies sit inside the French Africa aggregate (1846-1957). Every keyframe here is new and built from modern country outlines, which are soft claims for the Saharan north and for the Sangha-Ubangi lands.
Not settled: Chad's northern prefectures (Tibesti, Borkou, Ennedi) and the dates of their occupation (the French Chad page says only that the French 'gradually expanded into eastern and northern Chad'); Chad's status within the federation (colony 17 March 1920 to 30 June 1934, then unitary AEF in 1934 on the federation page, regions in 1934, territories in 1937); the formal return of Neukamerun (1916 seizure, the post-war line undated on the Neukamerun page; the French Africa dossier dates it 1919); the Vichy/Free French split of August 1940 (the federation page: 'most of the AEF rallied to the Free French in August 1940'), which is not drawn as a territorial loss because the federation itself went over to Free France.
Disagreement inside the federation page: 1908 versus 1910 for the division into four colonies. Infobox office-holders are partial (Merlin 1908-17, Chauvet 1951-58, Messmer 1958), so no ruler strip is given.
Gazetteer names needed: 'Neukamerun' (region: the lands of the French Congo ceded to Kamerun in 1911-13, mostly in the Sangha basin and western Central African Republic) and 'Dar al-Kuti' (region: northern Central African Republic). Counterparts: dar-al-kuti, wadai-empire (af-03-x/af-01-x dossiers), german-africa and free-french (Cliopatria rows).

## Italian Libya (italian-libya, 1911-1943)

Cliopatria has no Italian Libya row (Libya sits inside its Italian Africa aggregate, 1890-1943, which carries the Italo-Turkish and Senussi dates from other pages). Every keyframe is new; the early ones (1911-1922) are the least certain because the Italo-Turkish War page lists the enclaves only as they stood on 1 August 1915.
Not settled: who held the interior 1915-1922 (the page is silent); the Italian occupation of Fezzan before 1930 (the page gives none); the 1925 Egyptian treaty did not cede land on the pages opened (the Jaghbub oasis was named in a 1915 secret Anglo-Italian treaty that Egypt rejected); the Aouzou strip, 114,000 km2, was granted by the unratified Mussolini-Laval agreement of 1935 and the infobox area of 1939 (1,759,541 km2) matches Libya without it, so it is not drawn (the Aouzou page says it was 'added to Italian Libya in 1935 for some years', which no event here carries); the British and Free French dates of 1942-43 (the page: 'In February 1943', 'in 1943'); the Libyan kingdom of 1951 (not on the page). The Italian Libya page has a caption saying Kufra was 'ceded by Egypt in 1919' against a text date of 6 December 1925 for the Egyptian treaty.
Counterparts needing dossiers: tripolitanian-republic (1918-1922/23), senussi-order (c. 1880-1932, flagged by af-02-x and af-08-e). The Emirate of Cyrenaica has a dossier (1949-1951). Gazetteer names needed: 'Sarra Triangle' (region, also used in anglo-egyptian-sudan) and 'Sirtica' / 'Ghibla' (the dossier uses Surt district and Tripolitania instead).

## Northern Nigeria Protectorate (northern-nigeria-protectorate, 1900-1914)

Cliopatria has no Northern Nigeria Protectorate row (the north is inside its Sokoto Caliphate row to 1904 and the British Africa aggregate). The north is drawn from twenty modern states, an approximation; the keyframes 1900-1902 show only the part the British held, which is the claim minus the caliphate (Cliopatria 1900 shape) and Bornu.
Not settled: the Sokoto and Burmi dates of 1903 (the Sokoto Caliphate dossier by af-03 ends in March 1903 on its own page); the Anglo-French convention and the Anglo-German boundary that cut the caliphate's northern and eastern lands away (Adamawa); the protectorate's capital before Lokoja; and the Bornu conquest's detail (Rabih's state, dossier state-of-rabih-az-zubayr, held Bornu 1893-1900 and is not named on the page). The amalgamation of 1914 is carried on to colony-and-protectorate-of-nigeria.
Counterparts: royal-niger-company (af-04 dossier), bornu-empire (Cliopatria row only, to 1894), sokoto-caliphate (af-03 dossier).

## Colony and Protectorate of Nigeria (colony-and-protectorate-of-nigeria, 1914-1960)

Cliopatria has no row for the colony. The Southern Nigeria Protectorate (1900-1914) and the Lagos Colony (1861-1906) have dossiers by af-04 (niger-coast-protectorate, lagos-colony); the north is northern-nigeria-protectorate (this batch). The British Cameroons need their own dossier (counterpart british-cameroons, 1916-1961: flagged by af-08-c, af-08-g, af-08-h); the gazetteer lacks the region 'British Cameroons' (the researcher's reading: Southern Cameroons are the Nord-Ouest and Sud-Ouest regions of Cameroon, Northern Cameroons parts of the Adamawa and Taraba states of Nigeria).
Not settled: the infobox of the colony page is missing (no area, no day of founding); the Nigeria page puts the Cameroons mandate in 1920 and the British Cameroons page in 1922; Bakassi and Lake Chad frontiers; the Second World War moved no border and is not covered; the 1954 Lyttleton Constitution and the 1957/1959 self-government are administrative.

## German East Africa (german-east-africa, 1885-1919)

Cliopatria has no German East Africa row (its German Africa aggregate runs 1871-1918). Every keyframe is new. The colony's core in 1885 is the researcher's circle round Bagamoyo: the page gives treaties without boundaries.
Not settled: how the Imperial administration replaced the Company (the page lists High Commissioners from 1888 and Governors from 1891, no date for the takeover); the 1890 'gain' is the treaty's recognition of the sphere, at confidence 2; the Burundi year (page silent, confidence 1); the Anglo-German boundary of 1910 survey and the Kivu-Mfumbiro dispute with Belgium and Britain over the Rwanda frontier (not on the page); the Maji Maji dates; the Dar es Salaam capture date. The Treaty of Versailles took effect on 10 January 1920, so Tanganyika, Ruanda-Urundi and Kionga were administered by the Allies from 1916 to the mandates.
Counterparts needing dossiers: tanganyika-territory (1919-1961, the British mandate and trust; I intend one if budget allows), ruanda-urundi (1916-1962, this batch), Abushiri and Maji Maji (rebel movements, not states). Gazetteer: 'Kionga Triangle' (region) is a real name the gazetteer lacks; 'Tanganyika' (region) is used for the Maji Maji zone and may also be missing.

## Ruanda-Urundi (ruanda-urundi, 1916-1962)

A small state of two districts, written from three pages (the Ruanda-Urundi article gives no area). The wider Belgian occupation of September 1916 (Kigoma, Karema, Tabora: 'a large portion of German East Africa') is not drawn: Smuts ordered the Belgians back to the Congo, and the pages opened date neither the withdrawal nor the Belgian handing over of Tabora; a gap-filler could add an occupation event for the German East Africa lands (counterpart german-east-africa) from the East African campaign article.
The `rename` events mark status changes of the same state (occupation, cession of 1919, B mandate of 1922, trust of 1946). The administrative union with the Belgian Congo of 1 March 1926 is not a change of land and has no event. Successors: the Cliopatria rows republic-of-rwanda and republic-of-burundi both start in 1963 (polities.csv), so the dossier's end year of 1962 leaves one empty year on the map. The kings and the Tutsi-Hutu revolution of 1959-61 are for the Kingdom of Rwanda and Kingdom of Burundi dossiers.

## Somaliland (somaliland, 1991-2024)

Cliopatria has no Somaliland row; its Federal Republic of Somalia row (1960-2024) still draws the north, so the Somalia dossier needs a loss event in 1991 (and the same for the Puntland and SSC-Khatumo zones). Somaliland is unrecognised: the Somaliland page says it is 'recognised by one UN member state' (Israel, 26 December 2025) and that an Ethiopia memorandum of 1 January 2024 would give Ethiopia a lease of Berbera; none of this moves a border.
Not settled: the extent of Somaliland's actual hold on Sool and eastern Sanaag before 2007 (the pages give no map, and Natural Earth draws the whole claim); Erigavo and Buhoodle events (the Somaliland page only lists them as the Sanaag capital and a Togdheer district); the date of the withdrawal from Las Anod (the pages say 'early 2023'); the 2012-2017 Khatumo State (declared by about 2,300 Dhulbahante representatives on 12 January 2012, 'practically ceased to function' by 2015) and the Aynabo agreement of October 2017. The SNM war of 1988-1991 (offensive on Hargeisa and Burao in May 1988) precedes the state and is not a war entry. The 2023 contest is still open at 2024 ('a military stalemate presently holds').
Counterparts needing dossiers: puntland (1998-2024), ssc-khatumo (2023-2024; renamed North Eastern State of Somalia on 30 July 2025). Gazetteer: no new names (the country Somaliland and the places Laascaanood and Ceerigaabo exist).

## Sahrawi Arab Democratic Republic (sahrawi-arab-democratic-republic, 1976-2024)

Cliopatria has no row for Spanish Sahara or the SADR. The shape here is the republic's claim; what it controls is the Free Zone (gazetteer country 'W. Sahara', about 84,000 km2 against the infobox's 90,000), and the occupation events hatch the rest. The found event's region is the Free Zone, the core; the keyframe is the whole claim.
Not settled: the Spanish Sahara before 1975 (no dossier: Spain's colonial period belongs to the Spanish Empire dossier); the Mauritanian share of 1976 (page silent: the south is the researcher's reading); the date of the Moroccan-Mauritanian partition (Rabat convention of April 1976 is not on the pages); construction dates of the berms (mid-1980s on the war page); the per-cent controlled (20%, 30%, 34% across pages); recognition (36 UN members and South Ossetia per the infobox, 85 'at one time or another'). The Morocco occupation of 1976 and 1979 is still running at 2024, so those occupation_start events have no end; the 2020 contest likewise.
Counterparts: morocco (Cliopatria row; the Moroccan dossier should record the occupation as the mirror), mauritania, kingdom-of-spain (starts 1516 in Cliopatria; Spain's presence ended 26 February 1976). The gazetteer's W. Sahara polygon matches the controlled zone, not the claim; the Moroccan-run regions carry the rest.

## British Cameroons (british-cameroons, 1916-1961)

Written from one page (the British Cameroons article) within the small-state budget. Cliopatria has no row. The Nigeria dossier colony-and-protectorate-of-nigeria records the Cameroons as occupation (1916-1922) and a vassal relation (1922-1960) with counterpart british-cameroons; this is the mirror dossier.
Not settled: the Anglo-French boundary of 1916 and 1919 (not on the page), the Bakassi peninsula (not mentioned), the Northern Cameroons plebiscite of November 1959 (not on the page: only 11 February 1961 is), the separate areas of Northern and Southern Cameroons (the page gives only the 89,526 km2 total), and the 1954 and 1958 autonomy of the Southern Cameroons (not on the page). Gazetteer names needed: 'British Cameroons' and 'Northern Cameroons' (regions); the Southern Cameroons are drawn as the Nord-Ouest and Sud-Ouest admin-1 regions of Cameroon, a modern approximation.

## Tanganyika Territory (tanganyika-territory, 1916-1961)

Cliopatria has no row for the mandate; its Tanzania row (shapes from 1961) follows. The mainland outline cuts out Zanzibar and Pemba (five Natural Earth admin-1 regions) and, until 1921, the Kigoma District. The Belgian occupation of Tabora and Kigoma in 1916 is not clean in the pages: the Ruanda-Urundi page says the Belgians reached Kigoma, Karema and Tabora by September 1916, the East African campaign page that Smuts later ordered them back to the Congo 'leaving them as occupiers only in Rwanda and Burundi', yet the Tanganyika page has Belgium ceding the Kigoma District in March 1921. Tabora is drawn British from 1916 without a page for the handover.
Not settled: the Tanganyika-Kenya and Uganda borders, the Mafia Island status (not on the page), the internal self-government of 1960 (the page says only 'the following year', and a 1961 date of 1 May is not given), governors (the infobox names Byatt 1916-1925 and Turnbull 1958-1961 only, so no ruler strip). Counterparts: german-east-africa and tanzania (Cliopatria row).

## Tripolitanian Republic (tripolitanian-republic, 1918-1922)

A four-year state written from one page (the Tripolitanian Republic article) for the Italian Libya dossier's contest events. Not settled: the republic's leaders (the page names none, only later governors), the exact end (infobox 1922, 'By 1923' in the text), the Fezzan (not mentioned), and the relation to the Cyrenaican emirate (the Italian Libya dossier has the Senussi). The Italian Libya dossier records the same land as a contest zone 1918-1923 and a gain in 1923, one year after this state ends.

## Puntland (puntland, 1998-2024)

Written from three pages; the Puntland article does not mention Las Anod, Tukaraq or the 2023 Las Anod conflict, so Puntland's role there is not dated here (Sool, Sanaag and Cayn page: Khatumo captured Tukaraq from Puntland troops and Somaliland and Puntland recaptured it in a coordinated offensive on 16 June, year not given; the Federal Government acknowledged SSC-Khatumo on 19 October 2023 and Puntland on 17 July 2025 was 'refusing to recognize the Khatumo administration').
Not settled: Puntland's actual hold on Sool and eastern Sanaag between 1998 and 2007 (page silent); the northern limit of its Mudug territory; Galmudug's border; the status change of 31 March 2024, when the government 'no longer recognizes the authority of the Somali federal government' (the page's date; no land changes hands, so no event); the 2012 constitution convention (opened 15 April 2012). Counterparts: federal-republic-of-somalia (Cliopatria row), somaliland (this batch), maakhir (a quasi-state of 2007-2009 with no dossier). Gazetteer names needed: 'Maakhir' (region: eastern Sanaag coast, Badhan and Las Qoray).
