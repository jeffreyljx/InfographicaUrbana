## roman-empire

Roman Empire, -27..395, written by eu-04-a from 14 pages (the page budget); Cliopatria's 55 shapes all have a verdict, plus one new keyframe (roman-empire_395).

DECISIONS.
1. The dossier starts on 16 January 27 BC, not at Cliopatria's 31 (Actium), because the Roman Republic dossier ends the Republic then and carries Octavian's holdings of 31-27 in its own roman_-31 and _-30 keyframes. The two Cliopatria shapes for 31-28 BC are verdicts 'adjust' (years only).
2. The dossier ends on 17 January 395 with split events to western-roman-empire and eastern-roman-empire (the Cliopatria Q12544 sharing with 'Eastern Roman Empire' is treated as a partition: the eastern dossier already says so). 395 is shared with the successors; roman-empire_395 is a new keyframe (the 394 shape with Britain restored).
3. Client-kingdom tints are re-opened on 16 January 27 BC (Thrace, Judaea, Cappadocia, Commagene, Nabataea, the Bosporan kingdom, Galatia, Pontus) because the Roman Republic dossier opens them in the 30s BC and never closes them. The Bosporan kingdom (cimmerian-bosporus) and Armenia's 387 share have no vassal_end: the relation lasts to 395.
4. Tribal peoples (Britons, Ordovices, Chatti, Cantabri, Raetians, the Agri Decumates' inhabitants) are counterpart 'nobody' (rule 8); peoples with a name but no dossier are given as strings.

WHERE I DID NOT FOLLOW THE REVIEWER TO THE LETTER.
- Finding 1 says 'the 270 shape minus the /ledger/80 region'. Cliopatria's 270 shape has already dropped Dacia (516 cells), and /ledger 81 takes Dacia in 271, so the plain 270 shape would leave nothing for that event to remove. I used union(261, 270) minus the /ledger 80 region, which is the shape of roman-empire_261 (keyframe 32) with the 267 loss applied. Run through /ledger 81-84 the state in 274-282 differs from the 283 shape without Dacia by about 1,800 cells (mostly the country-wide Egypt and the Levant region of /ledger 83, which are wider than Cliopatria's Roman East); the 283 keyframe takes over from 283.
- Finding 6 (Mauretania): the gain stays at 40 beside the new contest 40-44. The Roman client kingdoms page dates direct Roman control to 40 (Ptolemy's death); the Mauretania page has the annexation 'Around 44 AD', and Sabalus 'surrendered in 44 AD' (that page gives no start year for Aedemon's revolt and lists his rule as 40-42). af-02's mauretania dissolves at 44, so the Roman gain (40) and the Mauretanian dissolve (44) are four years apart; af-02 may prefer to move one of them. The contested overlay covers the gap.
- Finding 7: the First Jewish-Roman War zone is the whole of Roman Judaea, although the page does not say the rebels held all of it ('Vespasian later subdued most of the province'; the provisional government sat in Jerusalem and 'divided the country into military districts'); it ends in 70 (fall of Jerusalem), not 73/74 (Masada, a single fortress). The Bar Kokhba zone is the gazetteer's Judea (36 cells), the page's 'Judea proper (not the entire province of Judaea)'. Both rebel counterparts are the string 'Jewish rebels of Judaea'; both held ground for years (the Bar Kokhba state 'lasted several years'), so each may deserve a type: rebel dossier (eu-04-x or me-05).
- Finding 5: the strip lost in 298 is the 115 km radius around Aswan intersected with Lower Nubia, the kingdom-of-kush spec; 12 of its 28 cells are cells that Cliopatria's Roman shapes already hold, so the 298 loss also takes a few cells north of the Dodekaschoinos; the Kush dossier is the model, a better strip (Philae to Maharraqa) would be a gazetteer name ('Dodekaschoinos', not in regions.jsonl).
