## Cliopatria rows: one state, two states, or mislabelled

- **Qin Dynasty (-218..-209) and Zhai (-208..-204), Wikidata Q7183**: two states. zhai_-208 (208-207, 1.63 M km2) is the Qin remnant under Qin Er Shi and Ziying; it is reviewed in qin-dynasty as the new keyframe qin-dynasty_-208 and marked wrong in zhai. zhai_-206 (26,000 km2, northern Shaanxi) is Dong Yi's Kingdom of Zhai (Di), a state of the Eighteen Kingdoms. zhai.json has wikidata null (Q7183 is the Qin's).
- **Dai (-208..-204)**: its 208-207 shape (central Hebei, 25,569 km2; 88 of 142 cells lie in the later Changshan shape) is Zhao Xie's rebel Zhao at Julu, not the Dai of the Eighteen Kingdoms (northern Shanxi and northwestern Hebei, which the 206 shape matches). The Eighteen Kingdoms table calls the state "Zhao, briefly called Dai" with the same king, so one dossier keeps the Zhao line (ea-01 zhao and ea-01 notes expect this). It ends October 205 (Han Xin), not 204.
- **Jibei (-208..-204)**: jibei_-208 (146,000 km2) is the whole of Shandong, the revived Qi of 208-207 (the Qi row has no shape for 218-207); marked wrong, years belong to qi. jibei_-206 is the Kingdom of Jibei (northern Shandong), 206 only.
- **Western Chu (-208..-203)**: one state: the rebel Chu of King Huai II (208-207) becomes Xiang Yu's Western Chu (206); Cliopatria's one row is kept. It ends January 202 (Xiang Yu's death), so the 203 shape is extended one year. northern Zhejiang is moved from the Qin remnant to Chu in 208 (Kuaiji page: Xiang Liang governed Kuaiji 209-208).
- **Chang-shan (-206..-204)**: cut to 206-205 (destroyed 205).
- The six rebel shapes of 208 (Western Chu, Yan, Wei, Han, Dai, Jibei) account for 2,685 of the 2,695 cells Qin loses between its 210 and 208 shapes; that is what dates every rebel loss in qin-dynasty to Cliopatria's 208 (Yan 209, from the yan dossier). Those losses are confidence 1 because no page opened names the rebel kings or lands except Chu.
- Qin's southern gains: Cliopatria staggers them (212, 210); the Qin and Nanyue pages date the whole conquest 214, so qin-dynasty_-212 is marked wrong and -210 adjusted to hold 214-210. Zhejiang is added to Qin from 222 (Kuaiji page), matching the minyue dossier's cut of Minyue.
