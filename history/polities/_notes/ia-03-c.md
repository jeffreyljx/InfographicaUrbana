## eastern-moghulistan (Yarkent Khanate), 1514-1680
- Cliopatria's Eastern Moghulistan (1519-1686) is the Tarim Basin: 3,098 of the 3,289 cells of its 1521 shape are in the gazetteer's Tarim Basin and Turfan-Hami is excluded. It is the Yarkent Khanate of the pages (Said Khan, May 1514). The Turpan Khanate page says the Turpan Khanate was "also known as the Eastern Moghulistan"; turpan-khanate (other group) is the Turpan state 1487-1570.
- Not merged with moghulistan (1463-1518), eastern-chagatai-khanate (1492-1511) or western-moghulistan: they tile and are different realms.
- Start 1514 (not Cliopatria's 1519): new keyframe eastern-moghulistan_1514. End 1680 (annexation by Galdan, Altishahr page infobox 1678-1680) against 1705 (Yarkent and Moghulistan pages), 1706 (Altishahr footnote) and 1686 (Cliopatria). Cliopatria's Dzungar shape takes the Tarim in 1683 (2,069 cells) and 1687 (1,211): the dzungar-khanate dossier should gain it in 1680 and may carry the nominal Yarkent khans (Abdu'l Rashid II 1680-82, Muhammad Mumin 1695-1705) as a vassal tint.
- Unsettled: who held Turpan between 1570 (Turpan page: came under Yarkand control) and 1678 (Altishahr page lists the Turfan Khanate beside the Dzungars); the 1678 loss is confidence 1. The eastern frontier before 1570 (Aksu was Mansur's court in 1503 per turpan-khanate). Said Khan's death 1533 or 1531.
