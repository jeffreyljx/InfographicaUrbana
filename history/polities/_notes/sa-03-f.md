## Cross-cutting

- Utpala, Karkota and Kashmir are ONE realm on the same land (Cliopatria rows 'Kashmir' 626-1343, 'Karkota Dynasty' 592-859, 'Utpala Dynasty' 960-1002, 'Lohara' 1003-1259). The kashmir dossier (another batch) carries 625-1339 with rename events (rule 2). utpala-dynasty here is a dynasty-period dossier (type dynasty) whose shapes, ruler strip and events mirror the kashmir ones exactly, so a merge loses nothing. Cliopatria's Utpala shapes (597 cells; 7 of 56 Valley cells; 0 of the 370 cells of its own Kashmir shape of 960) are a ring of Gilgit-Baltistan, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Xinjiang, Ladakh, the Pamirs and Badakhshan that neither page supports: verdict wrong, replaced. Recommendation: draw ONE of the two (kashmir) for 855-1003, or the colour agent will paint the Valley twice.
- Wikidata Q1068371 is shared by 'Chauhan Dynasty' (this batch) and 'Han Dynasty' (han-dynasty): they are different states; the id was kept in chauhan-dynasty as Cliopatria gives it and should be checked by someone who can reach Wikidata.
