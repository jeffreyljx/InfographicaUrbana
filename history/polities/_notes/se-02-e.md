- **Sultanate of Gowa (1512..1669)**: both Cliopatria shapes are the same five-cell core. REPAIRED 2026-10-19: the dossier now runs to 1911 (loss of independence), not June 1669; see the repair round below.

## Disagreements between pages

- Somba Opu fell on 12 June 1669 (Gowa page) or about 22 June 1669 after an assault from 14 June (Makassar War page; infobox end 24 June).
- Kedah was conquered by Aceh in 1619 (Iskandar Muda text), 1620 (its footnote, Ricklefs) or 1629 (Aceh Sultanate page).
- Pasai: Aceh invaded in 1523 (Acehnese-Portuguese conflicts page) and annexed in 1524 (Samudera Pasai page).
- Aceh Sultanate infobox: Founded 1480, capital from 1496, Pedir vassal 1497-1514, reign list from c. 1514; the body credits Ali Mughayat Syah with campaigns from 1520. Dossier starts 1514.
- Blambangan: infobox independence 1478 and a Hindu kingdom to 1768/1777; the Mataram page has it a Mataram vassal from 1639, independent in 1659 and "rejoined Bali" in 1697; the Banyuwangi page says "around 150 years of Balinese occupation".
- Lombok: the Bali page has Gelgel suzerainty over Lombok and West Sumbawa at its mid-16th-century apogee; the Lombok page has petty Sasak states before the 17th century, Balinese in the west from the early 17th century and the Makassarese in the east.
- Bone: infobox 14th century and a rulers' table from 1350; the body says "early 15th century". Gowa: infobox "Established 1320", the text "Not much is known".
- Dalem Baturenggong is c. 1460-1550 in the Bali infobox, which does not fit a Gelgel period starting in 1478.
