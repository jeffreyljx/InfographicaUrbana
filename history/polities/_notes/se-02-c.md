## malaya (1294-1351)
- The Wikipedia page "Malaya" is a disambiguation page. The Cliopatria row is a catch-all for the Malay principalities of the peninsula (Pahang, Kelantan, Terengganu, Patani/Langkasuka, Johor and others; Kedah has its own row, Nakhon Si Thammarat is outside). It is NOT the Federation of Malaya (1958-1962): the shared Wikidata id Q1479726 is the region, the two are different states.
- Kept as an aggregate with soft borders and no capital; the right fix is dossiers for the individual principalities. Pages say "By the late 13th century, the Siamese kings of Sukhothai had brought most of Malaya under their rule" and "In the 14th century, the Hindu Majapahit Empire came into possession of the peninsula" (History of Malaysia); the Tambralinga page has Tambralinga split by Singhasari and Sukhothai in 1278.
- Open: Sukhothai's southern vassals, Pahang, Kelantan, Terengganu, Patani, Langkasuka articles not opened.
