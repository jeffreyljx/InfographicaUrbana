## Colony overlap with Cliopatria (for the me-10 dossiers and the renderer)

- turco-egyptian-sudan, equatoria-province and habesh-eyalet are type colony, so they are drawn as the vassal tint of the metropole. Cliopatria's muhammad-ali-dynasty (1805..1865) and khedivate-of-egypt (1866..1884) shapes already include Sudan (and the Khedivate shape 1877-1884 reaches 1.9 N, 45 E, probably including Harar and the Somali coast): the me-10 dossiers should name turco-egyptian-sudan (and habesh-eyalet for the Red Sea coast) as counterparts for those events, and the renderer should subtract the colony from the metropole's own shape.
