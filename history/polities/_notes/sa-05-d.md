# Notes for batch sa-05-d (Mughals and Marathas, part d)

Written 2026-10-24 by the Phase B research agent. Four dossiers: maratha-empire, thanjavur-maratha-kingdom, sikhs, carnatic-sultanate. Every dossier validates (python3 -I history/tools/validate_dossier.py). Pages were read through WebFetch only; the page budget was respected (Maratha Empire 9 pages, Thanjavur 3 new, Sikhs 2, Carnatic 5).

## maratha-empire (Cliopatria "Maratha Empire", 29 shapes, 1677..1845; dossier 1674-06 .. 1818-06)

- One state from Shivaji's coronation (6 June 1674) to the Peshwa's surrender (3 June 1818). The Peshwa-led confederacy is a `rename` in 1713 (Balaji Vishwanath appointed Peshwa, 16 November 1713). Pages: Maratha Empire, Shivaji, Mughal-Maratha Wars, Baji Rao I, Maratha invasions of Bengal, First, Second and Third Anglo-Maratha War, Battle of Udgir.
- Cliopatria errors found: (1) the 1677-1690 shapes are only the Kolhapur / south Konkan / Karwar patch (no Pune, one cell at Satara, nothing at Vellore, Gingee or Bangalore) while the pages give Shivaji 240-280 forts and 'about 4.1%' of the subcontinent; new keyframes 1674, 1675 and the 1677/1687 adjusts add the homeland and the 1677 conquests. (2) No shape for 1691-1717: bridged at confidence 1 (new keyframes 1691, 1698, 1707). (3) Shapes lag the events by 1-3 years in 1719 (Delhi treaty), 1738 (Malwa ceded 7 January 1738, drawn from 1741), 1760 (Udgir, drawn from 1762) and 1804 (Deogaon and Surji-Anjangaon, December 1803, drawn 1803/1805): new keyframes. (4) The 1820-1845 shape (82,885 km2 around Gwalior) is not the Maratha Empire, which ended in 1818: marked wrong; it belongs to Gwalior (Shinde).
- Infobox area 2,500,000 km2 for 1760 against 1,016,560-1,170,359 km2 (ratio 0.41-0.47): the page counts tribute and raided land; the shape is the ruled core.
- Not settled: Kolhapur as a separate state (split recorded 1707 on 'two rival seats'); chauth (1719 Delhi grant, 1751 Bengal and Bihar chauth) and the 1802 Treaty of Bassein subsidiary alliance are not modelled as vassalage; Salsette and Bassein taken from the Portuguese (page says only 'five years later' than 1733; confidence 1, open 'Battle of Vasai'); Gujarat consolidation date (confidence 1); Trichinopoly garrison 1741-43 and Rohilkhand 1752 (no dated tenure); the Cliopatria changes of 1780, 1783, 1792, 1794 and 1814 (Karnataka, Uttar Pradesh, Odisha, Rajasthan) are unexplained by the pages opened (Third Anglo-Mysore War, Kharda 1795, Treaty of Gajendragad not opened).
- Page conflicts: Palkhed 28 February 1728 (Maratha Empire page) vs 25 February (Baji Rao I page); Pratap Singh's reign ends 1818 (infobox) or 1839 (body); Baji Rao II Peshwa from 1803 (infobox) or 1796 (body); Bhaskar Pant 'killed' and fleeing Katwa on 27 September 1742 on the same page.
- The Portuguese dossier records the 1783 handover of Nagar Haveli to the Marathas; this dossier has no matching `loss` (that page was not opened here).

## thanjavur-maratha-kingdom (5 shapes, 1677..1799; dossier 1676 .. 1799)

- Pages: Thanjavur Maratha kingdom, Serfoji II, Thanjavur Nayak kingdom (Shivaji page reused). Founding by Venkoji (Ekoji I) is 1674 (infobox), 1675 (lead; Nayak page) or early 1676 (text); 1676 used. Ends 1799 (Treaty of 1799: administration transferred to the Company; 'last sovereign ruler'); the principality survived on paper to 1855.
- The Cliopatria shape is the Kaveri delta, bounded on the north by the Kollidam river itself; no area is given on any page. The 2-3 cell differences between the 1677, 1700, 1713 and 1738 shapes are undated.
- Tribute to the Nawab of Arcot (1762 treaty) and the 1773-1776 occupation are in the carnatic-sultanate ledger (vassal and occupation events); the Thanjavur ledger records the 1773 loss and 1776 gain.

## sikhs (2 shapes, 1709..1717; dossier 1709-11 .. 1715-12, type rebel)

- Pages: Banda Singh Bahadur; Battle of Lohgarh (a disambiguation page, no detail). The First and Second Battle of Lohgarh articles were not opened (budget).
- Cliopatria draws one 24,700 km2 polygon (the province of Sirhind, between the Sutlej and the Yamuna) for 1709-1717; it holds only May-December 1710. New keyframes: Samana 1709, Sirhind 1710, hill refuge 1711, Sadhaura-Lohgarh 1712, Jammu hills dera 1713-14, Gurdas Nangal 1715. 1711-1714 holdings are bridges (confidence 1-2).
- The Mughal dossier has no Banda event (checked 2026-10-24): a gap-filler should add the contest (1709/1710..1715) and the recovery of Sirhind.
- Saharanpur and Muzaffarnagar (taken by the Sikhs, undated) are outside the polygon and not in the ledger.

## carnatic-sultanate (7 shapes, 1713..1802; dossier 1710 .. 1801-07)

- Pages: Carnatic Sultanate, Carnatic Wars, Nawab of the Carnatic, Madurai Nayak dynasty, Madras Presidency (plus Thanjavur Maratha kingdom, Serfoji II and Maratha Empire reused). 'Carnatic Sultanate' and 'Nawab of the Carnatic' are near-duplicate articles.
- Start 1710 (infobox 'Independent state 1710-1801'; the Mughal dossier already records the loss in 1710) instead of 1713; end 26 July 1801 (Carnatic Treaty) instead of 1802.
- Cliopatria errors: the 1800-1802 shape adds the whole Thanjavur kingdom (48 cells), which went to the Company in 1799: corrected. The 1738 southern annexation (Madurai) happened in 1736 (new keyframe 1736). The 1738 shape keeps 3 cells of Madras (Chingleput jaghire, 1763).
- Silent on every Carnatic page opened: the Nizam's 1743 intervention and the Maratha garrison at Trichinopoly; Muhammad Ali Wallajah's cessions to the Company; the Northern Circars; Hyder Ali's 1780 invasion; the 1792 Seringapatam settlement; the Nawabs' reign years (no ruler strip).
- The English Colonial Empire dossier records the 1763 Chingleput gain from the Carnatic; this dossier records the matching loss (Madras Presidency page).
