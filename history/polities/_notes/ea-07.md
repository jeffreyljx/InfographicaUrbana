## Cliopatria rows to split, merge or redraw

- Ming Dynasty starts 1375; the dynasty began 23 January 1368 (Hongwu page). Three new keyframes (1368, 1369, 1371) rebuild the conquest from zhu-yuanzhang-s-wu, ming-xia and the Yuan.
- Ming 1375 shape includes Liaoning and Jilin; the Hongwu page dates Manchuria 1387 and Liaodong 1388. Treated as premature.
- Hami and the Amur lands are vassal tint, not direct rule; Cliopatria drops Hami about 70 years too early (1440s against 1513).
- The 1619-1642 Ming shapes drop Liaoning too early (Liaoxi was Ming to 1642-44).
- "Li Zicheng" shapes of 1632 and 1636 are wrong (a rebel band held no land; the cells are Ming); the 1642 shape is right only for March to June 1644. New Shun keyframes 1643, 1644, 1645.
- "Southern Ming" 1662 shape (Taiwan) is the Kingdom of Tungning; the 1673 and 1677 shapes are the Revolt of the Three Feudatories, now the dossier wu-sangui-s-zhou (verdict wrong in southern-ming, years belong there). Taiwan in the 1673 shape is Tungning's.
- Cliopatria has no row for Zhang Xianzhong's Xi (xi-dynasty), Wu Sangui's Zhou, the Jianzhou or the Haixi Jurchens; all four are new dossiers with cliopatria_names empty.

## Disagreements between pages

- Tumu: 1 September 1449 (Tumu page) or 8 September (Ming page).
- Fall of Beijing: 24 April (Qing conquest page) or 25 April 1644 (Ming page, list of emperors); the Shun page has 5 June for the loss of Beijing, the Qing conquest page 6 June.
- Capital move to Beijing: 1421 (Great Wall page), designated 28 October 1420 (Ming infobox), 1403 (Ming text).
- Xi capital 1643: Huangzhou (Xi infobox) or Wuchang (Zhang Xianzhong page).
- Wu Sangui: occupied Hunan, Hubei, Sichuan, Guangxi by April 1674 (Wu page); the Revolt page does not mention Hubei. 11 provinces by April 1676 (Wu page) against the Revolt page's separate risings. Hubei is not drawn; four provinces are drawn as contested.
- Hada: defeated 1601 (Haixi, Hulun, unification pages) or conquered 1603 after an attack in 1599 (Nurhaci page).
- Fushun: Ming commander killed 1473 (Jianzhou page) or 1573 (unification page).
- Joseon: tributary 1401-1637 on the Joseon page, not 1395-1571 as Cliopatria's allegiance row has it.
