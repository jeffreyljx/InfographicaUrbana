## bohemian-confederacy (rebel, 1619-1620)

Written because Cliopatria's Habsburg Monarchy shape of 1619 lacks the Bohemian Crown and no other row draws it. Three pages (Bohemian Revolt, Frederick V Elector Palatine, Thirty Years' War). Formed 31 July 1619 by Bohemia, Silesia, Lusatia and Moravia; Frederick elected 26 August and crowned 4 November 1619; ends at White Mountain, 8 November 1620. Two keyframes (1619; 1620 without Lusatia), four events (found, loss of Lusatia to Saxony, loss of the rest to the Habsburgs, dissolve). Open points: the Habsburg ledger carries the contest from May 1618 and the loss on 19 August 1619, a month after the Confederacy's founding on 31 July; much of Upper Austria joined the revolt (undated, not drawn); no page gives the lands actually held or the surrender dates of Moravia and Silesia; the Lusatia conflict above.
