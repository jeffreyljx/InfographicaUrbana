## Overlap with other dossiers (the orchestrator must decide the drawing)

All nine are sub-units of larger states that other dossiers or Cliopatria rows already draw as one shape. Nothing here subtracts anything from them.

- kingdom-of-the-franks (eu-07-b) draws the whole Frankish realm 509-843 and subtracts only Burgundy (and Austrasia 612-613). Austrasia, Neustria, the Spanish March and Alsace sit inside that shape 511-751 and must be subtracted (or the Franks shape treated as the remainder).
- west-franks books Lotharingia as direct land (911/11, 923-925 contest, 925 loss to kingdom-of-germany); east-franks and kingdom-of-germany likewise 900-959. duchy-of-lotharingia needs vassal_start/vassal_end pairs in those dossiers (911 west-franks, 925 kingdom-of-germany, vassal_end 959) or their direct holding must be dropped.
- duchy-of-francia lies inside West Francia's Cliopatria shapes (850-989).
- march-of-pannonia and principality-of-lower-pannonia lie inside the East Franks 850-910 shape and near Great Moravia, the Magyars and the Principality of Hungary.
- spanish-march 795-897 overlaps Cliopatria's Ribagorza (866), Urgell (870) and Pallars (875) rows, which west-franks books as 870/872/878 losses (Aragon is the valley only after the 2026-10-25 repair); the compile should prefer those rows' cells.
- kingdom-of-lotharingia: Cliopatria draws Lothair II's realm as Lower Burgundy 860-869 (860-865 also contains Charles of Provence's Provence). The 863 and 870 regions are copied from the lower-burgundy, west-franks and east-franks dossiers so the cells match.

- Colour families used: kingdom-of-austrasia, merovingian-neustria, kingdom-of-lotharingia, duchy-of-francia, duchy-of-lotharingia, duchy-of-alsace = france; spanish-march = iberia (its successors the Catalan counties); march-of-pannonia = austria (the Margraviate of Austria is its successor); principality-of-lower-pannonia = east-europe.
