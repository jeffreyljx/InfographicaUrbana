# fix-fx-12 notes (written 2026-11-01)

All eleven Tughlaq and Durrani trims below are page-reading judgements of confidence 2: the pages opened name a local dynasty or rulers for the land in these years and name no Delhi (or Durrani) governor there. Mechanics: tughlaq-dynasty keyframes 1-20 (1320-1407) and ledger/0 (the 1320 found region) now subtract the listed land (the `minus` takes exactly two regions, so the exclusions are one `union` in the second slot); ledger/1-3 (the 1323 Kakatiya events) subtract the Reddi land. durrani-empire keyframes 1762-1811 subtract Rajasthan (and, from 1802, the Bahawalpur country), with a new one-year keyframe durrani-empire_1802. Resolved cell counts before and after are in the report.

- F070 (soomra-dynasty / tughlaq-dynasty, Sind): PARTLY. Lower Sindh (Thatta within 220 km, inside Sind: 306 cells, a reading) is out of tughlaq-dynasty keyframes 1320-1350 and ledger/0; the 1351 keyframe, which was 'without Sind' but still held 67 Sind cells, now drops the whole province. Pages: Soomra dynasty (emirs Khafif III 1315-1333 to Hamir II 1341-1351, 'probably centered in lower Sindh'), Samma dynasty ('in 1333 the Sammas were in rebellion', 'The Sammas overthrew the Soomras soon after 1335', capital Thatta, 'Mohammad bin Tughlaq made an expedition against Sindh in 1351 ... Following this, the Sammas became independent'), History of Sindh, Muhammad bin Tughluq (died at Thatta campaigning against Taghi). No page has Delhi governing lower Sindh before 1351. The overlap with soomra-dynasty falls from 387 to 158 cells (175 in 1344-1350, 0 in 1351): the rest is upper Sind, which soomra-dynasty (read-only) draws as the whole province; handoff to narrow it to lower Sindh.
