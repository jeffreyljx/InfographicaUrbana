# eu-06-x (Migration kingdoms, additions batch): notes

Nine new states, none with a Cliopatria row (`cliopatria_names: []`, every keyframe new). All nine validate with 0 errors, 0 warnings (also with `--resolve`). 49 ledger events and 37 source entries (after the repairs of 2026-10-24/25) (the same page recurs across dossiers); 17 events are confidence 1 (bridges, flagged in each note). Order of importance used: heruli, sciri, danubian-suebi, gothic-foederati, thracian-goths, alans-of-orleans, kutrigurs, utigurs, antes.

## Per state

- heruli (460-567, east-europe). Six pages (Heruli, Battle of Nedao, Battle of Bolia, Odoacer, and after review Bassianae and Lombards; Grepes, Sinduald and Sindual are not Wikipedia pages: url_not_found). Two phases in one dossier: the Danubian kingdom 460-494 (Jihomoravsky plus Bratislavsky and Trnavsky kraj, all north of the Danube) and the Balkan federates 512-567 (heruli_512 Bassianae, heruli_527 Bassianae plus the Roman side of Singidunum, heruli_539 the Singidunum side only), with 495-511 empty. The site of the kingdom is disputed on the page (southern Moravia / south-western Slovakia, or north-eastern Lower Austria). End of the kingdom 494 is Procopius; the page notes 493-511; no page says who held the land afterwards (counterpart 'nobody'). The end of the Balkan phase (567) is the last dated item (Sinduald), not a stated dissolution. No capital, no ruler list.
- sciri (454-468). Five pages. The kingdom between the Danube and the Tisza is undated on the Sciri page (454 is the Nedao year of the same passage). Bolia is 468 on its own page, 469 on the Hunimund page, 468/469 on the Sciri page: 468 used in this dossier and in danubian-suebi. The Sciri on Roman soil in Scythia Minor and Lower Moesia (undated) and Odoacer's Sciri in Italy are not drawn.
- danubian-suebi (453-468). Four pages. Region is admin1 Nitriansky (south-western Slovakia), the Quadic bank opposite north-eastern Pannonia, after review; the first draft's Hungarian counties lay on the ostrogoths dossier's Pannonian land. The marcomanni dossier names this state as successor; no page says the Marcomanni became it, so no Marcomannic land is drawn. The flight west to the Alamanni is 469/470 (Suebi page).
- gothic-foederati (382-395). Three pages (Gothic War 376-382, Visigoths, Thervingi). Treaty 3 October 382 and the provinces 'Scythia, Moesia and possibly Macedonia' are on the Gothic War page; the gothia dossier's hand-over text says 'Thrace', which this page does not. No king, no capital for 382-395. Land drawn (Dobruja plus northern Bulgaria) is certainly larger than what the Goths held.
- thracian-goths (471-481). Four pages. The revolt is 471 on the Strabo page and 473 on the Ostrogoths page. The Goths' extent is not given anywhere ('his people in Thrace'); after review the shape is the Thracian plain between Philippopolis and Arcadiopolis (Plovdiv, Stara Zagora, Haskovo, Edirne, Kirklareli; 92 cells, without Constantinople), soft, contested 471-473 and 474-481 with a federate interval 473-474. The dossier now ends in 481 (Strabo's death); the Ostrogoths page has Recitach killed in 484 and the Zeno page has Theoderic the Amal king of the whole nation after 481, and no page gives Recitach land. The Macedonian strip on the Ostrogoths page has an unclear subject and is not drawn.
- alans-of-orleans (442-503). Five pages (Alans, Sangiban, Aetius, and after review Kingdom of Soissons and Goar). 442 start is firm (Alans and Aetius pages); the end is a bridge: the Alans page's last dated item is Clovis's failed attack of 502-503, while the Sangiban page has the Visigoths conquering them 'a few years' after 451 and the Alans page has them beating the Goths at Orléans in 463. After review the Orléanais is a federate tint over the land of western-roman-empire (to 476), kingdom-of-soissons (476-486) and salian-franks (486-503), not the Alans' sole land. The Alans of Valence (440) are not drawn. Beorgor's Alans (Liguria, 464) are not this state.
- kutrigurs (551-561) and utigurs (558-576). Kutrigurs: three pages; Utigurs: four. Peoples known from Procopius and Menander; the Wikipedia pages are mostly undated. Start years are the first dated sightings (551 Chinialon; 558/559 Pohl's quote on the Avars), end years follow the Avar conquest (page: 'by 562'; the avar-khaganate dossier founds the Avars in 561) and Menander's Utigur army under the Türks at Bosporos (576). Regions are readings of 'the Kutrigurs in the western part, the Utigurs towards the East' of the Don-Azov steppe. All Kutrigur events are confidence 1.
- antes (518-602). Three pages (the Antes page twice; the Avar-Byzantine wars page is nearly silent). Start 518 is the first Roman contact, not a founding; Boz (fl. 376-80) is not drawn. The contest with the Avars 562-602 is a reading of 'often involved in conflicts with the Avars' that avoids drawing the Avars as sole holders of land Cliopatria gives them from 561.

### One problem for the orchestrator

The schema describes vassal_start/vassal_end as 'region becomes this state's vassal ... counterpart is the vassal itself'. This batch (gothic-foederati 382-395, heruli 512-567, thracian-goths 473-474, alans-of-orleans 442-503) records the federate's own tie to its suzerain with the suzerain as counterpart, as the burgundian-kingdom dossier does.
