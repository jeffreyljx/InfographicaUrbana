# ea-08-c notes (Qing to the People's Republic, part c)

Written 2026-10-20 by the Phase B research agent of batch ea-08-c. Six dossiers, all validate (taiping-heavenly-kingdom, panthay-rebellion, republic-of-china, empire-of-china, kuomintang, chinese-communists). Every page was opened with WebFetch on 2026-10-20.

## Decisions the reviewer should check first

1. **Q148: the Republic of China and the People's Republic are two states.** The ROC (1912-) continues on Taiwan to 2024 (Cliopatria's 1950-2024 shape is Taiwan); the PRC was proclaimed 1 October 1949. republic-of-china keeps only its own Name; the Q148 on the ROC row is Cliopatria's mistake. The PRC is listed as a successor of the mainland (1949-1950).
2. **Cliopatria's "Kuomintang" 1917-1929 is not the KMT's land.** A gazetteer cross-check shows country-wide outlines (1917 includes Beijing, Shandong and Jiangsu; 1924 omits Beijing and Jiangsu; 1926 omits Yunnan, Guizhou and Qinghai). The KMT held Guangdong 1917-1925 and the Northern Expedition's gains from 1926. kuomintang therefore marks 1917-1924 "wrong" (correction: Guangdong), 1926-1927 "adjust" (the expedition's gains), 1928-1929 "ok" (nominal country, with the four allied army groups as vassal tints). The country-wide Beiyang land of 1916-1927 is carried by republic-of-china as new keyframes (the 1914 outline less the KMT's land). 1928-1929 is drawn by kuomintang only (merge event 1928, gain event 1930 in republic-of-china); republic-of-china has no keyframe for 1928-1929.
3. **Q17427 (chinese-communists / communist-party-of-china):** one continuing party-state; two dossiers kept because the second belongs to another batch. chinese-communists ends on 7 November 1931 (Chinese Soviet Republic proclaimed at Ruijin) with communist-party-of-china as successor. Pages were thin (the Jinggangshan page was a disambiguation page): founding year 1928 and the 1930 extension to Ruijin are confidence 1.
4. **Empire of China (1916):** same state as the Republic in every sense but the title ("the Republic of China was restored"). Kept separate as the brief assigns it; colour agent: the Republic's colour. Cliopatria gives all of 1916 to the Empire although it lasted 1 January to 22 March; republic-of-china has a 1916 keyframe for the restored republic.
5. **Panthay Rebellion:** Cliopatria's shapes (342,000-409,000 km2) are as large as all of Yunnan and in 1859-67 reach Guizhou and Sichuan; all four are adjusted to western Yunnan from the pages' words. The name is the Sultanate of Dali (Pingnan Guo).

### Where I differ from the reviewer, and why
1. Guominjun date. I applied 1927-05 for the land (Northern Expedition page: 'On 28 May, ... the Guominjun took Luoyang'). The same page has Feng meeting the Wuhan leaders at Zhengzhou on 10-11 June and, the day after meeting Chiang at Xuzhou on 19 June, announcing 'that he would align with the Nanjing faction' (20 June). So the allegiance is June 1927 and the land May 1927; the vassal tint in kuomintang starts in May to stay paired with the Republic's loss. Both notes say so.
2. Outer Mongolia. The reviewer cited the Occupation of Mongolia page; it says the occupation 'began in October 1919', lasted 'until 18 March 1921' and, in another line, that Mongolia 'had regained its independence in February 1921', and it does not mention Kyakhta. I used the Mongolian Revolution of 1921 page (17 November 1919; early February 1921; 18 March 1921), which the great-mongol-state dossier cites.
3. 1945-08 gains. Applied as asked, at confidence 2, but the pages show the handover was neither clean nor immediate: Manchukuo's infobox gives 20 August 1945, its body 'formally dissolved ... in September', and the territory 'was transferred to Chinese administration the following year'; Mengjiang 'disestablished 19 August 1945', 'returned to Chinese control after ... 1945' (Kalgan excepted). The Communists' and Nationalists' contest for Rehe and Chahar in 1945-46 is on no page I opened.
4. Dachen Islands. One loss event (1955-02, the evacuation 'in February') rather than two; the PLA took Yijiangshan on 18 January 1955 (First Taiwan Strait Crisis page) or 20 January (Dachen Islands page). The islands are smaller than the gazetteer cells: a 30 km circle at 28.46 N 121.90 E resolves to no cells (60 km would draw mainland coast), so the loss is a caption until the grid can show it. This is the one --resolve warning that is not a missing slug or place name.
5. National Protection War. The three provinces come back on 14 July 1916 (confidence 3), but the page also lists Guangdong, Shandong, Hunan, Shanxi, Jiangxi and Jiangsu as declaring independence 'shortly thereafter' without dates; they are not subtracted in the 1916-1917 keyframes and not booked.
6. Kumul 1912. The page gives only '1912-1930'. I set the month to February because that is when /ledger/1 gives the Republic the Qing's land; the kumul-khanate dossier has no month.
7. Hainan 1939 is confidence 3 (the Battle of Hainan Island page gives the year and names the island), the other four starts confidence 2 as asked.
8. Not done, for lack of a page: Tibet 1912 is still confidence 1 (the Tibet 1912-1951 page names no holder; it even says Chinese forces 'lost ground control beyond the city of Kangding' after 1911); the Nationalist takeover of the Manchurian cities (Soviet occupation page silent); the 1930 vassal events and the 1929 vassal ends. Central Plains War page: the Guangxi clique 'broke off relations with Chiang in March 1929', Feng 'also clashed with Chiang' in May 1929, Zhang Xueliang declared for Chiang on 18 September 1930, the infobox runs March 1929 to November 1930. This suggests the Guangxi and Guominjun tints end before December 1929 and that the Republic's 1930 vassal_start events for them are bridging; a gap-filler should decide with the Chiang-Guangxi War page. The Guangxi pledge and the National Government of July 1925 are not on the New Guangxi clique page ('By the spring of 1924' the clique formed; Li Zongren 'joined the Kuomintang in 1923').
