## duchy-of-styria

Cliopatria's five 'Duchy of Styria' shapes are not Styria. (1) 1260-1271, 16,888 km2, bbox 10.23-13.38E 46.10-47.59N: by the gazetteer 62 of 67 cells lie in Tyrol, 3 in Carinthia, none in Steiermark (13.65-16.10E, 46.72-47.77N). The area equals modern Steiermark's (16,944 km2) but the position is about 3 degrees of longitude west; it may be a Tyrolean outline (a county-of-tyrol dossier is needed in any case). (2) 1272-1421 and 1422-1449: one Friulian cell (Udine province, about 190 km WSW of Graz), inside Cliopatria's Patriarchate of Aquileia outline. (3) 1450-1496 and 1497-1501: 8 cells (about 2,000 km2) in the Gorizia-Tolmin-Kobarid-Udine borderland, with a bbox identical to the Patriarchate of Aquileia's 1422-1449 outline (the Aquileia row ends 1449, this one starts 1450); probably the Aquileian remnant relabelled. All five are verdict wrong; the page names no Friulian or Tyrolean possession.

Where Styria's real land sits in Cliopatria: Kingdom of Bohemia 1260-1278 (62 cells of Steiermark, 66 from 1272), House of Habsburg 1279-1486 (64-66 cells), Kingdom of Hungary 1487-1491 (all 67 cells; House of Habsburg has 0), House of Habsburg 1492-1515, Habsburg Monarchy (0 cells in 1490 and 1500, 66 in 1520, 67 in 1600). The orchestrator must decide how to render this dossier against those rows. This dossier treats the duchy as a state in its own right 1180-1918 (the page's infobox: raised 1180, dissolved with Austria-Hungary in 1918) held by changing dynasties in personal union, recorded as rename events (rule 2); the bohemia, house-of-habsburg and habsburg-monarchy dossiers (other parts of eu-09) should treat Styria as a union partner or vassal tint rather than draw it twice. If the project prefers Cliopatria's partition, keep this dossier only for 1180-1259 and for its ledger/captions and drop its shapes after 1260.

Life span: Cliopatria's row is 1260-1501, the page's duchy is 1180-1918. I used the page. The 1502-1918 stretch rests on one page (no territorial change is dated on it except the 1918-19 loss of the southern third of Lower Styria) and can be cut at 1806 if the orchestrator wants the duchy to end with the Empire; a dissolve event at 1501 would have been invented, so none is written.

Not settled (page silent or budget spent, 5 pages): (a) whether any part of Lower Styria (Maribor, Ptuj, Celje) belonged to the duchy before 1456: the page says only 'northern Slovenia' and 'acquisition and re-acquisition of the comital Celje estates in Lower Styria'; the base outline is Steiermark only before 1456 and a gap-filler should open 'Styria' or 'March of Styria'. (b) The Hungarian seizures of the 1480s: Cliopatria draws Hungary over all of Styria in 1487-1491; the two pages opened say Frederick fled to Graz in 1487 and name only Mautern (1481) and the Archbishopric's fortresses (1479). No event entered. (c) The 1254 transfer is dated by the Duchy page only ('in 1254'); the Ottokar II page has an undated papal mediation under which Ottokar 'would yield large parts of Styria to Bela'. The Ennstal and other border parcels are not placed. (d) 1276 against 1278: the Ottokar II page gives November 1276 for the renunciation; the Duchy page gives 'To Habsburg Austria: 1276/78' and Rudolph I from 1278; the ruler strip follows the page (1278), the ledger the treaty (1276), Cliopatria moves the land at 1279. (e) Ulrich II of Celje died on 8 November 1456 on the Celje page and on 9 November on the Matthias Corvinus page; the Celje page also says the county was annexed in 1458. (f) The 1512 entry of the duchy into the Austrian Circle is a Reichskreis membership, not territory, and has no event. (g) Ruler list: read from the page through a WebFetch extract that ends at Ferdinand II (1637); later dukes are not recorded.
