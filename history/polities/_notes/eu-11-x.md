## Cross-cutting

- Cliopatria's Kingdom of England shapes (936-1707) and the Great Britain/United Kingdom rows contain Irish land. The lordship-of-ireland and kingdom-of-ireland keyframes are drawn from named Irish regions; the compiler should let them override the England shapes on the island. Cliopatria's Earldom of Ulster (1326-1332) and Earldom of Desmond (1333-1458) use kingdom-of-england as predecessor, but the legal predecessor is the Lordship of Ireland.
- Counterpart strings that are names, not slugs (no dossier exists, or yet): "Lordship of Garmoran", "Earldom of Ross", "Gaelic lordships of Ulster", "Gaelic Irish lordships of Laois and Offaly", "Gaelic Irish lordships (the Gaelic resurgence)", "Hugh O'Neill, Earl of Tyrone (the Ulster confederacy)", "James II (Jacobite Ireland)". They are the pages' own wording.
- The kingdom-of-scotland dossier (another part) records a vassal pair with the counterpart string "Lordship of the Isles" for 1294-1493, and a 1472 gain of Orkney and Shetland from kalmar-union; kingdom-of-the-isles is also another part's. The new lordship-of-the-isles dossier starts in 1336 (John of Islay), and earldom-of-orkney covers Orkney and Shetland only. The compiler should map the string to the slug and decide whether the Hebrides are drawn in the Lordship's colour or as a Scottish vassal tint; the two cannot both be filled in 1336-1493. Likewise old-kingdom-of-norway has Orkney, Shetland and "Caithness and Sutherland" as an 875 gain; the earldom's own mainland piece is left undrawn here because the page says it was "subject to the kings of Alba" with no year.
- The eu-11-a notes (brythons) say Cliopatria keeps Galloway, Gododdin's Lothian, Dyfed and Elmet inside the Brythons or neighbouring rows; those pieces now have dossiers (lordship-of-galloway, gododdin, dyfed, kingdom-of-elmet), and the Brythons shapes still contain the same land: the new keyframes should win for their own years.
- Slugs that other groups asked for under a different name: ulaid (eu-11-i wanted kingdom-of-ulaid), kingdom-of-elmet (eu-11-a/b/f used elmet), deheubarth (some used kingdom-of-deheubarth). The files use the slugify(name) spelling of the names above. The Gwynedd and Powys dossiers name kingdom-of-seisyllwg as Hywel Dda's realm for 942-950; the Deheubarth page says Hywel united Seisyllwg and Dyfed as Deheubarth about 942, so that counterpart should read deheubarth.
- Start years of the Irish provincial kingdoms (Leinster, Munster, Meath, Ulaid 400; Connacht 450) are rounded bridges, confidence 1. The pages give "the fourth and fifth centuries" or nothing.
