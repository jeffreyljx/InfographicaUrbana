## The Cliopatria row
"Kingdom of Great Britain" 1709-2024 (38 shapes) is not one state. Shapes 1709-1799 are Great Britain plus Ireland; 1800-1819 adds Malta and Menorca; from 1820 it becomes the UK plus a drifting set of other people's land (Hanover 1820-1852, Ionian Islands 1820-1869, the Aden hinterland 1890-1968, the Trucial Coast and Bahrain 1895-2023, Cyprus 1880-1968, the Cologne zone and the Straits 1919-1926, Palestine and Transjordan 1936-1947, Iceland 1940-1944, the German zone 1945-1946, Oman 1920-2023, Kuwait 1932-1968, Qatar 1936-2023). Every keyframe verdict is Cliopatria's shape minus the lands the pages show were not UK territory; the ledger carries them as vassal or occupation events. Wikidata Q23666 and the Wikipedia title "Great Britain" in Cliopatria are the island's; the state's article is "Kingdom of Great Britain".
