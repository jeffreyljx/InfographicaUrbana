# sa-03-a notes (Medieval kingdoms, part a)

Written 2026-10-19. Dossiers: chalukya-dynasty, maukhari-dynasty, turk-shahis, karkota-dynasty, pandya-dynasty, gauda-kingdom, plus zunbils (a state Cliopatria lacks, written because turk-shahis removes its land). All validate; pages read are listed in each dossier's sources. Page budgets were used in full for chalukya (5), turk-shahis (5), karkota (5); maukhari (2), pandya (2), gauda (2), zunbils (2, shared).

## Cliopatria rows found wrong (read this first)

- turk-shahis 561..874: the Turk Shahi page has the dynasty at 665-822 (Nezak Huns 484-665 before, Hindu Shahis from c. 822). Shapes 561 (561-643) and 644 (644-664) are the Nezak country (drawn in alchon-huns, which folds the Nezaks in and ends 670): verdict wrong. Shape 870 (870-874) is wrong: Kabul is Saffarid from 870, the dynasty sits at Udabhandapura. The 666-866 shapes include Tokharistan (Bactria and Badakhshan, vassals of the Yabghus) and, from 692, the Zunbils' Zabulistan and Arachosia (about 124,000 km2): both are cut. No Yabghu/Tokharistan dossier exists in this batch.
- karkota-dynasty 592..859: shapes 592 and 602 (127,000 km2) lie in Afghan Bactria, Badakhshan and Tajikistan, 33 years before the dynasty (625): wrong. The 626 shape (209,000 km2) is half Afghan; adjusted to Cliopatria's 656 shape. The Cliopatria row 'Kashmir' (626-1343) shares no cell with the Karkota shapes and adds 85,000-93,000 km2 in 626-850: the two rows are two halves of one realm. Whoever writes 'kashmir' (sa-03) must reconcile (merge the halves or take them for 626-855 into one state); this dossier does not draw that half.
- maukhari-dynasty 555..611: the shape is the whole last Gupta shape (2,051 cells) and reaches West Bengal, Bangladesh, Odisha and Chhattisgarh; the page gives 'Thaneshwar in the West to Nalanda district in the east'. Those four are cut (Bengal to gauda-kingdom, Odisha and Chhattisgarh to nobody). The dynasty is taken to end c. 606 (infobox); Cliopatria's 607-611 shape and Harsha's start at 612 leave five years: harsha-s-empire (sa-03) should start at 606 (Harsha ruler of Thanesar in 606 per the Shashanka page) and take Kannauj.
- pandya-dynasty 592..921: the flat 77,500 km2 for 318 years is the Pandya core; the end is 915 (Vellur 'around 915', Madurai c. 910), not 921. pandya-empire (1216-1325, sa-04) shares Wikidata Q844910 and is the same dynasty after a Chola eclipse: treated as a separate state; the colour agent should give it this family and hue.
- gauda-kingdom 602..611: single shape; the infobox has the kingdom 554-636 (Shashanka 593-636). The dossier lives 554-637 and draws Bengal (Cliopatria's Gauda shape plus the Bengal land given to the Maukharis) and, from 612, the Odisha coast (undated, confidence 1).
- chalukya-dynasty 555..756: 644-655 keeps the peak shape although the Pallavas held Vatapi 642-655 and Vengi was independent by 641: adjusted. The north (Madhya Pradesh, Gujarat) is Rashtrakuta by 750, the rest of the empire from 753, the south to 757; Cliopatria keeps the whole empire to 754.

## Per state: what could not be settled

- chalukya-dynasty: no infobox area on the Chalukya page, so no area comparison; extents were checked against 'from the Kaveri ... to the Narmada' and 'Vishakhapatnam to Nellore and Guntur'. The infobox has 543-753, a footnote c. 540 for the Kadamba takeover (kadamba-empire dossier dissolves 540, a three-year gap), Cliopatria 555-756; the dossier uses 543-757 (Vakkaleri inscription). The 602, 623, 732 steps are undated on the pages. Kalinga and Dakshina Kosala suzerainty (Aihole) are vassal events with the years bridged (confidence 1); Kosala stays inside Cliopatria's shape. The 642-655 interregnum is 'unclear' on the pages: only Vengi and the Vatapi occupation are applied. The kalachuris-of-mahishmati dossier ends 625 with Chalukya as successor; this one dates the Maharashtra step 623 (Cliopatria) at confidence 1 against that. Kanchi raids (c. 670, c. 742-744) are contests at confidence 1-2.
- maukhari-dynasty: no reign years for Harivarman, Adityavarman, Isvaravarman, Avantivarman; Sharvavarman's accession is c. 560 in the text and c. 574 in the list: no rulers strip. The later-gupta-dynasty dossier has Magadha taken c. 575 and restored c. 606; this page dates neither and Cliopatria draws Bihar Maukhari from 555 (unverified, page budget). Jharkhand (295 cells) and Nepal (116) left as drawn.
- turk-shahis: the 'Kabul Shahi' page is a disambiguation page. The state is the Kabul Shahi kingdom 665-870 with a rename at 822 (Kallar); a hindu-shahis dossier is needed from 870 at Udabhandapura (Lalliya's 878 recapture, Punjab, to 1026) and should continue this colour (rule 2). Dates: Turk Shahi infobox 665-822, disambiguation page 665-850, Hindu Shahi page c. 822-1026; the alchon-huns dossier hands Kabul over in 670 (five years later than the Nezak and Turk Shahi pages' 665). Tang (c. 658 protectorate, 719/720 and 745 titles) and Abbasid (c. 775-785 al-Mahdi) suzerainty are recorded in the Tang and Abbasid dossiers, not here; no rulers strip (page dates only some reigns). Gandhara's gain in 666 comes with the page's own question mark.
- zunbils: written from one page (Zunbils) plus the Turk Shahi page; infobox start 680 against Rutbil's appearance in Arab sources from 666; no capitals' years; the 720 Tang recognition is not a ledger event; Wikidata unknown (null). The saffarid-dynasty dossier (me-08) should record the 870 gain with zunbils as counterpart.
- karkota-dynasty: the pages give no border move after 625 (the 732, 751, 772 steps are unexplained; Lalitaditya's conquests are legend or raids per Stein, Sen, Huntington). Only found, a capital move and the dissolve are in the ledger. Start 625 is five years before the alchon-huns dossier ends its Kashmir line (630). Chippata-Jayapida: 832-885 in the table, c. 840 in the text. Lalitaditya's 733 letter calling himself a Tang vassal belongs to the Tang dossier.
- pandya-dynasty: the 765 (Kongu), 825 (Venad) and 897 (Kongu) keyframes are reign-dated; the victor at Kumbakonam c. 880 is not named on the page, so no war; the Chola dossier (sa-03) should date the Pandya conquest 910-915 (Cliopatria 921).
- gauda-kingdom: nothing on the pages about the pre-593 Gauda (Jayanaga possible); the dissolve year 637 is Shashanka's 636 plus Manava's eight months and the Kamarupa dossier's 637, not a page date. Shashanka's Magadha and Bodh Gaya episodes are undated and not drawn.
