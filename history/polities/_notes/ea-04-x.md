Conventions used throughout. (1) No page names a frontier for any of these states, so every shape is a named region, a place circle or a coordinate circle, `certainty` is `soft`, and each note says so. (2) The three Tang protectorates (Anxi, Beiting, the northern one, the southern one) follow the existing Andong protectorate dossier (`type: colony`); the Tang dossier already carries the same land as direct Tang gains, losses and vassal events, so a reviewer must decide which of the two the renderer draws (see 'Cross-dossier mirror gaps'). (3) A nominal submission of one rebel to another (Xu Yuanlang to Li Mi, Zheng, Xia and Tang) is not drawn as a vassal pair in the submitter's ledger, because `vassal_start` is the suzerain's event. (4) Where two pages disagree the dossier uses the more specific sentence and the other date is in the event note and below.
