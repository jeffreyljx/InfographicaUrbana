## Conventions followed

- A vassal records no vassal event of its own; the suzerain records vassal_start with the vassal as counterpart. So vientiane, luang-phrabang and chiangmai carry no Burmese or Siamese suzerainty events; konbaung-dynasty (Luang Phrabang 1765-1778, Vientiane 1765-1779) and thonburi-kingdom (Chiang Mai 1775, Nan 1775, Luang Phrabang 1778, Vientiane 1779, Pattani 1769) do.
- Occupations are recorded by the owner with the occupier as counterpart.
