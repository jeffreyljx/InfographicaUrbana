# eu-19-x notes (Caucasus additions batch)

Written by the Phase B research agent for eu-19-x. Thirteen dossiers, all Cliopatria-lacking states (cliopatria_names [] except kingdom-of-abkhazia, which carries the Cliopatria "Georgia" shapes 724-1009). 25 distinct Wikipedia pages were opened; every event, verdict and war cites one of them. Not reached: see the end.

## Cross-dossier issues for the orchestrator and reviewer

- kingdom-of-abkhazia lists cliopatria_names ["Georgia"] and reviews georgia_724..georgia_980 (the Georgia row's Abkhazian-kingdom years). eu-19-b owns the Georgia row and must not write a second kingdom-of-abkhazia; its georgia dossier should treat 724-1009 as reviewed here. The validator warns about the other seven Georgia shapes having no verdict here (expected).
- principality-of-armenia carries Cliopatria's kingdom-of-armenia_638 and _644 shapes as new keyframes (cliopatria_names []), because kingdom-of-armenia (eu-19-a) already marks them "wrong"/status change.
- kingdom-of-georgia (eu-19-d) /ledger 1010 gains the whole Kakheti-Hereti (Kakheti + Zaqatala, Balakan, Qax, Sheki); the Kakheti pages say Bagrat III annexed Kakheti, while Hereti was a separate kingdom until c. 1014. kingdom-of-kakheti-hereti loses Kakheti only in 1010. Same for /ledger 1122: its counterpart is great-seljuk-empire, the land was emirate-of-tbilisi's.
- shirvan (eu-19-c) /ledger 1201 uses counterpart "Maliks of Darband"; it should read emirate-of-derbent (this batch): the Emirate of Derbent page has Derbent annexed by Fariburz I in 1075 and absorbed "after 1225".
- bagratid-armenia vassal events name kingdom-of-syunik, kingdom-of-kars, kingdom-of-tashir-dzoraget; those slugs now exist.
- republic-of-armenia draws the seven districts around Karabakh as Armenian occupation; republic-of-artsakh deliberately does not repeat them (its 1994 shape is 0.35 of the infobox 11,458 km2).
- kingdom-of-abkhazia vassal events use counterparts kingdom-of-the-iberians and kingdom-of-kakheti-hereti. Its ledger 904 (Kartli) has counterpart "nobody" (page silent on who held it).

## Per state

- emirate-of-tbilisi: no page gives an extent; Tbilisi + 30 km. 1032 Georgian siege/truce undated. Nominal 1080-1122 drawn as the same city. Caliphal (Umayyad, Abbasid) and Seljuk (from 1068) suzerainty are for those dossiers.
- kingdom-of-abkhazia: 778 start is "circa 778" (page); infobox gives the first king from 767. The Cliopatria Georgia shapes 888-979 (up to 86,640 km2, east to 47.8 E) are adjusted to western Georgia + Kartli (about 45,300 km2 on the gazetteer); 875, 886, 947, 960 steps have no page. Kakheti vassal 935-960 and Tao-Klarjeti vassal 914-c.973 are bridges (conf 1). Pages disagree on Kartli (904 Constantine III page vs "about 908" Abkhazia page) and on the viceroy appointments (917 vs 923). Needs a dossier: principality-of-abasgia (to 777; the Abasgia page is thin).
- kingdom-of-kakheti-hereti: one state, principality 787-1010 + kingdom 1014-1104, rename 1014. The Kingdom page's king list shows Kvirike III twice. Needs a dossier: kingdom-of-hereti. Gazetteer lacks Hereti and Kukheti.
- kingdom-of-syunik: only one page, machine-translated and garbled (970s vs 987; "Seleucids"; "Baghdad" for Baghk). The 1103 destruction of Kapan and the 1170 end are the page's. Successor eldiguzids is a reading. A gap-filler should read a page on the Orbelians and Syunik.
- kingdom-of-tashir-dzoraget: David I Anhoghin's conquests from Tbilisi and Ganja, Tiflis suzerainty and Seljuk vassalage are undated on the page and not ledgered. Matsnaberd coordinates approximate.
- kingdom-of-kars: one page; extent not on the page (Kars province used). The Byzantine Empire dossier dates the cession 1064, this page 1065.
- republic-of-artsakh: start is the infobox 2 September 1991 (the text has 6 January 1992). The Shahumyan/Getashen claim is not drawn. Hadrut date and the Lachin corridor are not on the pages opened.
- principality-of-armenia: the page is the Arab-province article Arminiya (title "Principality of Armenia" redirects). Shapes after 646 are the 646 outline (conf low). Needs dossiers elsewhere: marzpanate-of-armenia (428-636), Qaysid emirs.
- emirate-of-derbent: extent a 40 km circle; the post-1075 restoration (c. 1130) is a bridge; end c. 1225 (infobox "c. 1173 or c. 1225").
- kingdom-of-artsakh: Kingdom of Artsakh (1000-1261) and Principality of Khachen (1261-1603) as one state (rename 1261). 1182 "subdivision" and the 1261 "acquisition of Dizak and Gardman" are infobox entries without detail (Gardman not drawn). The Melikdoms of Karabakh (Khamsa, 1603-early 19th century) need a dossier.
- circassia: the 82,000 km2 area is drawn with a 12-point polygon (70,050 km2 on the gazetteer). The Russian advance on the coast and the Kuban plain, 1830-1863, is not drawn (page gives no remaining territory by date). Start 1250 is the midpoint of "c. 13th century" (conf 1).
- republic-of-abkhazia: Upper Kodori is a 25 km circle (approximate). The war page and Abkhazia page give 14 August 1992, 27 September 1993; the 14 May 1994 Moscow agreement is on neither page.
- republic-of-south-ossetia: circles stand for the oblast (Tskhinvali 28 km; Akhalgori 20 km); the Georgian pockets before 2008 are not drawn.
