# am-06-c notes (Spanish and Portuguese America, part c)

Written 2026-10-19 by the Phase B research agent for am-06-c. Six owned states, all six dossiers validate. Four further dossiers were written as direct counterparts of the New Netherland and Netherlands Antilles rows (dutch-brazil, dutch-suriname, essequibo, berbice), as brief section 10 allows for counterparts that no row covers. Pages opened are listed in each dossier's sources[].

## Group-level

- Two Cliopatria rows are lumps and are the main finding of this batch. "New Netherland" (1622..1802) bundles New Netherland proper (Hudson, Long Island, Connecticut River, New Jersey, Delaware, 1622-1669) with the Dutch West India Company's other colonies: Essequibo/Berbice (Guyana cells from 1622), Saint Martin (1629), Dutch Brazil (Pernambuco, then 967 cells of north-east Brazil in 1642-44), Curacao and Aruba (1636), Suriname (from 1670) and Guyana to 1796. "Netherlands Antilles" (1815..2024) is Suriname (524 cells) plus 12 Guyana cells plus the islands from 1820 to 2023. The new-netherland and netherlands-antilles dossiers mark those shapes adjust or wrong, with intentionally empty corrections (a minus of the whole shape) where the land is not theirs; the validator's "region resolves to no cells" warnings there are expected (11 in new-netherland).
- "French Louisiana" (1800..1804) is a bundle of fragments of Louisiana, Missouri, French Guiana, Guadeloupe, Haiti, the Dominican Republic and Martinique; both shapes are marked wrong and replaced by the Louisiana territory.
- Colour family: jesuit-reducciones is family americas; the other nine dossiers (French and Dutch) use family france. The colour seed fixes Dutch rows to Netherlands orange, so the colour agent may want to re-family the Dutch states (new-netherland, netherlands-antilles, dutch-brazil, dutch-suriname, essequibo, berbice).
- Title versus administration is expressed with occupation events (Spain administering French Louisiana 1800-1803; British occupations of the Guianas 1781-1783, 1796-1802, 1803-1814).

## Per state

### france-antarctique (1555/11..1567/1)
- Cliopatria runs 1556..1571; the page gives November 1555 and January 1567. The 1564 shape bundles Fort Caroline (Florida), which the pages treat as a separate settlement lasting 1564-1565. A separate French Florida dossier does not exist.
- The Cabo Frio extent and Henriville are undated on the pages; the 1556 gain is confidence 1 (page silent, 1556 is Cliopatria's start). Fort Coligny coordinates are the island's, not on the page. The war name is descriptive.

### jesuit-reducciones (1609..1767)
- Cliopatria starts 1595, fourteen years before the first reduction (1609); the row carries Guayra and Itatin to 1756 although both were deserted by 1632, and drops the Misiones Orientales (Rio Grande do Sul) in 1640-1737 although the Seven Missions were founded between 1682 and 1707 (founding years not on the pages opened). Tapé founding dates (1626-1634) and the end of the Tapé missions (1638 is confidence 1) are not on the pages.
- Page budget (up to 5) was exceeded by one page: Treaty_of_Madrid_(1750) was a thin list page and the real article was opened as well (both are in sources). Battle_of_Mbarori returned not found; Battle_of_Mbororé was used.
- The Guaraní missions' relation to the Spanish crown was not recorded as vassalage because no page dates it. The 1767 expulsion is the page's date; 1768 is Cliopatria's end.

### new-netherland (1614..1674/11)
- Lump row; see group-level. The dossier keeps New Netherland proper. Cliopatria keeps the colony to 1669 and its 1665-69 cells are English, not Dutch; its New Sweden row ends 1658, the page 1655.
- Counterparts now written: dutch-brazil, dutch-suriname, essequibo, berbice. Still without a dossier: Curacao, Aruba, Saint Martin.
- Undated on the pages: Fort Goede Hoop (1626 gain, confidence 1), Fort Nassau on the Delaware (1632, confidence 1), the 1636 Connecticut and Massachusetts cells (confidence 1). Kieft's War, the Esopus wars and the list of Directors-General were not opened (no rulers). New Castle (Fort Casimir) has no gazetteer name: coordinate -75.55, 39.66 used.
- 6 of 9 pages used.

### french-colony-of-guiana (1643..1946)
- Cliopatria has this colony for the single year 1791 only; the dossier extends it over its whole life and reuses the 1791 coastal shape for 1643-1790 and 1792-1929 (unverified: page budget). The Cliopatria shapes of the Kingdom of France and the Third and Fourth Republics may or may not contain Guiana; the gap-filler should compare.
- Undated on the pages: the Franco-Brazilian Counani/Contestado dispute and its arbitration, the Kourou expedition of 1763, the deportation camps. Dutch and English occupations of Cayenne are not on the page. No dossier exists for the Counani state.

### french-louisiana (1800..1804/3)
- Both Cliopatria shapes are wrong (see group-level); the Louisiana territory replaces them. The Third Treaty of San Ildefonso and Aranjuez dates are not on the pages opened and were left out. The Spanish administration 1800-1803 and the lower/upper split are the researcher's way of expressing title versus administration; a reviewer may prefer plain gain 1800 and loss 1803.
- Saint-Domingue (to 1804), Guadeloupe and Martinique need dossiers; none is in polities.csv as a French row.

### netherlands-antilles (1634/8..2010/10)
- Cliopatria's row is mostly Suriname (see group-level); the Netherlands Antilles page says Suriname "did not become part of the Netherlands Antilles". The union was real only in the merged West Indies colony of 1828-1845. The dossier extends the row back to 1634 to carry the islands Cliopatria filed under New Netherland (1636-1802) and omits 1803-1814.
- Successors without rows: curacao, sint-maarten, aruba (used as counterparts, not yet dossiers). Saba and Sint Eustatius resolve to no cell on the 255 km2 grid (Natural Earth names "Saba" and "St. Eustatius"; zero cells), hence the warnings.
- Unsettled: who held Aruba, Bonaire, Sint Eustatius and Saba before the Dutch; Sint Maarten's Spanish and French interludes; the 1781-1784 Sint Eustatius episode; the 1845-1954 reorganisations; the 1983 split of the SSS islands.

## Disagreements between pages

- 1674 Treaty of Westminster: the Surinam page says it "confirmed" Dutch Suriname; the Conquest/Reconquest of New Netherland page says New Netherland was given up "in exchange for Suriname"; the Willoughbyland page says "de facto exchanged". Recorded in dutch-suriname and new-netherland.
- Dutch Brazil: infobox 28 January 1654, text 26 January.
- Essequibo: infobox 22 January 1782 capture, text 24 February 1781 (British privateers) and 27 February 1782 (French occupation).
- Berbice: Fort Nassau capital until 1785 (infobox) or 1790 (text).
- Netherlands Antilles Charter: 15 December (text) or 10 December (infobox) 1954.
- Anglo-Dutch Treaty: the Berbice page gives 1814 (ratified 20 November 1815); the Essequibo page gives 13 August 1814 for the cession and 20 November 1815 for the treaty in its infobox.
