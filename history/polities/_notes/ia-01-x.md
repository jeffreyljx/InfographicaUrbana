## Unsettled by the pages

- Sarmatians are confederations, not a state: one soft combined shape, starting 313 BCE since the fix pass (the second Scythian defeat, after which the page has them 'crossing the Don'); the page's 200 BCE supremacy date is now only a note. The Sauromatians before it are unwritten, the Crimean Scythian kingdom is not drawn, and the Goths' take-over uses Cliopatria's Gothia shapes (207, 260), whose dates the page does not confirm (the Goths page, re-read, gives no year either).
- kangju: the infobox (1st century BCE (?), to the 5th century) disagrees with the dated sentences (128 BCE; about 270 CE); the dated sentences are used.
- alania: the ia-01-d notes proposed 723-969 from the Khazars page; the Alania page dates independence to the late 9th century, so the dossier starts 880.
- kingdom-of-kucha ends 648 (Tang conquest) though the Bai dynasty reigned under Tang and Tibetan overlords to about 789; the Tibetan period is not on the page opened.
- No capital or ruler list is recorded in most dossiers because the page tables were not read in full.
