## county-of-barcelona

Spot checks (all confirmed): /ledger/0 801 (County of Barcelona: 'in 801, the city of Barcelona was conquered by King Louis the Pious of Aquitaine'); /ledger/3 948 (Borrell II: 'In 948, Borrell inherited Urgell from his uncle Sunifred II'); /ledger/11 1148 (Siege of Tortosa (1148): infobox '1 July – 30 December 1148', Almoravid Emirate; 'the Tortosans surrendered on 30 December'); /ledger/13 1149 (Ramon Berenguer IV: Fraga, Lleida and Mequinenza fell after a seven-month siege; Tortosa page 'On 24 October, Lleida capitulated'). Also confirmed in passing: /ledger/15 1162 personal union, /ledger/6 1112 Provence ('incorporating Provence county through his marriage to Countess Dolça in 1112').

Applied by the reviewer:
- /ledger/0/counterpart and /identity/predecessors/0/slug: `west-franks` -> `kingdom-of-the-franks`. West Francia dates from 843; Cliopatria's Kingdom of the Franks row runs 462-849 and its dossier records the 801 gain of Barcelona (kingdom-of-the-franks /ledger/87; emirate-of-cordoba /ledger/10 loses Barcelona to it). The page infobox: 'County of the Frankish Empire (801–10th century)'. The predecessor note's claim that Cliopatria has no Frankish shape for 801-849 was wrong.

Not applied (author and neighbour owners; non-blocking, routed in the cross-check matrix):
- /ledger/6 and /ledger/9 (Provence 1112 gain, 1131 loss of the whole region "Provence"): county-of-provence exists and keeps Provence as its own state (/ledger/1 1112 rename; /ledger/2 1125 loss of the north to Toulouse). The two dossiers draw Provence twice for 1112-1131 and Barcelona ignores the 1125 partition. The dossier itself treats the 1137/1150 union with Aragon as a personal union not drawn; do the same for Provence (drop both events, keep the facts in notes) unless the orchestrator wants the Provence dossier to record a loss.
- Osona: the Barcelona keyframes add the Vic plain (Ausona) from 886 to 1162 (the page: 'the core formed by the counties of Barcelona, Girona and Osona remained undivided'), while county-of-osona draws Osona as a separate state 798-1111 and loses it to Barcelona in 1111 (/ledger/12, confidence 3), which Barcelona does not mirror. One of the two must give way; the County of Barcelona page supports Barcelona's reading, the Osona page dates a 1107-1111 spell under Bernard III of Besalú. Route to the Osona owner; if Osona stays a state, Barcelona needs a 1111 gain from county-of-osona and must drop the Vic circle for the years Osona holds it.
- /ledger/11 and /ledger/13 (Tortosa 1148, Lleida-Fraga-Mequinenza 1149, from almoravid-dynasty): the Almoravid dossier has only a confidence-1 lump loss in 1149 'to Castile, Aragon, Portugal and the Almohads'. Route to af-02.
- /ledger/1 (878 Girona from west-franks): west-franks /ledger/12 gives 'Barcelona, Girona and Besalú' to county-of-urgell in 878. Route to eu-07.
- kingdom-of-navarre /ledger/3 (1137 loss of Zaragoza to county-of-barcelona) has no mirror here; Zaragoza went to Ramon Berenguer as prince of Aragon, so Navarre's counterpart should be kingdom-of-aragon.

Thin: ruler list gaps 820-878, 911-945, 1076-1086 (author flagged). The 1000, 1003, 1040, 1056, 1085 steps are 'unverified' within the page budget (not faults).
