# Notes for group xx-01-c (Cultures 6000 to 3400 BCE, part c: Africa, the Americas and Oceania)

Written 2026-10-18 by the xx-01-c research run. 18 dossiers, all `type: culture`, `cliopatria_names: []`, all new states Cliopatria lacks. Every one validates with 0 errors (`--resolve` too; two warnings only, for successor slugs without a dossier: machalilla-culture, ancient-pueblo-peoples). 34 source entries over 29 distinct Wikipedia pages listed in the dossiers (31 pages opened successfully; the two others are the unrelated city pages Las Vegas and Khartoum), all opened with WebFetch on 2026-10-18 (WebFetch returns a model's summary of the page, so quotation marks in notes are the quotes as that summary returned them). ## Read first

- Brief list covered: Saharan cattle herders (nabta-playa-neolithic, tenerian-culture, green-sahara-cultures), Khartoum Mesolithic and Neolithic, Capsian, Tichitt, plus the A-Group of Lower Nubia; Norte Chico, Valdivia, Chinchorro; the Archaic Southwest (oshara-tradition, cochise-culture); the Eastern Archaic (watson-brake-culture), the Old Copper Complex and the Maritime Archaic; Aboriginal Australia (mainland), Aboriginal Tasmanians and Kuk. 18 dossiers against the 8-14 aimed at; the extra ones are short (2-5 events).
- Method: every ledger event, keyframe and note rests on a page opened in this run. Extent of every culture is a placement from the pages' words (a province, a river valley, discs round named places or the page's coordinates); no page gives a frontier or an area, so every certainty era is soft. Confidence 3 only where the page gives both the year and the land in its own words (A-Group start, Nabta and Tichitt phases, Cochise phases, Watson Brake end); confidence 1 for the Sahara-wide and continent-wide placements and for the three events that stop an Oceanian dossier at -3400.
- Geometry was checked with the gazetteer: for every dossier the cells added by the ledger (found, gain, loss) equal the cells of each keyframe at its start year (symmetric difference 0); no two dossiers of this batch share a cell in any year; none of my keyframes shares a cell with any other dossier in `polities/` that starts before 460 CE (checked against every keyframe or Cliopatria first shape). Trimmed on purpose: nabta-playa-neolithic (cut at the Egyptian border, less the Naqada Nile band) and a-group-culture (less the Naqada Nile band and the Cliopatria Early Dynastic Egypt shape), see below.
- Horizon convention: Cultures that outlive 3400 BCE on purpose because the page gives the end and nothing else draws the land: Norte Chico (to -1800), Valdivia (-1500), Chinchorro (-1500), Cochise (-200), Oshara (to 460 CE), Old Copper (-1580), Khartoum (-1600), Tenerian (-2200), Tichitt (-400), A-Group (-2900), Watson Brake (-2800). Three dossiers STOP AT -3400 although the culture continues (aboriginal-australians, aboriginal-tasmanians, kuk-swamp-agriculture): the pages carry them far past it (Tasmania to British colonisation in 1803; mainland Australia to the colonies; Kuk undated). Their `dissolve` events at -3400 are confidence 1 and say so. The orchestrator should extend these three (end year, dissolve, certainty, successor colonies) or have the colonial group take the land from them, otherwise Australia and the New Guinea highlands go blank from 3399 BCE.

## Per state

- norte-chico-civilization (-3500..-1800): first city "around 3500 BCE", settlement clear "from 3100 BCE" (the page also says 3200), influence to the Chillon and Santa valleys "by around 2200 BCE" (gain), decline "c. 1800 BCE". Core drawn as a 60 km disc round Caral (30 cells, against 1,800 km2 for the three principal valleys). The Caral page gives a later, narrower Caral (c. 2600-2000 BC). Preceramic antecedents (La Yerba II 5570-4670 BCE, an oldest date of 9210 BCE) have no culture or extent on the page: central Peru before 3500 BCE is blank. Chavin (c. 900 BCE) follows with no dossier.
- valdivia-culture (-3800..-1500): three start dates on three pages (infobox 3800, prose 3500, Real Alto page 3600); infobox used. Real Alto, Santa Elena, "was settled between 6000 and 3800 BC", so the Santa Elena coast was occupied before Valdivia by the Las Vegas culture (Valdivia infobox "Preceded by"); that culture has no dossier (the page title "Las Vegas culture" is the Nevada city; "Las Vegas culture (Ecuador)" and "Las Vegas Culture" returned URL_NOT_FOUND). Santa Elena -6000..-3801 is blank. Machalilla (successor) and Cotocollao have no dossiers.
- chinchorro-culture (-6000..-1500): begins c. 7000 BCE, clipped at the floor; four 70 km discs for the strip "from Ilo, southern Peru to Antofagasta in northern Chile". Ends "around 3500 BP"; Wankarani (infobox successor) is a highland culture, so the coast passes to nobody.
- maritime-archaic (-6000..-3500): "approximately 7000 BC ... until approximately 3500 BC"; drawn as whole provinces (Newfoundland, Labrador, Nova Scotia, New Brunswick, PEI, Maine), which overstates a coastal culture (2,165 cells). Paleo-Eskimo successors unnamed on the page (citation needed). No infobox.
- watson-brake-culture (-4000..-2800): occupied c. 4000 BCE, mounds from 3500, abandoned c. 2800; one 80 km disc round Monroe (75 cells). The Eastern Woodlands Archaic as a whole is NOT covered: the Shell Mound Archaic page failed and the Archaic-period page gives only "starting around 4000 BC, people exploited wetland resources" and shell rings 3000-1000 BC. Poverty Point (c. 1500 BCE) has no dossier.
- oshara-tradition (-5440..460) and cochise-culture (-5000..-200): infobox "ca. 5,440 BCE to 460 CE", range New Mexico and Colorado (both states, less a 150 km disc at 32.77 N 108.28 W that the Cochise holds); Cochise "circa 5000 until circa 200 BC", phases Chiricahua (3500-1500) and San Pedro (1500-200). Phase years in BP converted with BP = 1950 minus. The Oshara page's San Jose phase "6,750 to 3,750 years BP" overlaps Bajada (6,750-5,150) and is not used. The border between the two is the dossier's. Picosa (predecessor), Ancient Pueblo Peoples, Mogollon and Hohokam have no dossiers.
- old-copper-complex (-6000..-1580): dates disagree (artifacts 6500-1580 BCE; burial ground and occupation 4000-2000 BC; Archaic-period page 4000-1000 BC). 6500 (clipped) to 1580 used; a gap-filler may prefer 4000-1000. Extent is Wisconsin plus a 150 km disc at Hancock (Keweenaw Peninsula and Isle Royale).
- aboriginal-australians, aboriginal-tasmanians, kuk-swamp-agriculture (-6000..-3400): see the horizon convention. Mainland Australia (Tasmania removed) is a confidence-1 placement: the Indigenous Australians page is "silent on whether people occupied the whole continent, including the arid interior". The Tasmania page contradicts itself on the flooding of Bass Strait (6000 BCE against 14,000-12,000 BCE); 6000 BCE used. Torres Strait Islanders: page gives no formation dates and Indigenous Australians gives the first occupation "about 4,000 years ago", after the horizon: no dossier. Kuk: drainage "about 9,000 years ago" (before the floor), bananas and sugar cane 6,900-6,400 years ago; a 40 km disc round Mount Hagen stands for a 116 ha site (overstated); the phases 1-6 the brief expects are not on the page.
- capsian-culture (-6000..-5400): "ca. 9000 to 5400 cal BC" in "Algeria and Tunisia"; drawn as Tunisia plus Algeria north of 33.5 N and east of 3 E (the Iberomaurusian page: Capsian "In Algeria, Tunisia, and Libya, but not in Morocco"; "In Morocco and western Algeria" the Iberomaurusian is succeeded by the Cardial). Libya is named by that page without sites or dates and is left to green-sahara-cultures. The Neolithic of Capsian Tradition "succeeds" it with no dates or extent on either page.
- nabta-playa-neolithic (-6000..-3600): site dated "circa 7500 BC"; phase renames at 5500 (Late Neolithic) and 4500 (megalith period "4500 BC to 3600 BC"); the page gives no end, so 3600 is a floor and the Prehistoric Egypt page's timeline puts the Saharan aridification and migration to the Nile at c. 3900 BC. The cattle are "morphologically wild" in several studies, so the dossier is a desert-camp culture. The disc is cut at the Egyptian border (the part in Sudan belongs to green-sahara-cultures) and less the Nile band drawn by naqada-culture.
- tenerian-culture (-5200..-2200): Gobero Phase III 5200-2200 BCE; the page also says "6250 BCE to 2550 BCE", "Abandoned: c. 3000 BCE" and Phase IV 2500 BCE-300 BCE; 2200 used. The Kiffian (7700-6200 BCE, "probably remained ... until around 6000 BCE") has no dossier.
- khartoum-mesolithic-and-neolithic (-6000..-1600): Khartoum_Mesolithic and Khartoum_Neolithic do not exist as Wikipedia titles (URL_NOT_FOUND); the Shaheinab page gives "Early Khartoum (8800 to 5000 BP)" and "Khartoum Neolithic (c. 5700-3550 BP, approximately 3750-1600 BC)" and is internally inconsistent; the Nubia page gives the Mesolithic as "the ninth to the sixth millennia cal BC". identity.wikipedia is null.
- a-group-culture (-3500..-2900): start from the Nubia page ("Around 3500 BC ... Early A-Group culture, arose in Lower Nubia"), end "between 3100 and 2900 BC", "apparently destroyed by the First Dynasty rulers of Egypt" (not a transfer of land). Trimmed against the Egyptian dossiers: the gazetteer's Lower Nubia has 188 cells, 54 of which lie in naqada-culture's Upper Egypt Nile band and 10 in the Cliopatria Early Dynastic Egypt shape, so those are removed (134 left) although the A-Group page puts Kubaniyya (near Aswan) and Qustul in the A-Group. The Egyptian group (me-03) should stop the Naqada band and the Early Dynastic shape at the First Cataract (Aswan); the cells can then be returned here.
- tichitt-tradition (-2600..-400): the brief's "Tichitt precursors where dated" are the Pre-Tichitt pastoralists (Phase I, 2600-1900 BCE), after the horizon; the page's four-period chronology gives the renames (1900, 1600, 1000) and the end (400). The page also says inhabited "between 2000 BCE and 300 BCE". Dhar Tichitt is not in the gazetteer (coordinate disc plus the place Oualata).
- green-sahara-cultures (-6000..-3550): the umbrella for "the Saharan cattle herders" outside Egypt, the Capsian country and the Gobero disc (27,973 cells before 5200 BCE, 27,856 after): a confidence-1 placement. The African humid period page says the Sahara was widely settled and dates the end to "6,000-5,000 years ago" ("5,500 years ago in the Sahel, Arabia, and East Africa"): 3550 BCE used. The Acacus page dates domestic animals there "by around 7000 BP" (rename at 5050). If the project would rather not draw the whole desert on this evidence, drop this dossier; nothing else depends on it.
