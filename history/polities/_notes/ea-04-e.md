## later-tang (Later Tang, Cliopatria 926-935, 1 shape)

- The state runs May 923 (infobox "Established in Daming: May 923") to 11 January 937 (infobox "Overthrown by Khitan and Shi Jingtang"). Cliopatria starts three years late and ends two years early.
- Cliopatria's 926 shape is the exact union of its Later Liang Dynasty, Former Jin, Qi Kingdom and Former Shu shapes (it contains the whole Later Shu 936 shape and none of Jingnan, checked by intersection). New keyframes later-tang_923, 924 and 925 are built from it; the 926 shape is verdict adjust (valid 926-933); later-tang_934 removes Sichuan and Hanzhong for 934-936 (about 1.25 M km2) and keeps the Qin/Feng/Cheng/Jie country.

The Qin/Feng/Cheng/Jie prefectures were never lost to Later Shu by the Later Tang: the Meng Chang page has them surrender to Later Shu around the new year 947 from the Later Jin, so they stay Later Tang to 11 January 937 (the first draft's 934 transfer at confidence 1 is deleted).
- Jingnan: the vassal pair is now 14 April 924 (Gao Jixing page: 'In 924' the Later Tang 'created him the Prince of Nanping'; infobox reign 14 April 924) to 927 (Mingzong page: general campaign declared 'in spring 927'), confidence 2 for both: the vassal reading is mine. - Sichuan 933-934: vassal_start/vassal_end (Prince of Shu, spring 933 to 16 March 934) are paired with a loss on 16 March 934; Hanzhong (Shannan West) follows "in the last days of Meng Zhixiang's reign" (he died autumn 934), placed in September as a sort key at confidence 2.
- Shi Jingtang: the Later Tang page does not date the Later Jin founding; the loss in 936 now cites the Shi Jingtang page (sent to Taiyuan 'in late 932'; moved to Tianping 'in summer 936'; Khitan in Hedong 'in fall 936'; Later Jin declared during the Jin'an siege, no day) and is drawn as a 170 km radius round Taiyuan cut to Shanxi (about 104,000 km2), not all of Shanxi. No contest events were written because the later-jin dossier has none for this war and the two dossiers would draw Shanxi twice.
- The Sixteen Prefectures: this dossier does not record a Later Tang loss of them to the Liao; the cession was Shi Jingtang's act (Five Dynasties page, Later Tang page). The later-jin dossier has the loss in 938.
