### Disagreement (not applied)
- **cholula, thin: vassal events for Tlaxcala and the Aztec alliance.** The reviewer asks for them in cholula's ledger as a mirror (counterparts tlaxcala and aztec-triple-alliance). I did not write them. The schema says that in `vassal_start`/`vassal_end` 'counterpart is the vassal itself', and window-brief rule 4 turns a `vassal_start` into 'status vassal with suzerain = this slug'; in cholula's ledger with counterpart tlaxcala it would draw Cholula as the suzerain of Tlaxcala, the reverse of the page. The page also dates the Tlaxcala conquest only as 'Sometime between 1200 and 1517', so an event would need an invented year. The facts, with the Cholula page's words ('Sometime between 1200 and 1517, Cholula was conquered by the neighboring city-state of Tlaxcala'; 'In 1517, Cholula seceded from the Tlaxcala Triple Alliance, opting to join the far more powerful Aztec equivalent'), belong to the ledgers of tlaxcala (no dossier yet) and aztec-triple-alliance (am-03), where cholula is the named vassal. If the project wants the relation also recorded on the vassal's side, the schema needs an event type for 'becomes a vassal of'.
