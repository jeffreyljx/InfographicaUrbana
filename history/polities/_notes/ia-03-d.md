## Decision to review first: Dzungar Khanate and Oirat Confederation

Cliopatria draws one continuous realm under three labels: "Dzungar Khanate" 1609-1618, "Oirat Confederation" 1619-1676 (identical shapes: 365,737 km2, then 839,091 km2 from 1636) and "Dzungar Khanate" again 1677-1756. The Wikipedia pages put the break at 1634 (Dzungar page infobox "Established: 1634", Oirat page infobox "Disestablished: 1634"). Both dossiers follow the pages:
- oirat-confederation runs 1609-1634 (start moved back from Cliopatria's 1619 to 1609 at confidence 1, because the Dzungar row has the same shape from 1609 and the Dzungar page shows Oirat campaigns in 1609-1616); its Oirat shapes of 1636-1676 are marked wrong for its own years.
- dzungar-khanate runs 1634-1757 and carries the Oirat rows of 1634-1676 as new keyframes (dzungar-khanate_1634 for 1634-1657 at 365,737 km2, dzungar-khanate_1658 for 1658-1676 at 839,091 km2: the Chu and Talas victory of 1658 fixed the border from the Ayagoz to the Talas).
- The 1634 handoff year is drawn by both on identical cells.
- Consequence for another dossier: kazakh-khanate names oirat-confederation as counterpart of its 1643 contest_start, 1658 contest_end and 1658 loss; with this split the counterpart should be dzungar-khanate. The alternatives (Cliopatria's partition with Oirat to 1676; or one dossier for the whole realm with both Names) are easy to restore and are described in the dossiers' notes.
- oirat-confederation shares Wikidata Q4332415 with four-oirats, but they are different polities 129 years apart (four-oirats ends 1480); four-oirats is named as predecessor.
