## County of Flanders (862-1384, 10 pages; budget 9 plus the Crown lands page for 1185)
- Start 862 is the Flanders page's date (Baldwin I); Cliopatria begins 990. Keyframes 862-897 (gazetteer region) and 898-989 (plus Artois, Artois page) are new, confidence 2.
- The Cliopatria shapes 1010-1259 leave out Bruges (the infobox capital) and the coast to Zeelandic Flanders; every shape in that span has an adjust that adds a 25 km radius at Bruges.
- The 1272-1304 and 1326-1384 shapes carry a 54-cell Nevers block. Before 1322 Nevers was held by Robert III (count by marriage, 1272-1280) and then by his son Louis, not by the Count of Flanders (Robert III page); the block is removed for 1272-1304 and kept from 1322 as a personal union (Louis I page). Nevers has its own Cliopatria row 1285-1325.
- Lille, Douai and Orchies stay in every Cliopatria shape after 1305, though the Franco-Flemish War page has Lille, Douai and Bethune pass to the French crown by the Treaty of Athis-sur-Orge (23 June 1305) and the Louis II page has Romance Flanders return on 19 June 1369. Adjusted for 1305-1368. Treaty of Pontoise (1312) and the 1320 settlement were on no page.
- Namur: Guy was Marquis of Namur 1264-1305 (Guy page); Cliopatria draws Namur 1272-1304. New keyframe 1264-1271.
- Antwerp and Mechelen (Peace of Ath, 4 June 1357, Louis II page) are in no Cliopatria shape; added by new keyframes 1357-1384. The loss of the two is on no page.
- Artois: dowry of 1180 (Artois infobox), claimed by the crown after 1190, 'removed in 1191' (Flanders page), Bouvines 1214, Melun 1226. Cliopatria drops it in 1188; the 1188 row is adjusted for 1188-1190.
- Vermandois: inherited 1167, ceded 10 March 1186 (Philip I page); the Crown lands page has the king acquire it in 1191, and Amiens and Montdidier in 1185 (Boves). Ten Cliopatria cells around Cambrai-Douai-Valenciennes (1169-1187) are on no page.
- Mons/Hainaut cells in the 1260-1304 shape: no page opened has Flanders hold Hainaut (the Flanders page lists it as a separate county); left as ok, unverified: page budget.
- Rethel enters the shape in 1326 on no page (confidence 1). Zeeland west of the Scheldt, given as a fief by Henry II (Baldwin IV page, undated), is not drawn. Valenciennes: 1013 (Baldwin IV page) against Cliopatria's 990.
- Capital: the infobox says 'Bruges, later Ghent and Lille' without years; Bruges is held throughout. No rulers list: the pages give no continuous one.
- Needs dossiers: county-of-namur, county-of-rethel, county-of-hainaut (Hainaut 1071-1432 and Namur 981-1421 in the Flanders page table).

## County of Blois (832-1401, 10 pages; budget 9 plus the Crown lands page)
- Start 832 is the Blois page's date for the creation of the title; Cliopatria begins 990. New keyframes 832-939 (a 25 km radius at Blois) and 940-989 (the 990 shape; the three extra counties are undated, confidence 1).
- Cliopatria's shapes 1028-1045, 1066-1110 and 1126-1151 are Blois plus Champagne's own shape. The pages give Champagne to Blois 1022-1036 (Odo II), 1063-1088 (Theobald III, 'reunited in 1063') and 1125-1151 (Theobald II). New keyframes correct 1022, 1037, 1044, 1063, 1089; the pages disagree on the first union (1019, 1021, 1022, 1023). Provins and Chateau-Thierry stay with Theobald III from 1037 (radius; later fate on no page).
- Chartres was sold in 1286 (Chartres page); Cliopatria removes it in 1294 with Chateaudun, whose departure is on no page (confidence 1).
- Blois passed to Louis I of Orleans in 1397 (Blois page, Louis I page, Crown lands page; the Chartres page says 1391). The county lasted to 1660 as an appanage; the dossier ends in 1401 because Cliopatria does (confidence 1); the Kingdom of France shape of 1402 holds 14 of the 15 cells. A gap-filler may extend it.
- Sancerre (Theobald III's county) is in no Cliopatria shape. No rulers list.

## County of Champagne (990-1284, 7 pages, 3 new)
- Start 990 is Cliopatria's (confidence 1): the pages name no count of Troyes before Odo II and the infobox says 'Established: 12th century'. Herbert and Stephen I need a page.
- Cliopatria has no Champagne shape in 1028-1039, 1066-1093 and 1126-1151 (the land is in the Blois shape). New keyframes for 1037-1039 and 1089-1093; the ledger records the passings with county-of-blois.
- The nine-cell Sens block (1003-1055) is Cliopatria's; the Crown lands page has only the 1055 annexation of the County of Sens. Whether Champagne held Sens is on no page (confidence 1). county-of-sens has no dossier.
- Henry the Liberal's expansion and the later additions are undated on the page; Cliopatria's shape is flat at about 25,000 km2 from 1094. Navarre (1234) was a personal union and is not drawn. No rulers list: gaps 1037-1102 and 1181-1198.
