Fix: 1789 gain of a small Andaman disc (Port Cornwallis / Chatham Island, about 92.7 E 11.7 N), 1796-05 loss to nobody, source Andaman_Islands; remove the admin-1 from K13-K32.

Fix: land gain in 1799.

6. /ledger/8, /ledger/10, /ledger/11: nawabs-of-bengal ends 1757 (dissolve to british-empire); part of the british-empire overlap the author flagged.
7. /ledger/84: Nagpur 1854 vs kingdom-of-nagpur ending 1853 and the dossier's own Doctrine-of-lapse table (1853).
8. Missing mirror: east-india-company should carry the 1848 loss of Sabzalkot and Bhung to bahawalpur-state (see below) and align with kingdom-of-tripura's 1764 loss (it has a 1765 gain from nawabs-of-bengal for the same land).
