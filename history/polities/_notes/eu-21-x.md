# Notes for group eu-21-x (Europe 1914-1945, additions batch)

Written 2026-11-01 by the eu-21-x research agent. The batch owned no Cliopatria row; every dossier below is a state Cliopatria lacks (cliopatria_names []). One section per state: what could not be settled.

## Scope and decisions of this batch

The batch owned no Cliopatria row. The states were chosen from the group scope (Slovakia, the Protectorate, Serbia, Montenegro, the Axis occupation zones and puppet states, the Italian Social Republic) plus states that other dossiers already name as counterparts (saar-basin-territory, memel-territory) and gaps in Cliopatria for 1941-1944 (Greece). Every keyframe is new (cliopatria_names []). Pages are Wikipedia only, read 2026-11-01 through WebFetch; no page failed to load. Region specs use modern admin units and radii; unknown names are listed in the report.

## Slovak Republic (slovak-republic, 1939-1945)

Client state of Nazi Germany. The Treaty of Protection of 23 March 1939 (the page: 'signed on 23 March 1939'; the Slovak–Hungarian War page cites 18 March for a guarantee treaty) and the German 'Protective Zone' in western Slovakia from August 1939 are the Axis relation, recorded as vassal_start 1939-3 and vassal_end 1945-4 with nazi-germany (added in the second pass, below) and mirrored in the nazi-germany dossier. The Slovak Republic page does not date the Slovak invasion of Poland beyond 'September 1939'. Prime ministers (Tuka, Štefan Tiso) were not read, so only the president is listed in rulers. Region names the gazetteer lacks: Orava, Spiš (regions), Sobrance (place). The 1939 shape is the state of 14 March 1939 (strip still Slovak, Orava and Spiš still Polish); the 4 April 1939 loss and the 21 November 1939 gain move them (corrected in the second pass). The page gives no year for the 2,655,053 population estimate.

## Protectorate of Bohemia and Moravia (protectorate-of-bohemia-and-moravia, 1939-1945)

Cliopatria lacks the Czech lands for 1939-1946. The relation to Germany is recorded as occupation_start/end (the owner's side of the czechoslovak dossier's event) because the infobox calls it a 'nominal Protectorate and partially annexed territory'; the nazi-germany dossier records the same land under czechoslovakia. The infobox has no area; the 'about 38,000 km2' of the resolved shape is a gazetteer figure, not a page figure (page silent). Party planning to divide the land between four Gaue ('Reichsgau Sudetenland, Gau Bayreuth, Reichsgau Niederdonau, Reichsgau Oberdonau') was never carried out; 'Hitler stated as late as 1943 that the issue was still to be decided'; no territorial change inside the Protectorate is recorded. Minister presidents (Beran 16 March - 27 April 1939, Eliáš to 2 October 1941, Krejčí 19 January 1942 - 19 January 1945, Bienert to May 1945) are in the page but not in the ruler strip. Hlučín (Hultschin) and Těšín (Zaolzie): the pages are silent on whether any part was inside the Protectorate.

## Saar Basin Territory (saar-basin-territory, 1920-1935)

Cliopatria lacks the Saar as a state. Other dossiers already use the slug saar-basin-territory as counterpart (weimar-republic, nazi-germany). The pages give no km2 figure for the territory, so no infobox comparison was possible; the page's 'slightly smaller than modern Saarland' is the whole of the evidence on extent. The French coal-mine ownership (the 'coalfields were also to be ceded to France') and the customs union with France are not territorial and are not drawn. The Governing Commission's chairmen were not read, so no rulers are listed. The 1946-1957 Saar Protectorate is a separate state (saar-protectorate, eu-22-x) and is not covered here.

## Memel Territory (memel-territory, 1920-1923)

Scope decision: the Klaipėda Region page covers 1920-1939 in one article, but from the January 1923 annexation (confirmed in 1924) the land is Lithuanian and Cliopatria draws it in the Kingdom of Lithuania from 1924; one dossier for the League-protected stage 1920-1923 avoids drawing the same land twice. The autonomy 1924-1939 and the German annexation of 22/23 March 1939 ('In the early hours of 23 March 1939 ... signed the Treaty of the Cession of the Memel Territory to Germany') belong to the kingdom-of-lithuania and nazi-germany dossiers; the 1945 return (the Soviets incorporated it into the Lithuanian SSR) belongs to lithuanian-soviet-socialist-republic. The pages do not give a day for the Convention of May 1924 beyond 8 May on the Klaipėda Region page. Cliopatria's Lithuania gains the region in 1924, a year after the de facto annexation; this dossier covers 1923. Gazetteer name wanted: region 'Memelland' (Klaipėda Region) for the strip north of the Neman.

## Italian Social Republic (italian-social-republic, 1943-1945)

Cliopatria lacks the RSI; its Kingdom of Italy row has 455,534 km2 in 1943, 267 km2 in 1944 (wrong) and 289,198 km2 in 1945. Pages disagree on the Operational Zones: the RSI page's footnote has them 'still formally part of the RSI', the OZAK page says 'separate'; the shape follows the de facto line and leaves them out. They need dossiers or a nazi-germany occupation record (OZAK: Udine, Gorizia, Trieste, Pola, Fiume, Ljubljana, from 10 September 1943, Friedrich Rainer; OZAV: Belluno, Bolzano, Trento). The RSI page gives no area or population; the Gustav Line is traced only as 'between Monte Cassino and the western seaboard'. Rome was German-held from 10 September 1943 ('Rome fell to the Germans'); the RSI never governed it, so the 60 km circle in the 1943 shape is generous. The nazi-germany dossier records the RSI land as occupation of kingdom-of-italy, not as a vassal_start for italian-social-republic; the eu-21 reviewer may want the second form added there. The Kingdom of the South ('informally known as the Kingdom of the South') belongs to the kingdom-of-italy dossier (eu-13). Abruzzo, southern Lazio and the Sangro front are not named on the pages and are outside the shape.

## General Government (general-government, 1939-1945)

Cliopatria lacks the General Government. Bezirk Białystok is not on the pages and is not in the shape (the nazi-germany dossier gives it to the Byelorussian SSR occupation). Region names the gazetteer lacks: none (circles stand in for the Zichenau district and the Oświęcim area, which are real names the gazetteer lacks as places: Ciechanów, Płock, Oświęcim, Mława); a 'Zichenau' region would be better.

## Reichskommissariat Ukraine (reichskommissariat-ukraine, 1941-1944)

Cliopatria lacks the Reichskommissariat; the nazi-germany dossier draws occupied Ukraine from Cliopatria's 1942 shape as occupation of ukrainian-soviet-socialist-republic, so the overlap should be settled by the rendering step. Oblast boundaries stand in for the Generalbezirke (the page names them but not their limits), and the pages are silent on: Kiev's capture day (6 November 1943 is not on the pages), Rovno's capture (2 February 1944 is not on the pages), Mykolaiv and Kherson, the Korsun pocket and Poltava. Military-governed land (Chernigov, Kharkov, Donets Basin, Crimea) is outside the shape; Reichskommissariat Ostland (the Baltic and Belarus) is a separate state that needs its own dossier; Eastern Galicia is in general-government; the Romanian Transnistria Governorate is a separate counterpart that nobody has yet. The infobox line 'Remainder part of Generalbezirk Weißruthenien: 25 February 1944' is unexplained on the page.

## Reichskommissariat Ostland (reichskommissariat-ostland, 1941-1945)

Cliopatria lacks the Reichskommissariat; the nazi-germany dossier draws occupied Lithuania, Latvia, Estonia and Belarus from Cliopatria's 1942 shapes as occupation of the Soviet republics, so the rendering step must not draw both. The page gives no area or population (page silent). Generalbezirk Weißruthenien (Minsk) is read as the Minsk and Grodno oblasts, an approximation; the page names West Belarus and Nowogródek but not borders; Bezirk Białystok and Vilnius-Memel arrangements are not on the pages. The page lists end of existence as 'Formally dissolved: 21 January 1945' and the rump held out in the Courland Pocket to 10 May 1945; the dossier ends at 10 May 1945 because the land was still held. The Baltic offensive page gives offensive dates, not the capture days of Tallinn or Riga; Riga's 13 October comes from the Ostland infobox. The Lithuanian losses are dated from Operation Bagration only to the month; the west of Lithuania is a confidence-1 bridge. Region names the gazetteer may lack: none.

The Wikipedia page 'Hellenic State' covers only the name (First Hellenic Republic 1828-1832, Second Hellenic Republic, the Axis occupation 1941-1944); the regime itself is described in 'Axis occupation of Greece', which has no infobox, so the area, capital and start are from body text and the Tsolakoglou page. Gaps: the Ionian Islands' status under Italy is not located (only 'the Greek civil authorities were replaced by Italians'); the Italian-German zone line, Thessaly and central Greece are not named; the EAM-ELAS 'liberated zones ('Free Greece')' of 1943-1944 (the page says only 'By mid-1943') have no extent or date (no rebel/contest events written); Logothetopoulos's and Rallis's end dates; the Dodecanese (Italian since 1912, German-held from 1943) are outside the shape. The nazi-germany dossier has occupation_start for kingdom-of-greece (German-held part) from April 1941; the eu-22 kingdom-of-greece dossier founds in 1945 without 1941-1944. The Bulgarian annexation is recorded as a loss here; the principality-of-bulgaria dossier (eu-18) should hold the matching gain.
