Convention used for the new vassal events, and a caution. The reviewer's rule 4 reading is applied: both sides of a vassalage carry the pair. In the vassal's own ledger the counterpart is the suzerain and the region is the vassal's own land (Terengganu to rattanakosin-kingdom, Bulungan to sultanate-of-sulu and dutch-east-indies, Yogyakarta and Surakarta to the Dutch, Batavian and British slugs). The schema's own text for vassal_start/vassal_end says 'counterpart is the vassal itself in both events', which is the suzerain's side; rattanakosin-kingdom /ledger/8 and /41 follow that (counterpart 'Terengganu'). A renderer that reads a vassal_start as 'this state becomes the counterpart's vassal' on the suzerain's side and 'this state becomes a vassal of the counterpart' on the vassal's side will draw both correctly; one that reads only the schema sentence may draw the same tint twice. The orchestrator should state the vassal-side wording in the schema or the brief.
