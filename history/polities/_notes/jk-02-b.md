## Cliopatria rows

- **Goguryeo (-36..681) is one state that ends 668**, not 681. Cliopatria keeps the 360,601 km2 shape to 673 and a 148 km2 sliver (near 125.2-125.5E, 37.64-37.77N) for 674..681 that no page explains (goguryeo_674, verdict wrong, correction an empty region: the shape minus itself). One-year shape goguryeo_633 (-126,496 km2) is wrong too (the Tang row grows 2.5 M km2 that year). Start moved to 37 BCE (page) with a one-year keyframe goguryeo_-37.
- **Dong-okjeo and the Ye are Goguryeo vassals**, not Goguryeo land, until 401 (both are 'early 5th century' on their pages; the Dongye dossier uses 401). Cliopatria folds Okjeo into Goguryeo from 154 and the Ye from 392; every Goguryeo shape from 154 to 395 has an adjust verdict that subtracts them, and dong-okjeo_154 (154..400) is a new keyframe so the vassal tint has a shape. The Ye vassal start (53) is confidence 1 (the page gives only Taejo's reign 53-146).
- **Baekje (347..660) starts in 18 BCE** (page), inside Mahan. Two new keyframes (-18..233 a Seoul core; 234..346 Gyeonggi and Chungcheong, a reading) cover the years Cliopatria lacks; baekje_347 is adjusted (minus Jeolla) for 347..368 because the Geunchogo page has Jeolla annexed in 369, and baekje_369 restores the full outline. The Mahan dossier draws the whole Mahan shape to 346, so the two overlap on the Baekje core until then (the renderer should let Baekje win). New keyframe baekje_551 (551..552) puts the Seoul region back for the two years Baekje held it.
- **Tamna (263..1278) is one state**, not successive ones: the page has one kingdom on Jeju Island to 1404 whose status moved (independent, tributary, vassal, local autonomy). Cliopatria folds the island into Unified Silla's shape 682..935 and has no row 1279..1394; new keyframes tamna_682 and tamna_1279 fill them, and the end moves from 1278 to 1404. Suzerain relations belong in the suzerains' dossiers (see below).
- Silla's Cliopatria shape gains 48,888 km2 in 555 and reaches 40.16N (the east coast); the pages opened only give the Han River valley (Goguryeo page: 'Silla took possession of the entire Han River valley in 553'). A gap-filler should open the Silla pages for 551-568.

## Placeholders and readings (confidence 1 or 2)

- Jolbon is placed at Huanren, Biryu and Haengin as a radius round it, Dongbuyeo at Yanji: the pages name no place. Buyeo's final annexation (491) is dated to Munja's accession and placed by the Songhua River (confidence 1).
- Baekje 234..346 (Gyeonggi and Chungcheong) and the 234 gain are readings of 'full-fledged kingdom' (Goi), the Mahan page's 'only 20 statelets survived until the late 3rd century' and 'overthrew Mokji state'.
- The Baekje rename to Nambuyeo (538) has no year on the page (confidence 1). The fall of Sabi has no month on the Hwangsanbeol page (battle 9 July 660); Baekje's end carries no month.
- Capitals: Wiryeseong, Hansan and Seoul share Seoul's gazetteer coordinate; Tamna has one capital span (Jeju City) because the page gives no years for Mugeunseong and Jejuseong; Dong-okjeo has none (the page names no capital).
- Goguryeo shape 306 (+7,189 km2) and the small 383/387 changes have no event on the pages (Micheon's page was not opened); the 480 shape (+18,364 km2) is explained by the Jangsu page's 468 and 489 gains, which Cliopatria books together.
- The Wei page says the Ye and Okjeo tributaries came under Lelang and Daifang's influence after 245 and that Goguryeo lost much of its economic base there; no page dates the recovery, so the vassal tint is drawn continuously.
