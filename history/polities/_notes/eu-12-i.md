Written 2026-10-25 by the Phase B research agent. Six dossiers, all validated (0 errors). Pages were read through WebFetch's extraction, so the quotes in the notes are the extraction's answers, not verbatim page text; where an extraction said "page silent", the dossier says so.

## kingdom-of-navarre (1134-1620; Cliopatria 1139-1428, 6 shapes; 5 pages)
- One kingdom or two: Wikipedia treats Pamplona and Navarre as one kingdom (infobox 824-1841, Q200262). The eu-12-a dossier kingdom-of-pamplona ends it in 1076 and says this dossier continues from Cliopatria's 1139 row; kept separate, with kingdom-of-pamplona and kingdom-of-aragon as predecessors. The reviewer may merge them (cliopatria_names would carry both).
- Cliopatria errors: 1139 shape includes La Rioja (surrendered to Castile in 1136, García Ramírez page); shape runs two years past the 1199-1200 loss of Álava, Guipúzcoa and Biscay; no row for 1285-1332 (France's 1300 shape swallows Navarre; kingdom-of-france draws it as vassal tint 1284-1328); row stops in 1428 though the kingdom lasted to 1620. New keyframes: _1134, _1136, _1200, _1285, _1463, _1512; _1422 extended to 1462.
- Gaps: three Pyrenean cells (about 43.3 N, 1.2-1.6 W) flicker in Cliopatria (present 1202-1284 and 1363-1374, absent 1333-1362 and from 1375), so the step from the 1284 to the 1333 shape is unexplained; Charles II's treaties (page silent); rulers omitted (page budget); Évreux dynasty not named by the page.
- For the Castile/Aragon/Barcelona groups: García Ramírez became Alfonso VII's vassal in May 1135 (vassal_start counterpart kingdom-of-navarre); Zaragoza was held by García as a fief 1135-1137 (surrendered to Raymond Berengar); Castile's counterpart for 1512-1529 is written as crown-of-castile although Cliopatria's row ends 1515.
- Names for the gazetteer: Artajona, Tauste, Laguardia, Los Arcos, Oyón, Hondarribia, Saint-Jean-Pied-de-Port (places), La Bureba (region); a Lower Navarre region (Ultrapuertos, around Saint-Jean-Pied-de-Port) would help.

## county-of-portugal (1096-1143; Cliopatria 1139-1146, 1 shape; 2 pages)
- Both pages end the county in 1139-1143 (Ourique, Zamora); Cliopatria keeps the label to 1146 and starts its Kingdom of Portugal in 1147. This dossier ends the county in 1143 and starts it in 1096 (Alfonso VI grants Portugal and Coimbra to Henry of Burgundy). The kingdom-of-portugal dossier must carry the same shape for 1144-1146 or those years have no state.
- Pages silent: the boundary before 1139, the Santarém loss of 1111 (the shape already stops short of it), the 1137-1140 war, Valdevez, the day of Zamora. Theresa's 1121 subservience to Leon belongs in kingdom-of-leon's ledger as vassal_start.

## taifa-of-silves (1027-1150; Cliopatria 1040-1055, 1056-1065, 1147-1151, 1152-1168; 5 pages)
- Two lives: first taifa 1027-1063 (Banu Muzayn from 1048), absorbed by Seville in 1063; second ephemeral taifa 1145-1150. Landless 1064-1144.
- The 1152-1168 shape is marked wrong: the page ends the taifa with the Almohad conquest of 1150; Cliopatria's Almohad shape has no cell in common with it, so almohad-caliphate (af-02) should add these 20 Algarve cells for 1151-1168.
- Pages thin: Ibn Qasi (undated), Almohad Caliphate (silent on Silves), Taifa of Seville (no years), Almoravid dynasty (Muridun revolt 1144 only). The page "Second Taifa period" does not exist (url_not_found). Silent: extent beyond Silves and the western Algarve, Sidray ibn Wazir, the third period, Tejada, Santa Maria do Algarve.
