## Convention followed for occupied land
As in the me-10-k dossiers (united-arab-republic, arab-socialist-ba-ath-party): the owner keeps the occupied land in its shape and the ledger carries the occupation (hatch). So Egypt's 1976-1982 shape (Cliopatria drops the Sinai) and Syria's 1973-1981 shapes (Cliopatria drops most of the Golan) are 'adjust' to shapes that include them. Occupations that began before the dossier (Sinai 1967, Golan 1967) are restated as an occupation_start at the dossier's first year so the pairs close; the earlier dossier owns the real start.
