## great-mongol-state (1911-12 .. 1924-11)
- The Cliopatria row "Great Mongol State" 1912-1925 is the Bogd Khanate of Mongolia (official name Ikh Mongol Uls = Great Mongol State), not the Yuan dynasty. Its Wikipedia field "Yuan dynasty" and Wikidata Q7313 are shared with great-yuan and yuan-dynasty and are wrong for this state; the dossier sets wikidata null and wikipedia "Bogd Khanate of Mongolia". A colour/gap agent should not merge it with the Yuan.
- Cliopatria has no shape for 1911 or 1920-1921: new keyframes 1911 (minus Hovd and Dzavhan, still Qing in December 1911), 1920 (Chinese-held) and 1921. The 1924 shape runs 1924-1925 in Cliopatria; the state ends 26 November 1924 and the MPR's own new keyframe carries 1924-1925.
- Chinese rule 17 November 1919 to February 1921 is entered as loss/gain with republic-of-china. Cliopatria's Republic of China and Kuomintang rows do NOT include Mongolia in 1920-1921 (checked by cell intersection), so the ea-08 dossiers need the mirror events or the land stays unassigned.
- Autonomy under Chinese suzerainty (Sino-Russian Declaration of November 1913 on the Bogd page; Kyakhta treaty 25/29 May or 17 June 1915 on three pages) is not a ledger event here (the state is the vassal); the republic-of-china dossier should carry vassal_start/vassal_end with counterpart great-mongol-state, 1913/1915 to November 1919.
- Unsettled: Barga/Hulunbuir (only an undated list), the Inner Mongolia campaign of 2 February 1913 (no ground taken on the page), the Russian-Mongolian agreement of "the end of 1912", Uliastai's departure (December 1911 or January 1912), the date the western provinces left Chinese rule in 1921 (entered at confidence 1, July 1921).

## republic-of-uryankhay (1911-12 .. 1914-04)
- Thin: neither page gives an area, a capital or a boundary; capitals list is empty. Cliopatria's shape (148,695 km2, all in Russia) is kept; the small Mongolian part of Tannu Uriankhai is left to Mongolia. End date 14 April 1914 (text) vs 17 April (infobox).
- Russian troops were sent in in 1912 and a protectorate followed: the russian-empire dossier (eu-17) should carry the 1912 presence and the April 1914 protectorate with counterpart republic-of-uryankhay. The Tuvan People's Republic (1921-1944) needs its own dossier.

## kingdom-of-afghanistan (1926-06 .. 1973-07)
- Seven Cliopatria shapes are the same cells (modern Afghanistan, 644,005 km2 against the infobox 652,864 km2). 1929 is the real change: Habibullah Kalakani's Saqqawist emirate held Kabul (17 January-13 October) and Kandahar (31 May or 3 June-23 September); entered as loss/gain with "Emirate of Afghanistan (1929)", which lasted under a year and has no dossier (listed under counterparts_needing_dossiers).
- The Durand Line page gives the 3 February 1934 Dokalim treaty and the Arundu adjustment; both are smaller than a grid cell and the place is not in the gazetteer, so no gain from british-raj was entered. The Sino-Afghan delimitation (November 1963, "secret treaty", no day, no land exchange) is a certainty note only.
- The Kingdom page lists Hashim Khan, Shah Mahmud, Daoud and Shafiq among the rulers although they were prime ministers; the ruler strip is Amanullah, Inayatullah (three days), Nadir Shah, Zahir Shah.

## mongolian-people-s-republic (1924-11 .. 1992-02)
- Fourteen Cliopatria shapes are one outline with 1-3 cell edge noise, plus one outlier: the 1946 shape adds seven cells (about 1,770 km2) at 109.8-116.2E 42.5-45.8N toward Dolon Nur, kept as ok but flagged "unverified (page silent)"; the 1945-1949 shapes carry one extra cell near Baitag Bogd (90.82E 45.9N), removed in 1950. A gap-filler could drop both.
- Baitag Bogd (June-July 1947) is entered as occupation_start/occupation_end with the Republic of China; the 1962 division of the disputed lands is on the Baitag Bogd page without shares. The treaty of 26 December 1962, demarcation 1963-1964 and final treaty with maps of 30 June 1964 (China-Mongolia border page) make the certainty sharp from 1964.
- The Soviet client relation (1921 treaty, 1934 verbal and 1936 formal mutual aid, 1966 secret annex, troop withdrawal 1987-1992) belongs in union-of-soviet-socialist-republics as vassal_start/vassal_end with counterpart mongolian-people-s-republic; none of the pages dates the first stationing of troops. China treated Outer Mongolia as Chinese until the 1945 treaty, referendum (20 October 1945) and recognition (January 1946): no ledger type fits, so it is only in notes.
- Ends 12 February 1992 (infobox) while Cliopatria's Mongolia row starts in 1992: a new MPR keyframe 1992 covers the weeks.
- Counterparts without dossiers: Manchukuo, Mengjiang (Japanese puppet states). Gazetteer names needed: Baitag Bogd (Beitashan), Dolon Nur (Duolun).

## east-turkestan-republic (1944-11 .. 1949-12)
- The ETR page ends the republic on 27 June 1946 (Resolution 324, Ili District Council in the Xinjiang coalition government) but Cliopatria's shape runs to 1949 and the Ili Rebellion page ends the conflict in 1949; the Republic of China rows exclude the Three Districts in 1945, 1946 and 1948 (checked), so the dossier follows Cliopatria: continuity to the incorporation of the National Army into the PLA (20 December 1949), with rename events 27 June 1946 and 12 August 1947 (coalition collapse). A reviewer may prefer an end in 1946.
- The Cliopatria row "East Turkestan" (1463-1474) shares Q840601 but is a different state (Moghul-era Kashgaria); the id is shared by mistake.
- Unsettled: the dates of the fall of Tacheng and Altay, the Ashan zone (read as Altay, September 1947 offensive of Osman Batur, aftermath unknown), 12 or 15 November 1944, the Uyghur name (not on the pages). The Soviet Union's backing (satellite state) belongs in the USSR dossier.
