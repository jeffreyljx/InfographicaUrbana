## Merges and splits
- northern-song holds Cliopatria's Northern Song (961-1138) and Southern Song (1139-1278): both rows are Wikidata Q7462, the same dynasty, the Song page has one state and one colour. There is no southern-song dossier; the seed slug `southern-song` must map to `northern-song`. Other dossiers that name `southern-song` as a counterpart (mongol-empire, ngo-dynasty, the Dali neighbour list) should be read as this dossier.
- Cliopatria's `song` row (-750..-285) and `xia` row (410..438) carry the same Wikidata ids (Q7462, Q7427) but are different states (the Spring and Autumn Song state; the Xia of Helian Bobo). They are not part of these dossiers.
- western-xia is Cliopatria's Western Xia only (990-1226); the dossier runs 990-1227-09.

## northern-song (960-02 to 1279-03)
- Cliopatria's Northern Song shape of 1126 still holds Hebei and Shanxi after the Jin took them in 1126 and Kaifeng in January 1127; Southern Song's first shape is 1139. For 1130-1138 the 1139 Southern Song shape is used as the front line (confidence 2): the pages give no line.
- New keyframes: 960, 963, 965, 971, 976, 978, 979, 1072, 1081, 1103, 1123, 1130, 1139, 1273, 1276, 1277; adjusted Cliopatria shapes: northern-song_961, _1056, _1126, southern-song_1139, _1272. Shape steps explained by gazetteer diffs: 961 to 970 is Sichuan, Hubei and Hunan; 970 to 980 the southern and eastern conquests; the 1056 step is Quang Nguyen.
- Pages disagree on the end of the Southern Tang: 975 (one page) or 976 (another).
- northern-song carries no vassal events: the payments to the Liao (1005, equals) and the Xia (1044) are treaty events there, and the Song's tribute to the Jin (1142-10 to 1165, 1208-11 to 1233) is in great-jin as vassal events with counterpart northern-song, because a vassal event belongs to the suzerain's dossier. The Sixteen Prefectures have no gazetteer region; they are drawn from Beijing, Tianjin and Hebei/Shanxi admin units.

## kingdom-of-dali (937 to 1256)

## States added (Cliopatria lacks them)
- tsongkha (997-1103; the xiliangfu dossier says its page lists Tsongkha as a successor of Xiliangfu, which the Tsongkha page does not say): disc-shaped regions round Hezhou (identified with Linxia by the researcher) and Qingtang (Xining); the Song gains of 1072 (Hezhou) and 1103 (Xining) in northern-song have matching losses. No vassal event for 1074 because the page says "submitted to Song governance". Kuozhou and Miaochuan are not in the gazetteer. Infobox says 1104, the Song conquest is 1103.
- khitan-confederation (628 to 907-02): the Dahe federation from its first dated act (628); the pages place the Khitans only "south of the Xar Moron" and "east of the Laoha between Chaoyang". The Uyghur overlordship 755-840 ("the Khitans became vassals of the Uighurs") is not in this ledger: a vassal pair belongs to the suzerain's dossier, so uyghur-khaganate (another group) should carry vassal_start 755 and vassal_end 840 with counterpart khitan-confederation. Xianzhi's subjugation of the Kumo Xi and Shiwei (860?-882?) is undated and unplaced and not drawn. Songmo Commandery and the Sui and Northern Qi contacts are undated or earlier than 628.
- jurchen-confederation (1074 to 1115-01): federation of Wugunai (the Jurchen page has no dated evidence earlier). Shape: Manchuria minus Cliopatria's Liao shape of 1040, plus Hamgyong-namdo in 1104-1106 and 1108-1114 to match the goryeo dossier's gain of 1107 and loss of 1108. The Liao vassalage is carried by liao-dynasty. Tribal events before 1074 (748, 925, 961, raids of 973 and 976, Goryeo's wars of 1051-1080) are not in the ledger. The goryeo dossier calls this state `jurchens`; the liao-dynasty dossier uses `great-jin` for the 1114-1125 war.
- great-qi (1130-1137): no Wikipedia article (a red link); extent is the reading shared with great-jin; Daming not in the gazetteer. The Jin-Song wars infobox gives 1133-37, the text late 1130.
- great-chu (1127-04 to 1127-05): the Da Chu and Zhang Bangchang pages give Jiankang as capital; the shape is a Kaifeng disc. Northern Liao and Wuping (963, Zhou Xingfeng) have no dossiers.
- eastern-xia (1215-1233) and eastern-liao (1212-1269): the Mongol vassalage of both is not in their ledgers (it belongs to mongol-empire, another group: eastern-xia 1216-1217 and 1218-1222, eastern-liao 1212-1269, counterpart = the vassal). Eastern Liao ends with the Yuan's abolition of the title in 1269; the Eastern Liao page has Yelu Liuge occupying Dongjing (Liaoyang) in 1215 where the Eastern Xia page founds the Eastern Xia. Later Liao (Yelu Sibu, 1216-1219) is a counterpart name only.
