## Cliopatria rows that carry the wrong land (read first)

- **Grand Duchy of Würzburg and Principality of Regensberg are swapped.** Tested with the gazetteer: the four "Principality of Regensberg" shapes (29 cells, 6.0-6.2 thousand km2) contain the Würzburg city cells; the "Grand Duchy of Würzburg" 1806-1807 shapes (13 cells) lie round Aschaffenburg, Miltenberg and Hanau and its 1811-1814 shapes (42 cells, 9.8 thousand km2) round Frankfurt, Hanau and Fulda (the Grand Duchy of Frankfurt). eu-20-g reached the same result independently. grand-duchy-of-wurzburg marks all four shapes `wrong` and points its corrections at the Regensberg row by Cliopatria name (`{"cliopatria": "Principality of Regensberg", "year": ...}`); principality-of-regensberg does the reverse. Anyone who edits one row must edit the other.
- **Grand Duchy of Berg: none of the eight shapes is Berg.** 1806-1810 (71-90 cells) is a mix of Hesse, Thuringia, Lower Saxony and only 13-15 cells in North Rhine-Westphalia; 1811-1813 and 1820-1827 lie in Thuringia; 1814 (102,879 km2) covers France, Belgium and the Rhineland; 1815-1819 lies in Hesse and Thuringia. All are `wrong`; the dossier carries the corrections as radius regions round the places the pages name (Düsseldorf, Wesel, Siegen, Dillenburg, Hadamar, Essen, Dortmund, Münster, Tecklenburg, Lingen). Berg ends November 1813; the 1814-1827 Cliopatria years are not Berg.
- **Grand Duchy of Hesse 1806** is 2.17 times the infobox area (the 1807 shape is the same land with 52 fewer cells; the extra cells are probably Hesse-Kassel land occupied in 1806). 1815-1819 still has the Duchy of Westphalia; the left-bank Rhine district comes in 1816 (new keyframe grand-duchy-of-hesse_1816). Cliopatria has no Hesse shape for 1866-1867 (new keyframe grand-duchy-of-hesse_1866). The 1870 shape loses seven cells that coincide with the North German Confederation's 1870 shape: adjusted back to the 1868 land (joining the confederation was membership, not a cession).
- **Grand Duchy of Saxe-Weimar-Eisenach**: 1807-1808 shape holds only the Eisenach side (Weimar, the capital, is outside it); the Vienna gains (Neustadt district, Eisenacher Oberland, Blankenhain and Erfurt lands) are dated 1815 on the page but Cliopatria stages them 1820 and 1828, and the 1828-1865 shape (4,140 km2) overshoots the 1895 district total (3,595 km2) and reaches Altenburg and Gera. Corrections give the 1868 shape (3,610 km2).

## Per state

### grand-duchy-of-berg (15 March 1806 to November 1813)
Pages: en Grand Duchy of Berg (thin: no section headings), de Großherzogtum Berg (the working source), fr Grand-duché de Berg, en Treaties of Tilsit (dates the Prussian treaty 9 July 1807, does not name Berg), en Confederation of the Rhine. Not settled: exact month and giver of each 1807 transfer (the pages say "until January 1808"); Rheina-Wolbeck and Salm-Horstmar are 1806 Rhine Act additions on the de page but post-Tilsit on the fr page; the first-french-empire dossier cites the Duchy of Arenberg page for "Berg annexed Recklinghausen" (1810), not opened here; principality-of-nassau-orange-fulda cites a page for "Dortmund went to Berg on 1 March 1808" and for the Nassau territories going to Berg on 12 July 1806, not opened here (if right, Dortmund is 1808 from Nassau-Orange-Fulda, not 1807 from Prussia); Gummersbach and Friesenhagen added "later" (fr, undated); the interim Generalgouvernement Berg (1813-1815) and the Generalgouvernement between Weser and Rhine at Münster need an occupation entry by Russia/Prussia. The end date is the Allied entry into Düsseldorf on 10 November 1813 (de); the en infobox gives no end, the fr infobox runs Napoléon Louis to 3 April 1815.

### grand-duchy-of-hesse (13 August 1806 to January 1871)
Pages: en Grand Duchy of Hesse, Rhenish Hesse, Duchy of Westphalia, Landgraviate of Hesse-Homburg, North German Confederation. Disagreement: Westphalia goes to Prussia in 1815 (Westphalia and Hesse pages: Congress of Vienna; the kingdom-of-prussia dossier says 9 June 1815) but Rhenish Hesse is Hessian "from 1816" (treaty of 30 June 1816), so the ledger has a loss in 1815 and a gain in 1816. The 1866 loss figure on the page (82 km2) does not match the annexed districts (about 1,800 km2 of cells). Not settled: what the 1806 shape adds ("Electoral Hesse lands were taken in 1806", "The Electorate of Cologne was gained"), the 1806 mediatisations of the Standesherren (no names on the page), the August-November 1810 exchange with France and Baden (no place named), whether Hesse gave Worms and Pfeddersheim to Bavaria 1816-1819 (kingdom-of-bavaria records a gain from Hesse). 1867-1870: Oberhessen (3,287 km2) joined the North German Confederation; association, no territory change. The dossier ends in January 1871 as the sibling German states do; the grand duchy lasted to 9 November 1918.

### grand-duchy-of-saxe-weimar-eisenach (1806 to 1871)
Pages: en Saxe-Weimar-Eisenach, Principality of Erfurt, Neustadt an der Orla (thin), Kingdom of Saxony, Fulda Abbey (thin). Not settled: the month of the Vienna transfers; the Fulda share of the Eisenacher Oberland; the Weimar page says it got "most of the Principality of Erfurt" while the Erfurt page says only "some southern and eastern parts" (Blankenhain among them) and keeps Erfurt with Prussia; the Neustadt gain is recorded against Prussia (the Saxony page: annexed by Prussia, then "re-ceded"), whereas the Weimar page names Saxony. 1809 merger and 1815 elevation are `rename` events (colour continues). Start is the Cliopatria year 1806 (the personal union dates from 1741, the formal merger from 20 September 1809).

### grand-duchy-of-wurzburg (26 December 1805 to 3 June 1814)
Pages: en Grand Duchy of Würzburg, de Großherzogtum Würzburg, Schweinfurt, Ferdinand III of Tuscany, Confederation of the Rhine. The start is the electorate of 26 December 1805 (Cliopatria begins in 1806: new keyframe grand-duchy-of-wurzburg_1805); kingdom-of-bavaria has no matching loss of Würzburg in 1805-1806. The pages give no area, no 1806 possession date and no departments; the 1810 Paris treaty exchange with Bavaria is not on them. Dates differ: end 3 June 1814 (en, Ferdinand infobox) against 30 May and 28 June 1814 (de); Confederation of the Rhine accession 30 September 1806 (both Würzburg pages) against 23 September 1806 (CoR table); Ferdinand's return to Tuscany 27 April or 30 May 1814. The client-state relation to France is for first-french-empire's vassal events.
