## sultanate-of-rum (1077-1308)
- Start moved from Cliopatria's 1094 to 1077 (page: seceded 1077; capital Nicaea) and end from 1313 to 1308 (Mesud II murdered; Karamanid conquest). Extent 1077-1093 is a bridge.
- 1094-1098 shape (367,020 km2, Nicaea to Smyrna) is right only until June 1097 (Nicaea to the Byzantines 19 June; John Doukas retook Smyrna, Ephesus, Sardis, Philadelphia, Laodicea, Choma in 1097).
- Danishmend absorption: Sivas 1174, Malatya 1178 (Danishmends page); Cliopatria draws Sivas 1177 and Malatya 1192. Kayseri and the west to Dorylaeum/Afyon (1188 shape) and Kastamonu (1202) are not dated by any page opened: confidence 1 / unverified.
- Erzurum: Tughril from 1192, broke free 1211-1212, Jahan Shah to 1230, annexed after Yassıçemen 1230, Mongol 1242. Cliopatria has it in the shape 1202-1249; adjusted. Needs dossiers: saltukids, a Seljuk emirate of Erzurum (suggested slug seljuk-emirate-of-erzurum), mengujekids.
- Kayqubad I page claims Siverek, Urfa, Harran and Raqqa in 1234 and Ahlat/Lake Van in 1230; probably raids or short holds. The ayyubid-sultanate dossier should say whether they changed hands.
- Abbasid allegiance (Cliopatria relation 1202-1249) is not mentioned by the Rum or Kayqubad I pages: unconfirmed, no event written.
- 1243-1308: Rum is a Mongol/Ilkhanid vassal but stays one state. ia-02 (mongol-empire, ilkhanate) should write vassal_start for sultanate-of-rum at 1243 (Köse Dağ) and 1256 (Aksaray). Cliopatria has no Rum shape 1260-1304; the dossier draws the 1305 core at confidence 1 (new keyframe 1260-1304) and records a confidence-1 loss to mongol-empire in 1260.
- Rum is the suzerain of Trebizond (1214-1243) and Cilicia (1225-1244); the vassal_start/vassal_end pairs are in this dossier, with the end years at confidence 1/2 (Trebizond end undated).
- Antalya: shape has it from 1202, page says 1207 (Kaykhusraw I page: "from its Niceaen garrison"; counterpart nicaean-empire follows the page, the previous holder is not otherwise given).
- Gazetteer lacks Alanya (Kalon Oros), Ahlat, Siverek, Philadelphia (Alaşehir), Choma (Honaz); coordinates used.

- Start 1080 (Ruben's revolt) instead of 1099; end 13 April 1375 (Sis). Rulers omitted: the page's table has no end dates and the Hethum I page differs (abdication 1269 vs 1270).
- Capitals: infobox Tarsus 1080-1198 then Sis; body says Thoros I moved the seat to Sis around 1100. Body followed (Cliopatria's early shapes do not contain Tarsus).
- 1137-1144/5: Byzantine rule ("eight years"); no Armenian land 1137-1140 (shape 1139 adjusted to 1145-1146; new keyframes 1129 coast, 1141 Vahka, 1151 plain). Cliopatria's 1147 shape draws the plain four years before Mamistra and Tel Hamdoun (1151).
- West and coast: the Cilicia page (Kayqubad took regions west of Seleucia), the Rum page (Cilicia took the coast from Rum in the 1240s) and the Karamanids page (Karaman Bey took Lesser Armenian land in the 1240s, Larende and Ermenek from the Seljuks c. 1260) disagree; the 59.7k km2 shape reaching 30.6 E is unverified in the west. Mut and Ermenek removed from 1260 on the Karamanids page.
- Cliopatria's 1260-1304 shape lacks Gaban and Hajin although the Cilicia page keeps them Armenian until 1375; not repaired (no coordinate a page gives).
- Mamluk wars: Mari 1266 (territory conceded, unnamed), Tarsus 1275, truce of 1285 (fortresses ceded, 1,000,000 dirhams a year), Hromgla sacked 1292: no page says which lands were annexed; mamluk-sultanate should carry any vassal_start/tribute. Adana and Tarsus fell in 1359 (Ramadanids page); the pages give no date for Ayas (1337).
- Cilicia's own suzerains (Byzantium from 1158, Rum 1225-1244, the Mongols from 1244, the Mamluks after 1285) belong to their ledgers.
