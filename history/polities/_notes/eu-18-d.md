## Decisions the orchestrator and the colour agent must know

1. Cliopatria "Serbian Empire" (1352..1458) is split. serbian-empire is the Empire proper (1346-1371, Dusan and Uros V); the eight shapes of 1375-1453 are Lazar's Moravian Serbia and the Serbian Despotate (the Moravian Serbia page: the state "became known as the Serbian Despotate in 1402"), reviewed in the new dossier moravian-serbia (1371-1459, one state, a rename event in 1402). The Empire dossier marks those eight shapes verdict wrong (not this state); moravian-serbia gives them ok/adjust. New slug moravian-serbia (the colour agent should seed it; family balkans).
2. Cliopatria "Kingdom of Serbia" (eu-18, kingdom-of-serbia) draws 1346-1351 as the kingdom, while the Empire was proclaimed on 16 April 1346 at Skopje. serbian-empire has new keyframes for 1346, 1347 and 1348-1351 (the Macedonian conquests, then Epirus/Aetolia/Acarnania 1347, then Thessaly 1348). The kingdom-of-serbia dossier should end the kingdom in 1346 or the two will be drawn together in 1346-1351.
3. principality-of-valona and principality-of-valona-and-kanina are ONE state (same Wikipedia article "Principality of Valona", Q7245121, 1346-1417, one list of six rulers). Merged under principality-of-valona with both Names in cliopatria_names. The eu-18 part that was to write principality-of-valona-and-kanina must skip it. Cliopatria's 1333-1374 shapes are 773 km2 against about 5,058 km2 for the three counties the page names (gazetteer measure) and start 13 years too early; all corrected to the 1395 shape.
4. house-of-anjou-sicily is NOT merged with capetian-house-of-anjou (already concluded there). It is the Angevin dynasty's directly held overseas lands: Corfu, the Kingdom of Albania (1272-1368), Malta (to 1283) and the Principality of Achaea when held directly (1278-1289 and 1373-1383). Its 1279-1293 and 1375-1384 shapes carry Achaea, which Cliopatria's principality-of-achaea row leaves empty in exactly those years (1279-1293, 1326-1384); the principality-of-achaea dossier (eu-05) should record Charles I's direct rule 1278-1289 and the Angevin claim after 1373 and agree with this one. The Angevin Kingdom of Albania could have its own dossier.
