## Cross-cutting

- Every janapada's -600 start is the Mahajanapada convention ('from the sixth to fourth centuries BCE', Mahajanapadas page); the infoboxes give earlier, vaguer founding dates (Anga ~1100, Cedi c. 1200, Asmaka and Avanti c. 700, Gandhara c. 2000 BCE) that are not drawn. The Vedic-era states before -600 belong to batch sa-01-x.
- Cliopatria's janapada shapes are ~5,000 km2 city cores; the pages describe regions ten to thirty times larger (Malwa for Avanti, the Peshawar valley + Taxila + Kashmir for Gandhara). All ten janapada shapes carry adjust or wrong verdicts with named-region corrections; the rendering agents should expect much larger states than Cliopatria draws.
- No infobox of the six gives an area, so the area sanity check could only be made by extent.
- Shared page: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahajanapadas is cited in all five janapada dossiers.

## janapada-of-cedi

- Nothing dated between -600 and the infobox's 'c. 300 BCE'; Cliopatria's -451 end kept at confidence 1 with no successor because no page names a conqueror. A gap-filler with the Nanda and Shaishunaga pages should settle whether Cedi survived as a dependency.

## janapada-of-gandhara

- Cliopatria's Gandhara and Kamboja rows look swapped: Gandhara's shape (74.4E 33.4N) is at Rajouri, where the Mahajanapadas page puts Kamboja's capital Rajapura ('modern Rajori'); Kamboja's shape (73.3E 33.5N) is on the Rawalpindi plateau beside Taxila. Both Gandhara shapes are marked wrong and replaced by the Peshawar valley + Taxila + Kashmir valley (~74,000 km2). The Kamboja agent should check.
- End moved from -501 to -518 (Darius to the Jhelum; Behistun lists Gandara), with a new keyframe _-535 for the eastern remnant after Cyrus took 'Gandhara proper' c. 535 BCE. The Achaemenid dossier (another group) should carry gains in -535 and -518.
- Unsettled: Pukkusati as a possible Persian vassal; who held Kashmir after 518 (left to nobody); the undated Gandhari suzerainty over the Madrakas, Kekayas, Usinaras and Shivis; Sindhu-Sauvira (a belligerent of the conquest) has no row.
