## Modelling choices that cross dossiers

- Soviet republics are internal units. Cliopatria draws their land inside Russian Republic (1917-1919), Republics of the Soviet Union (1920-1921) and Union of Soviet Socialist Republics (1922-1991) shapes; the renderer must treat each dossier as a unit inside those shapes, not as a second owner of the same cells.
- Autonomous republics and oblasts are `vassal_start`/`vassal_end` events in the owner's dossier, with the vassal's slug as counterpart (RSFSR: Turkestan ASSR 1918-1924, Kirghiz 1924-1936, Kazakh 1920-1936; Ukrainian SSR: Moldavian ASSR 1924-1940; Uzbek SSR: Tajik ASSR 1924-1929). The land of an ASSR is cut out of the owner's keyframe where it would double-draw (Kazakhstan out of the RSFSR).
- The Transcaucasian federation (1922-1936) is the federal layer: each of the three SSRs has a `loss` of its whole country to it in March 1922 and a `gain` back in December 1936; the TSFSR dossier mirrors with `gain` and `loss`. The republics therefore reappear as union republics without a second founding.
- Occupations: the owner records `occupation_start`/`occupation_end` with the occupier as counterpart (Nazi Germany for Estonia, Latvia, Lithuania, Ukraine, Belarus; Romania for Moldavia and Transnistria; Finland for Karelia).
- The RSFSR dossier was revised after the Karelo-Finnish page was read: the Isthmus and the Ladoga shore went to the Karelo-Finnish SSR in March 1940 and to the Leningrad Oblast in September 1944, so the RSFSR now gains the Isthmus in September 1944, not in March 1940. The Estonian and Latvian strips are two gains with their republic slugs.
- Page budget: Ukrainian SSR (8 pages) and RSFSR (18 pages) exceed 5 pages, because the Civil War and 1939-1945 events cite the pages that the Russian State, South Russia, Far Eastern Republic and Poland-front dossiers rest on. All others are within 1-5.
