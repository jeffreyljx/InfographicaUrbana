- "applied" findings were made by the reviewer in the dossier (orchestrator rule: small, verified fixes), re-validated and read back. "pending" findings are for the author or a gap-filler; none is blocking, so no dossier fails.
- Thin items are confidence-1 bridges, listed so a gap-filler can look for a page. Most of these states have one keyframe and 2-5 pages by design.
