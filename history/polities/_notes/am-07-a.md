- WHAT THE ROW IS. Cliopatria's 'English Colonial Empire' (1609-1779, 27 shapes) is a composite, not one state: 1609-1712 it draws the English Atlantic, Caribbean, West African and Indian possessions (Virginia, Bermuda, Newfoundland, New England, Maryland, Jamaica, Barbados, Antigua, Suriname 1653-1669, Belize, the Bahamas, Carolina, New York and the Jerseys, Pennsylvania, Hudson Bay posts, Tangier, Bombay, Surat, Calcutta, Cape Coast); from 1713 it keeps only Madras and Bombay (to 1761) and Masulipatnam (1763-1777). Type 'colony', family 'britain'; the metropole is the Kingdom of England (kingdom-of-england, then Great Britain from 1707): the colony relation belongs in that dossier. The state is dated from Jamestown (14 May 1607, Thirteen Colonies page), two years before Cliopatria; it ends 1779 only because the row does.

A rendering rule for claims (soft outline) would suit it; the Treaty of Utrecht page has France cede 'territories in Rupert's Land' in 1713, so French and English posts shared the bay.
