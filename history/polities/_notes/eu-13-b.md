## kingdom-of-italy-medieval (855-961)

- Starts in 855 (Treaty of Prum, 19 September 855) because middle-franks and lower-burgundy already name this state as the successor of Middle Francia and the holder of Provence in 863; new keyframe 855-886 uses Cliopatria's 887 shape plus the Duchy of Spoleto of 860. Cliopatria draws Italy in 850-859 as Middle Franks and in 860-886 as West Franks (Pavia, Verona and Trento lie in the West Franks shape): the west-franks and middle-franks dossiers should remove Italy from those years.
- Cliopatria hands the Italian land to other rows in 896-897 (East Franks), 900-910 (Kingdom of Lower Burgundy), 922-925 (Upper Burgundy and Italy), 926-935 (Kingdom of Lower Burgundy, Provence and Italy): new keyframes 900-910 and 922-935 keep the kingdom whole and the rivals appear as contests (900-905, 923-926). 
- Charles the Fat's deposition: 887 (Kingdom page lead) or 888 (body), November 887 (Berengar page). Hugh: elected 925, crowned 926 (Hugh page); Berengar's imperial coronation December 915; Fiorenzuola 29 July 923 on the Berengar page (the Upper Burgundy dossier has 17 July).
- Reign years for Louis II's successors 875-887 (Charles the Bald, Carloman, Charles the Fat), Lambert, Lothair II's end and Berengar II's accession (950) are not on the pages opened: omitted or flagged in the ruler strip.
- Rome and the Papal States, Spoleto and Camerino after 898, Istria and the March of Verona's real extent: the pages are silent; the March of Verona is read as Friuli plus Verona (60 km). The oath of fealty of August 952 and the vassalage to Otto 952-961 are the kingdom-of-germany dossier's; the contest_end of 952 here is confidence 1.
- Berengar I held Italy as Arnulf's vassal from 888 (Berengar page); the vassal relation belongs to the east-franks dossier (the suzerain records it).
