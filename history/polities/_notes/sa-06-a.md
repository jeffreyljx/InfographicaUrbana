## portuguese-ceylon

- All eleven Cliopatria shapes are marked wrong (the 1505-1506 keyframe is new). Measured with the gazetteer against Natural Earth districts, every shape from 1507 to 1659 holds all 70 cells of the Kandy, Matale, Nuwara Eliya, Badulla, Moneragala, Kegalle and Polonnaruwa districts (64 of 70 in 1632-1658) and none of the Colonies row's 1570 coast cells; the shapes are the island minus the coast. Kingdom_of_Kandy puts Kandy 'in the central and eastern portion of the island'. From 1595 the row also holds the whole north (24 years before the 1619 conquest of Jaffna), in 1619 it drops it, and after the Portuguese left in 1658 it grows to 207 cells (87% of the island) in 1659 and holds 153 cells to 1686. The kingdom-of-kandy owner (sa-04) wrote that the overlap 'has not been measured'; it is 70 of 70 upcountry cells. Kandy has no row of its own, so these cells may help seed its shape; that is a decision for that dossier's owner.
- The corrected shape is a minimum (20 cells from 1597, 28 from 1619): Colombo, Mannar Island, the Kotte lowlands, the ports of Galle, Batticaloa and Trincomalee, the Jaffna peninsula. Kingdom_of_Kandy: Randeniwela (2 August 1630) 'resulted in large parts of Portuguese Ceilao being overrun by the Kandyans' and 'Kandyan forces seized Portuguese territories further inland' (1639-1640); no page opened names the lands, so no loss to Kandy is placed. A gap-filler should open 'Sinhalese–Portuguese War', 'Siege of Colombo (1655–1656)', 'Battle of Randeniwela', 'Battle of Danture' and 'Dutch Ceylon'. The page 'Portuguese Ceylon' is a stub (no area, no territorial history, no fort or province named).
