# Notes: batch sa-01-x (Indus to the Mahajanapadas, additions)

Written 2026-10-19 by the Phase B research agent for sa-01-x. Nineteen new dossiers, none with a Cliopatria row (cliopatria_names []), all validating with 0 errors and 0 warnings under `--resolve`: painted-grey-ware-culture, ochre-coloured-pottery-culture, ahar-banas-culture, malwa-culture, jorwe-culture, gandhara-grave-culture, southern-neolithic-ashmound-tradition (seven cultures); sindhu-sauvira, hindush, gandara, kingdom-of-porus (north-west); videha, kuru-kingdom, panchala-kingdom, kashi-kingdom, anga-kingdom (Vedic states before 600 BCE); shakya, koliya (the republics of the Buddha's country); kingdom-of-tambapanni (Sri Lanka). 36 distinct Wikipedia pages are cited; every cited page was opened with WebFetch in this run. Everything below is what could not be settled; each dossier's `notes` field carries the same points.

## Cross-cutting

- Every keyframe is new, verdict adjust, with a region spec and `text`. None of the 19 pages gives an area in its infobox, so the area sanity check was made by extent only (gazetteer cell counts at H3 res 5): Painted Grey Ware about 73,000 km2 early and 205,000 km2 late; Ochre Coloured Pottery about 37,000 and 165,000; Malwa about 178,000; Videha about 60,000; Kasi and Anga about 15,000 each.
- Culture type is used past -3400 (sa-01-a's Indus precedent). The seven culture dossiers should be drawn muted and dotted. Overlap rule for the renderer: where a state dossier covers a culture (kuru-kingdom and panchala-kingdom over the Painted Grey Ware culture), the state wins.
- Overlap is avoided by subtraction, not by order of drawing: OCP minus indus-valley-civilization's Haryana, Indian Punjab and Delhi (to -1300); Ahar-Banas minus Jaipur and Alwar (OCP's from -2000) and gives the Malwa zone to Malwa in -1600; Malwa gives the Maharashtra discs to Jorwe in -1400 and the plateau in -1300; Hindush takes Taxila (100 km) from Gandara.
- Colour continuity (rule 2, one state one colour): the pre-600 Vedic dossiers hand over to the sa-01 janapada dossiers of the same state. kuru-kingdom -> janapada-of-kuru (starts -600); panchala-kingdom -> janapada-of-pancala (-600); kashi-kingdom -> janapada-of-kasi (-600..-600); anga-kingdom -> janapada-of-anga (-600); videha -> janapada-of-vajji (-600; its predecessors already name videha). All five Vedic dossiers end at -601 (the dissolve events are dated -601, confidence 1 where the page gives only a century), so that the sa-01 dossiers begin at -600 with no one-year overlap and no gap. The janapada dossiers of kuru, pancala, kasi and anga do not yet list these slugs as predecessors; the reviewer should add them (not touched, other groups' files).
- Chronology of the post-Indus cultures: the infobox dates leave -1499..-1201 between OCP (to -1500) and PGW (from -1200); the pages' own alternates (Uesugi: OCP c. 1900-1300, PGW 'from 1300') would close it. Infobox dates are drawn and the alternates noted.

## Cultures of the west, centre and south

- ahar-banas-culture: -3000..-1500. The handover to Malwa in -1600 is bridging (confidence 1): Malwa's page says 'mainly c. 1600' with calibrated dates 'as early as c. 2000-1750'. Gilund's seal cache is dated but moves no land. Black and red ware (infobox successor) has no dossier.
- malwa-culture: -1600..-1300. The gazetteer's Malwa region (about 155,000 km2, 74.0-78.5E 22.0-25.15N) is the whole plateau; eight named sites do not prove that much, so a gap-filler may prefer a smaller shape. The Maharashtra discs are my reading of Daimabad and Inamgaon. The Savalda and Kayatha cultures have no dossier.
- jorwe-culture: -1400..-700. The page says most sites were abandoned about 1000 BCE and the rest declined to 700, without saying where; no loss is written and the late shape equals the early one plus Malwa. The Malwa-to-Jorwe handovers rest on the two pages' date ranges, not a dated event. Successors (Iron Age megalithic culture of the Deccan, NBPW) have no dossier.
- gandhara-grave-culture: -1200..-800 from the infobox. The body also dates Swat phases (Ghalegay IV c. 1700-1400, V c. 1400-1000) and an admixture 'between 1900 and 1500', and the Pakistani view runs the graves into the historic period; all soft. The shape lists Charsadda, Taxila, Timargarha and the spread to Dir, Kunar, Chitral and Peshawar, undated; the first decades should be narrowed to the Swat valley if a source dates the spread.
- southern-neolithic-ashmound-tradition: -3200..-1200. The Neolithic ashmounds page has no infobox, area, phases or 'preceded/followed by'; the name is mine and the shape is six discs on named sites (Ballari, Raichur, Kalaburagi, Kurnool, the Hagari valley, Hallur). The Tungabhadra-Krishna bands would give 180,000-220,000 km2. Valasai (Tamil Nadu) is left out. The 'Southern chiefdoms' of the scope need a page with an extent.

## North-west: hindush, sindhu-sauvira, gandara, kingdom-of-porus

- Cliopatria's Achaemenid Empire row reaches about 72.8E and draws western Sindh only in -500..-405 and nothing south of 29.5N from -404; the Hindush page contradicts that. The Macedonian Empire row is Greece-only in -326..-324, and the Ptolemaic Kingdom row for -326..-324 is really Alexander's empire. Neither is touched here.
- hindush: the pages disagree on extent. The Hindush and Sindhu-Sauvira pages put it in Sindh and the southern Punjab (Multan), with Sindimana (Sehwan) as probable capital; the conquest page makes Taxila the capital with the Indus and Jhelum as bounds; the Gandara page calls Taxila 'the former Hindush satrapy'. The shape unites the readings. Start -518 is the conquest date on the Sindhu-Sauvira and conquest pages (the Hindush infobox has c. 513, the Indian-campaign page c. 515). End -325 is the Peithon page's date. Between Gaugamela (331) and the end no page dates the end of Persian control in the Indus lands (local kings Ambhi, Sambos, Musicanus).
- sindhu-sauvira: -1000..-518; the sa-01 convention of -600 is not applied because the page's own start is c. 1000 and the whole life is soft. Nothing dated between 1000 and 518. Mousikanos (capital Roruka, met by Alexander in 326) belongs to the Macedonian-era lower Indus, which hindush covers to -325.
- gandara: -535..-327. The satrapy's start is the conquest page's 'around 535'; the Gandara infobox's 518 is the Behistun date, and its c. 330 end is the end of Persian royal authority, not Alexander's arrival (327, in the text). The infobox lists four local successor kingdoms (Taxiles, Peucelaotis, Abisares, Assacani) for 330-327; none holds land for ten years on the pages. The Kashmir valley is in neither this dossier nor hindush. sa-01-a's janapada-of-gandhara records its -535 loss with counterpart achaemenid-empire; since this batch draws the satrapy as the colony gandara, that loss's counterpart should be gandara (and the tint is the Achaemenid vassal tint). Fetch note: the Gandara_(Achaemenid_province) fetch returned only a partial extract; the canonical Gandara page was opened and is cited for everything except one quotation, which is labelled as partial.
- kingdom-of-porus: -326..-317. Starts at the first dated year (the page says 'before 326'). Porus is a satrap of Alexander after 326, so macedonian-empire (eu-02) should carry vassal_start (326) and vassal_end (317) for this slug. The 321 Indus grant (Triparadisus; the Porus and Peithon pages) names 'the territories along the Indus' and 'Patala'; the extent is my reading of the lower Indus and it is the land hindush held until -325. Disagreements: Porus's death is c. 317 (infobox) against 'between 321 and 315' (text); the Hydaspes is May on the battle page and July on the campaign page. The shape is the union of named rivers and neighbours (Gujrat, Sargodha, Sialkot, Gujranwala, Lahore, Amritsar), not a boundary from the pages.
- Achaemenid dossier: the Iran group's achaemenid-empire dossier is not yet on the mount; when it is, it should carry vassal_start/vassal_end for hindush (-518..-325) and gandara (-535..-327), or the gain events should name the empire.

## Vedic states before 600: videha, kuru-kingdom, panchala-kingdom, kashi-kingdom, anga-kingdom

- videha: the page contradicts itself on the start (infobox c. 1100; Mahagovinda Sutta 'around 800'; monarchy 'around 900 to around 700'; 'the maximum number of monarchical kings was eight' against a list of fifty-odd Janakas). -800 is the Sutta's date; the whole life is soft and the end (-601, the year before the league dossier's -600 start) is confidence 1. The polygon is the one sa-01-c's janapada-of-vajji uses (a fallback form; the Gandaki-Kosi band from the gazetteer's rivers would be better).
- kuru-kingdom: infobox 'c. 1900 BCE - c. 345 BCE' conflicts with the body (c. 1200 formation); the body is drawn. No territorial event is dated between 1200 and 600; the Kuru-Panchala relation (Kesin Dalbhya) moves no land. The Painted Grey Ware culture covers the same country; the state wins.
- panchala-kingdom: infobox c. 1100 BCE - c. 340 BCE with garbled intermediate years; the post-600 life is sa-01-c's. Kuru-Panchala alliance moves no land.
- kashi-kingdom: from the Kasi infobox 'Established c. 1375 BCE' (sa-01-b did not draw it); the body does not state it. The only dated sentence before 600 is the 9th-century King Asvasena, who moves no land. The shape is the Varanasi core, not the river bounds (a gap-filler may widen it). The page title resolves as Kashi_Kingdom.
- anga-kingdom: from the infobox ~1100; nothing dated until the 6th century. The page says the kingdom 'at times' included Magadha and Vanga and reached the sea: no date, not drawn.

## Republics and Sri Lanka: shakya, koliya, kingdom-of-tambapanni

- shakya and koliya: the pages give only 'c. 7th century' and 'c. 5th century', so every event is confidence 1 (-650 and -460 are bridging). Shakya is 'a vassal state of Kosala' on its page; its conquest by Vidudabha of Kosala should be in janapada-of-kosala (the sa-01-b dossier ends in -451 and I did not read it for the event). The Kalama republic (only a map caption: 'Vidudabha's conquest of Kalama, Sakya and Koliya') has no page opened. Koliya: infobox capital Ramagama against body Devadaha; disc on Ramagrama. Kapilavastu: Piprahwa (UP) or Tilaurakot (Nepal), unresolved.
- kingdom-of-tambapanni: -543..-437, chronicle chronology repeated by both pages (543 landing; 437 move to Anuradhapura; infobox succeeded by Anuradhapura Kingdom). The Anuradhapura Kingdom page (sa-02) may say 377 BC for its founding, and Cliopatria's Anuradhapura row begins in -291, so -437..-291 is covered by no Cliopatria row. The kingdom's extent is on neither page; the 50 km Puttalam disc is a placeholder. No Upatissa Nuwara dossier: that page treats Upatissagama as the second capital of Tambapanni (capital_move confidence 1: the page does not date the move). Gazetteer's name for Puttalam is 'Puttalan'.
