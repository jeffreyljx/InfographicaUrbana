## Cliopatria rows that are wrong or need another agent

- **Sultanate of Malacca (1459..1861).** The state ended in 1528 (partition into Johor and Perak). The 11 shapes 1529..1861 are verdict `wrong`: the town of Malacca was Portuguese 1511-1641, Dutch (VOC) 1641-1795 and 1818-1825, under British Residents 1795-1818, British from 1825. The 1552-1639 shape (65,760 km2) reaches East Java and is not Malacca on any page. The 1459 shape (6,939 km2) is Singapore, south Johor and the Riau islands, not Malacca town, and 94 percent of its cells lie inside Cliopatria's Majapahit shape. Founding is c. 1400, so two new keyframes cover 1400-1458. The 1512-1528 shape (230,357 km2) is replaced after review (see the repair pass at the end): the exile court is drawn on the Muar camp (1512), Bintan (1513-1525) and Kampar (1526-1528). Someone should decide whether the Cliopatria row after 1528 stands for Portuguese/Dutch Malacca (portuguese-empire, dutch-republic/VOC dossiers).
- **Sultanate of Sulu (1459..1879).** The 1459 shape is Basilan (4 cells), not Jolo; no shape for 1507-1528 (new keyframe); the 1673 shape adds eastern Sabah, which the North Borneo dispute page dates to 1704 (the Bruneian Civil War of 1660 page gives no year); the 1748 shape adds exactly the 79 cells of Cliopatria's Kingdom of Bulungan (1734-1747) although Bulungan was a Sulu vassal only 1789-1855 (Bulungan page): removed from the direct shapes and carried as vassal events. Palawan, which the Sulu page says Sulu gave up in 1705, is in no shape and is added to the 1673 keyframe. The state's sovereignty ended 22 July 1878; shapes run to 1879; nominal sultanate to 1915.
- **Sultanate of Cirebon (1482..1676).** The 1579, 1619 and 1673 shapes are the Cirebon core plus exactly the 83 cells of Cliopatria's last Sunda Kingdom shape (1578); the pages give that land to Banten and Sumedang Larang, so the shapes are adjusted to the core. The state ended 1677 (three-way partition), Cliopatria stops 1676.
- **Demak Sultanate (1475..1551).** Row ends 17 years before the page's end (1568); Cliopatria's Kingdom of Pajana row (1572..1587) repeats the 1519 shape cell for cell (175 cells); 1552-1568 is a new keyframe. 47 percent of Demak's 1519 shape also lies inside Cliopatria's Majapahit shape (1500): a matter for the Majapahit dossier.
- **Sultanate of Palembang (1459..1821).** Shapes include Bangka-Belitung from 1459, but the Bangka page says Palembang inherited it in the late 17th century (shapes 1459, 1512, 1556 adjusted; ledger gain at 1670, confidence 1). Bangka was ceded to Britain in 1812 while Cliopatria keeps it to 1819 (new keyframe 1812-1819). The Wikipedia article starts the sultanate in 1659; the dossier keeps Cliopatria's 1459 start with a `rename` in 1659.
- **Rajahnate of Cebu (1497..1571).** Place is right; years are not: infobox c. 1400-1565, Legazpi landed 27 April 1565, the second shape runs to 1571.
