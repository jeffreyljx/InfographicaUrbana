# Notes, group am-03 (Toltec and Aztec), written 2026-10-18

Owned: toltec, texcoco, michoacan, tenochtitlan, aztec-triple-alliance. Added (Cliopatria lacks them): azcapotzalco, tlaxcala, tututepec. All eight dossiers validate (0 errors). Pages were read through WebFetch, which summarises; quotes in notes are the tool's returned sentences.

## Cliopatria shapes that are misplaced (the main finding of the group)
- Toltec (875-1187): the 5,605 km2 shape lies east of Tula and does not contain it. Corrected to a 20 km disc at Tula (875-899) and the state of Hidalgo (900-1150).
- Michoacan 1305-1439: the 1305 and 1429 shapes lie in the Balsas lowlands and Cuitzeo side, not round Lake Patzcuaro; from 1440 the shapes contain Tzintzuntzan.
- Tenochtitlan and Texcoco (1326-1428): the Tenochtitlan shape sits over the east of the Basin (Texcoco, Coatlinchan) and does not contain the Mexico City cell; the Texcoco shape sits south of the lake (Xochimilco, Chalco). No Cliopatria Valley of Mexico shape contains the Mexico City cell. Both are corrected to discs at their cities (15 km and 30 km).
- Aztec Triple Alliance 1450-1520: contains all of Tlaxcala (by 1482) and Metztitlan (from 1450), both independent. Every shape from 1450 is corrected by removing them.
- Michoacan 1468-1481: 48 Jalisco cells and one Colima cell, where the Tarascan page puts the occupation in 1480-1510.

- texcoco: the 30 km disc is my reading of "most of the eastern Basin"; Coatlinchan's coordinates are not from a page; the 1418-1428 Tepanec hold is drawn as a contest (a vassal reading is possible).
- tenochtitlan: founding 1325 or 1345; no ruler list on the pages; the pre-1428 domain is undescribed; the Tepanec vassalage is recorded in azcapotzalco (start bridged to 1325, confidence 1).
- michoacan: the state's own article is "Tarascan state" (Cliopatria's Wikipedia field is the modern state "Michoacan"); capital dates Patzcuaro to about 1350 then Tzintzuntzan are a reading; the terms of 1522-1529 are not on the pages. The Tangaxoan II page would not load (Tangaxoan_II, Tangáxoan_II, Tang%C3%A1xoan_II).
- aztec-triple-alliance: twelve pages against a budget of five. Undated on every page: the growth at 1450, 1453, 1459, 1482, 1487, 1492, 1497, 1502 (ledger events at confidence 1 use Cliopatria shape-differences as regions). Conflicts between pages: Oaxaca 1445 / 1450s / 1486; Toluca 1472 / 1474; Tarascan defeat 1476 / 1479; Noche Triste 30 June / 10 July 1520. Cuitlahuac's short reign (1520) is not on the pages read. - azcapotzalco, tlaxcala, tututepec: see their notes. Tlaxcala's end (1521) is a bridge; its territory is the modern state (assumption). Tututepec's 25,000 km2 sphere is undated.
