### Disagreements with the reviewer (with the page)

- Ghurid vassalage confidence: I used 2, not 3. The Ghurid page's coinage table dates it (1206-1215) but names no frontier; Ghor and Bamiyan are my reading.
- Ghazni: the pages do not say whether Yildiz was a governor. Muhammad of Ghor page: 'Yildiz established himself as the king of Ghazni in 1206', after receiving a 'chatr' from Mahmud; Yildiz page: 'de facto ruler of Ghazni, while ... still recognizing Ghurid authority', 'seized the city from the Bamiyan Ghurids', killed Atsiz in 1214, and was defeated at Ghazni by Muhammad II after he conquered Firuzkuh 'one year later'. I took reading (a) and kept the nominal Ghurid tie in the notes only. Kabul is named by no page; the Ghazni region is the whole Afghan part of Cliopatria's 1206 shape outside Ghor and Bamyan (Ghazni, Kabul, Zabul, Paktika, Kandahar, Helmand, Nimruz), which is the same land the old keyframes called 'Ghazni and Kabul'.
- Balkh loss: the Qara Khitai page has no Khwarazmian capture of Balkh ('The Ghurids kept Balkh'); the Yildiz page has Muhammad II 'capturing Balkh and Termez' with no year, and the Qara Khitai page has him return Termez to the Qara Khitai. The 1206 loss is a bridge (confidence 1).
- Eastern Ganga: the reviewer named the Eastern Ganga dynasty page for the 1244-1247 occupation; that page has only 'advanced to Varendra as far as Lakhnawati in 1245' and 'ended the Muslim rule in Radha', no 1244 or 1247 and no 'occupied'. The source is Narasingha Deva I ('seized two provinces of Varendra and Rarh' in 1244; 'Rarh and Gauda remained occupied'; Yuzbak appointed 1247), as in the eastern-ganga-kingdom dossier. I also added the contest pair 1243-1247 because that dossier has it.
- Bhilsa: occupation_start 1234, not 1233: the Iltutmish page has the raid in 1234-35 and the Paramara page 1233-34; 1234 is the year both share.
- Abbasid investiture: added as a vassalage as the reviewer proposed, but it is nominal (no tribute on the page) and the renderer may prefer an association; the end 1258 mirrors abbasid-caliphate and rests on no page (confidence 1).
- 'Balbani' and 'Lakhnauti' in the Bughra counterpart are the reviewer's labels, not words of the Balban page ('Bughra declared independence after Balban's death, which he maintained for 40 years').
- Nellore: the Rudrama Devi page gives no year for the Pandya hold or its loss; it has Naga-deva, 'a vassal of Rudrama', ruling Nellore 1271-1275, so 'by 1271' is a bridge (confidence 1), counterpart kakatiya-dynasty.
