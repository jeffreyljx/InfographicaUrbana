## Cross-cutting

- Wikidata Q494354 (Kara-Khanids, Eastern Kara-Khanids, Western Karakhanid Khanate): three states, not one. The unified Khanate is kara-khanids (840-1045, written in another batch); after the split in "the 1040s" the East (Balasagun, then Kashgar from 1130; ends 1211) and the West (Samarkand; ends 1212) have different capitals and fates. Both new dossiers say so; no merge.
- Cliopatria ends the Eastern row in 1138 and the Western row in 1084, and draws their land as Kara-Khitans (East from 1139, West from 1147) and Great Seljuk Empire (West 1085-1146). The pages keep both khanates alive as vassals (Seljuk 1089-1141, Qara Khitai 1141-1211/1212), so the dossiers run to 1211 and 1212 with new keyframes. The same cells are therefore claimed by two dossiers in 1085-1146 (Western + Great Seljuk) and 1139-1211 (East + Kara-Khitans direct shape is corrected to exclude them). The compile stage must treat the vassal tint, not double territory.
