# Notes for group xx-01-b (Cultures 6000 to 3400 BCE, part b: Asia)

Written 2026-10-18 by the xx-01-b research run. 26 dossiers, all type culture, `cliopatria_names: []`, all new states Cliopatria lacks; every one validates with 0 errors (one warning class only: successor slugs that have no dossier yet; `mehrgarh-culture` also has 4 resolve warnings for the place name Mehrgarh, which the Indus dossier of sa-01 uses too). 66 source entries over 36 distinct Wikipedia pages (all opened with WebFetch on 2026-10-18; WebFetch returns a model's summary of the page, so the quotation marks in notes are the quotes as that summary returned them). Each dossier's own `notes` field carries the per-state detail; this file gathers what the reviewer and the gap-fillers need.

## Read first

- Scope: the brief's list (Peiligang, Cishan, Yangshao, Dawenkou, Hemudu, Majiabang, Songze, Liangzhu, Hongshan, Daxi, Qujialing, Jomon, Chulmun, Mehrgarh and the early Indus, Jeitun, Anau, Kelteminar, Hoabinhian and the South-East Asian Neolithic where dated) is covered, plus ten cultures that fill the gaps between them: Houli, Beixin, Xinglongwa, Zhaobaogou, Dadiwan (which merges the Laoguantai, whose own page would not load), Majiayao, Hakra Ware, Dapenkeng, Anau. That is 26 against the 8-14 the brief aimed at; the extra dossiers are short (2-9 events) and were written from pages already opened for their neighbours.
- Cultures that outlive the 3400 BCE horizon on purpose, because nothing else draws their land and the page gives the end: Yangshao (to 3000), Majiayao (to 2000), Dawenkou (to 2600), Hongshan (to 2900), Qujialing (to 2500), Liangzhu (to 2300; the brief asked for its start, the whole life is drawn), Hakra Ware (to 3000), Dapenkeng (to 3000), Jeulmun (to 1500), Hoabinhian (to 2000), Anau (to 1100, the infobox end), Jomon (to 300 BCE). East Asia is therefore covered up to those years and blank after them until Cliopatria's Shang (1600 BCE), Gojoseon (197 BCE) and Asuka Japan (540 CE); the Longshan, Qijia, Shijiahe, Xiaoheyan, Lower Xiajiadian, Mumun, Yayoi, Epi-Jomon, Tazabagyab, Namazga and Fengbitou cultures that follow have no dossier and no owner in the group list. A gap-filler or a new group should take them; the successor slugs below are already named in the dossiers.
- Extent of every culture is a placement from the pages' words (a province, a river valley, discs around named cities); no page gives a frontier or an area for a culture, so every certainty era is soft and extent confidence is 2 (1 for the Hoabinhian, Hakra Ware and the bridging events). Where a page gives only a site, a disc of 50-150 km stands in. Cliopatria holds nothing before 3400 BCE, so no area could be compared with a Cliopatria shape.
- Cross-dossier agreement was checked with the gazetteer: for every dossier the cells added by the ledger (found, gain, loss) equal the cells of each keyframe at its start year (symmetric difference 0), and no two dossiers of this batch share a cell in more than one year (Yangshao, Cishan, Houli, Beixin and Dawenkou were trimmed against each other, Hemudu against Majiabang, Zhaobaogou against Hongshan, Daxi against Qujialing). The two Indus-side dossiers (mehrgarh-culture, hakra-ware-culture) were checked against indus-valley-civilization (sa-01) by hand: Mehrgarh hands its disc over in 3300 where the Indus `found` event draws it; Hakra Ware shares no cell with the Indus shapes of 3300-3000 and ends in 3000 into nobody's land, so the Indus gain of the Ghaggar-Hakra country in 2800 (counterpart nobody) is consistent.

## Per state

- peiligang-culture (-6000..-5000): page 7000-5000 BC, cut at the floor; 100+ sites in about 100 km2 drawn as two 60 km discs; Jiahu (6600-5700 BC phases, abandoned 5700) drawn as a Luohe 50 km disc until 5700 and removed to nobody; hands over to Yangshao. Nanzhuangtou (predecessor) has no dates on the page.
- cishan-culture (-6000..-5000): page 6500-5000 BC; one Handan disc minus the Shandong cells; hands over to Yangshao. Only the Cishan and Yangshao pages opened.
- yangshao-culture (-5000..-3000): three phases as renames (4000, 3500); Henan, Wei valley (Guanzhong), Shanxi, the Cishan Hebei disc, a Gansu disc until Majiayao in 3300; the Henan cells inside the Beixin/Dawenkou discs are left to those cultures. Baijia (infobox predecessor) has no dossier and no dates. Longshan follows in 3000.
- houli-culture (-6000..-5500) and beixin-culture (-5300..-4100): the pages leave Shandong empty 5499..5301 (Houli 6500-5500, Beixin 5300-4100); not bridged. Houli is drawn as all of Shandong (page: 'Geographical range: Shandong', about a dozen sites), which overstates it.
- dawenkou-culture (-4100..-2600): page and infobox say 4300-2600 but give the early phase as 4100-3500 and the Beixin page says Dawenkou 4100; 4100 used for a gap-free hand-over from the Beixin (disagreement). Phases as renames in 3500 and 3000. Longshan follows (no dossier). One sentence on the Dawenkou page ('2600 BC+-4300 BC') is a page error, not used.
- hemudu-culture (-5500..-3300), majiabang-culture (-5000..-3800), songze-culture (-3800..-3300), liangzhu-culture (-3300..-2300): the Majiabang page's own span is 5000-3350/3300 but its later part is the Songze (3800-3300 on the Songze page); the Majiabang is cut at 3800. Hemudu and Majiabang are separate on the pages ('co-existed ... as two separate and distinct cultures') with Hangzhou Bay as the edge. The Liangzhu takes the Songze country, a Hangzhou core and the Ningbo plain (Hemudu's land; the Liangzhu page lists the Hemudu as a predecessor but says only 'Jiangsu, Shanghai & Zhejiang') and dissolves into nobody's land in 2300 (no successor on the page). The Kuahuqiao (Hangzhou Bay, before the Hemudu) page would not load and has no dossier.
- xinglongwa-culture (-6000..-5400), zhaobaogou-culture (-5400..-4500), hongshan-culture (-4700..-2900): Xinglongwa body 6200-5400 vs infobox 6200-5000 (5400 used). The Zhaobaogou page (5400-4500) overlaps the Hongshan (4700-2900); the Chifeng part passes to the Hongshan in 4700 (confidence 1, pages silent), the Luan valley part runs to 4500 then nobody. The Xinle culture (Shenyang; page has no dates) has no dossier. Hongshan hands to the Xiaoheyan (3000-2000 BCE, no dossier); the Lower Xiajiadian (2200-1600 BCE) leaves 2899..2200 blank.
- daxi-culture (-5000..-3300) and qujialing-culture (-3400..-2500): the Qujialing page says its earliest evidence (3400-3000 BC) 'may belong to the final phase of the Daxi'; the Qujialing starts in the Jianghan plain in 3400 and takes the Yichang disc in 3300. Chongqing is lost to nobody (confidence 1). The Middle Yangtze 6000..5001 BCE is blank: Pengtoushan (7500-6100 BC, northwestern Hunan) ends before the floor and its successors Chengbeixi and Lower Zaoshi have no dates on the page opened. The Shijiahe follows the Qujialing (no dossier).
- dadiwan-culture (-5800..-5400): merges the Laoguantai (whose page failed). The Wei valley is nobody's 5399..5001 before the Yangshao.
- majiayao-culture (-3300..-2000): phases Majiayao/Banshan/Machang as renames (2500, 2300); the western Wei disc comes from the Yangshao; Qijia follows (no dossier; the page gives no dates for it).
- jomon-period (-6000..-300): sub-periods as renames (5000, 3520, 2470, 1250); the table's Final phase ends 500 BC but the page 'refined the final phase of the Jomon period to 300 BC' (300 used). Without the Ryukyus. At the end Hokkaido and Aomori/Iwate/Akita go to the Epi-Jomon (loss), the rest to the Yayoi (dissolve); neither has a dossier. Sannai Maruyama (Aomori, 3900-2200 BCE) is in the note, not a region.
- jeulmun-pottery-period (-6000..-1500): the brief calls it Chulmun; the page is titled Jeulmun and the dossier carries 'Chulmun' as an alias. Lead and body disagree on the start of the Early phase (8000 vs 6000); the body's phases are used (renames 3500, 2000). The whole peninsula is drawn. Mumun follows (no dossier).
- mehrgarh-culture (-6000..-3300): periods as renames (5500, 4000, 3500); the site goes on to 2600 inside the Indus shape. Hands over to indus-valley-civilization in 3300. The Mehrgarh page gives two starts (7000 BCE or 5250 BCE) and an infobox build date of 5200 BCE.
- hakra-ware-culture (-3500..-3000): the page's dates are loose (3500-3100/3000 BCE estimate; Bhirrana material 'may be older, in the 8th millennium BCE'; 'early 6th to late 4th millennium'); the 3500-3000 estimate is used at confidence 2 and 1.
- jeitun-culture (-6000..-4500) and anau-culture (-4500..-1100): the Jeitun page gives 7200-4500 BC, the Namazga-Depe table 6200-5000 BCE; 4500 used because the Anau page begins at 4500 (the Anau page also gives 4000). The Iranian side of the Jeitun discs goes to nobody in 4500. The Anau runs to the infobox end 1100 with one static shape; the Namazga culture (contemporary on the pages) has no dossier.
- kelteminar-culture (-5500..-3500): the Tazabagyab (ca. 1850-1500 BC on its page) is listed as the follower, so the desert deltas are nobody's 3499..1851.
- dapenkeng-culture (-4000..-3000): 4000-3000 BC on two pages; 'around 2500 BC' it differentiated into the Fengbitou, Niuchouzi, Yuanshan and Dahu cultures (no dossiers), so Taiwan is blank 2999..2501. The Fujian coast is a Fuzhou disc.
- hoabinhian (-6000..-2000): an industry, drawn as five mainland countries and the Malay Peninsula at confidence 1; the Bacsonian (8000-4000 BCE) and Da But (5000-1000 BCE, Vinh Loc) have no dossiers; the page does not say when the Hoabinhian ended (2000 BCE is its latest date).
