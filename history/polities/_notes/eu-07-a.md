(4) The roman-empire dossier's 260 loss goes to the Alemanni (the Agri Decumates); the Ripuarian 260 strip (4,000 km2, 15 of 16 cells from the Roman 259 shape) has no matching loss there.

Disagreements between pages. The Franks page has the Franks controlling the area round Trier in 407-411 and sacking it three times within a few decades; the Trier page dates the Frankish seizure to 459 and Roman control of the limes to 460. The Franks page gives 496 for Tolbiac without comment; the Tolbiac page gives 496 traditionally and 506 in a revised chronology (the contest events are dated 496 and flagged).
