### Where I differ from the reviewer, and why
- Yeke shape: the reviewer proposed Katanga minus the Luba region (719 cells). That still held 146 cells of the Lunda 309 km disc and the north-east and north-west strips that no page gives to Msiri, so I used the Bunkeya page's own boundary sentences ('from the Luapula River south to the Congo-Zambezi watershed', 'from Lake Mweru in the east to the Lualaba River in the west'). The polygon edges (25 E for the Lualaba, 8.4 S for Mweru) are approximate. The block is 0.17 of the page's 'about half a million square kilometres' (no year): the shape stands and the figure is read as the tribute and trade sphere. The Bunkeya sentences would also put the Luba region's southern strip (10 S to Lake Mweru) in Msiri's state; luba-empire draws it as Luba's and is outside this pass.
- Yeke 1870 counterparts: the reviewer wanted luba-empire (and kingdom-of-lunda) as counterparts with mirrored losses. The redrawn block excludes both drawn shapes, so no drawn Luba or Lunda land is taken and nothing is owed; 'nobody' stays at confidence 1.
- Ndongo and western Malanje: the reviewer allowed it if a page supports it; the Pungo Andongo page (Cacuso municipality, Malanje; capital in Ndongo's last years) does, so the Lukala side stays in Ndongo.
- Matamba's end: the reviewer left two options and no year. No page gives the integration year (Matamba page: 'late nineteenth century'; Ana II, History of Angola and Britannica: nothing). The Ambundu page says 'The Portuguese defeated Matamba in 1836, and advanced to Kasanje by the middle of the century', the only dated conquest, so Matamba ends 1836 at confidence 1 in both dossiers. It disagrees with the lead (1744; the 1744 treaty 'had little effect on the real sovereignty'), so 1744 is no longer an end. A gap-filler should date the integration.
- Cabinda: the reviewer allowed a gain with a stated reason; the Portuguese Angola page says 'established a Portuguese protectorate by the 1885 Treaty of Simulambuco' and 'sometime during the 1920s ... incorporated into the larger colony', so it is a vassal_start now.
