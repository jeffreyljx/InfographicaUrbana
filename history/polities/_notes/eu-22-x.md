## Choices that other agents should know about
- Living states (transnistria, vatican-city, san-marino) end in 2026 (the project's last year, per the schema's "current year"); the other eu-22 parts end living states in 2024 (Cliopatria's last year). The orchestrator may normalise.
- allied-occupied-germany is typed `state` (the four zones under the Allied Control Council) and carries the occupation events with roles reversed (occupation_start/end, counterpart = occupier) so that the occupiers' dossiers (eu-22-a's french-fourth-republic, the US, UK and USSR dossiers) can mirror them. It ends in 1949 with the zones; the Western occupation of the Federal Republic to 5 May 1955 belongs to federal-republic-of-germany (Cliopatria row starts 1950). It takes Berlin out in 1945 (split to west-berlin) and the Saar in 1946 (loss to saar-protectorate), and loses the lands east of the Oder-Neisse line to Poland and the Soviet Union on 2 August 1945 (counterpart republic-of-poland, whose Cliopatria row starts only in 1953; Poland 1945-52 has no shape of record).
- west-berlin starts in 1945 (eu-22-a's occupation_start for the French sector uses counterpart west-berlin from 1945); the page's formal separation of the two city governments (September 1948; the infobox says November 1948) is a `rename` event. Natural Earth's Berlin is the whole city, so East Berlin cannot be separated at the grid's resolution.
- republika-srpska, croatian-republic-of-herzeg-bosnia, republic-of-serbian-krajina, autonomous-province-of-western-bosnia, donetsk-people-s-republic and luhansk-people-s-republic are `type: rebel`. republika-srpska ends 14 December 1995 (Dayton makes it an entity of Bosnia and Herzegovina, matching eu-22-e's gain from republika-srpska in 1995-12); the entity from 1996 to 2026 has no dossier type (an `entity` type or two sub-states of the Bosnian dossier would be needed if the site wants the Federation and the Republic drawn). croatian-republic-of-herzeg-bosnia ends in March 1994 (Washington Agreement, as eu-22-e's gain does); the page puts the formal abolition at August 1996 (agreed 14 August 1996).
- Shapes of the de facto states are readings (circles round towns the pages name) wherever the pages give no boundary: Krajina, Zone B of Trieste, Western Bosnia, the DPR and LPR lines, Baden-Wuerttemberg's split between the American and French zones. The notes of each keyframe say so.
