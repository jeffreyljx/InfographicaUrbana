# Notes, group af-03-x (Sahel empires, additions batch)

Written 2026-10-19 by the af-03-x research agent. 41 dossiers for states Cliopatria lacks (every keyframe is new, `cliopatria_names: []`); all validate with 0 errors. Each section repeats the dossier's own `notes` field; the preamble lists what applies to the whole batch.

Batch-wide points: (1) every dossier is a new state (no Cliopatria shape), so every keyframe is a correction-form region built from admin-1 units, radii and gazetteer regions; none rests on an outline from a page, and each keyframe note says so. (2) Pages are the English Wikipedia articles, each opened with WebFetch on 2026-10-19; where a page was thin the dossier says so and the event is confidence 1. (3) French conquest of the Sudan 1880-1900 as gains: the Tukulor, Wassoulou, Futa Jallon and Mossi dossiers carry the French takeovers as losses with counterpart french-africa; the matching gains belong to the french-africa (af-08) dossier, which should also carry the nominal protectorates (Samori 1887-1891, Tukulor 1887-1890, Futa Jallon 1896-1912). (4) States whose pages are modern-geography or colonial-history pages (Yatenga, Tenkodogo, Adrar, Tagant) or whose URLs were not found are listed under gaps. (5) The discs used for the Yao sultanate, Damagaram, the pashalik cities and similar are stand-ins from areas or radii the pages give; capital points marked 'proxy' reuse gazetteer coordinates. (6) Counterpart slugs used but without a dossier yet are listed in the report under counterparts_needing_dossiers.

## tukulor-empire (Tukulor Empire, 1852-1893)

Tukulor Empire, additions batch af-03-x (no Cliopatria row). Pages: Toucouleur Empire, Ahmadu Tall, Umar Tall (the three that gave dates), Louis Archinard and French Sudan (both thin). Page budget 5 used.

Conflicts: (1) Ségou fell 10 March 1861 (Toucouleur Empire page; the Bamana and Massina dossiers use the same) versus 10 March 1860 (Umar Tall page): 1861 followed. (2) Hamdullahi: May 1862 (Toucouleur page), reoccupied spring 1863 and lost February 1864 (both pages, with the Umar Tall page contradicting itself). (3) Muntaga's revolt 1884 (Toucouleur page) versus 1885 (Ahmadu Tall page). (4) Nioro: 1891 (Toucouleur page) versus an advance in December 1890 and evacuation "shortly afterward" (Ahmadu Tall page). (5) The Toucouleur infobox says 1852-1903; the text ends the empire in 1893 and the dissolve follows the text. (6) Nioro/Kaarta: April 1855 (Toucouleur Empire and Umar Tall pages) versus 1854 (the Kaarta page, opened for the kaarta dossier); 1855 followed, the kaarta dossier ends in April 1855.

Not on any page opened: an area; the outline between 1864 and 1890; Khasso (submitted 1854, rebelled later, not drawn); Makhana and Guidimakha; Timbuktu (Umar Tall was repulsed 1863); Futa Toro (only a sphere of influence under the 1860 treaty); the fate of Dinguiraye and Bambuk before 1893 (carried to the end at confidence 1 in the 1891 and 1893 keyframes); the dates of Archinard's Koundian-Ségou-Nioro-Bandiagara campaigns beyond what the Ahmadu Tall page gives.

For the french-africa dossier (af-08): the Treaty of Gory (12 May 1887) made the empire a nominal French protectorate (vassal tint 1887-1890); Ségou 1890, Nioro 1891, Bandiagara 1893 are French gains. For the Tidiani/Aguibou line at Bandiagara the pages say only that Tidiani Tall contested the succession and the French installed Aguibou in 1893.

Gazetteer: Bambuk (region, Kéniéba/Kédougou gold country) and Kaarta (region around Nioro du Sahel) and Bandiagara and Hamdullahi (places) are not in the gazetteer; proxies used (Kédougou admin1, Nioro du Sahel 150 km, Mopti 60 km). Wikidata id unknown (null).
