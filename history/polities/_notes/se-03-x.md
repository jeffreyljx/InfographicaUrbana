In the 20 dossiers of this run every event, keyframe verdict and war cites a page opened with WebFetch in this run (accessed 2026-10-24); where a page was silent the event is confidence 1 with the words 'page silent', or is not recorded. Confidence 3 is meant only for events where the page gave both year and land. Natural Earth names and discs (lon/lat/km) stand in where the gazetteer has no name.

## Method and conventions

- The suzerain's ledger names the vassal as counterpart (the schema's convention); occupation events sit in the owner's ledger with the occupier as counterpart; both sides of a dependency should carry events, and the sibling dossiers' counterpart strings are listed under 'Reconciliations' below.
- Colony dossiers take the metropole's colour family (britain, portugal, france for the Dutch, americas for the American Philippines); rebel states are type rebel; native states are type state, family southeast-asia.
- Where no page gives an area or outline the shape is a disc, a province or a Cliopatria outline of a neighbour, and the certainty level is 'soft'; the keyframe note says so.
- The Dutch East India Company needed seven pages (budget 2-5) because it is the largest state the group lacked; the other new dossiers used one to three pages each.
