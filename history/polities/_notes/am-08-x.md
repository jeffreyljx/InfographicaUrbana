Written by the am-08-x research agent on 2026-10-19. Every dossier here is a new state (cliopatria_names []). Pages opened with WebFetch only; thin pages are said so per state. Cross-dossier issues are first.

- centralist-mexico (am-08-e) records the 1840 loss and the 1842-43 contest against republic-of-yucatan, whose dossier mirrors them; federated-republic-of-mexico is the counterpart of the August 1848 merge; mexico (1868-) is the counterpart of the 1901 loss of chan-santa-cruz.
- empire-of-brazil (am-08-b) uses the counterpart strings 'Cabanagem rebels' and 'Confederation of the Equator'; the dossiers cabanagem-rebels and confederation-of-the-equator are those states. Its Olinda contest zone of 1824 is the empire's reading; the Confederation page names only Pernambuco.
- haiti (am-08-a) records the October 1806 split and the 1820 merge with counterpart kingdom-of-haiti, and the 9 February 1822 gain with counterpart republic-of-spanish-haiti; both dossiers are written here and mirror those events.
- essequibo and berbice (am-06-c) end at the cession of 13 August 1814; british-guiana (written here) is their successor, and guyana (Cliopatria 1967-) follows it; the essequibo and berbice dossiers name british-colonial-empire as successor, which could be re-pointed to british-guiana.
- republic-of-cuba-in-arms-ten-years-war (written here) is the first Republic in Arms of 1869-1878; cuban-nationalists (am-08-h) is the second (1895-1899); both use kingdom-of-spain as opponent, captaincy-general-of-cuba (written here) is the colony itself.
- colony-of-jamaica lists the Cayman Islands, Turks and Caicos and the Bay Islands as dependencies by name (no dossiers); british-honduras is also its dependency 1749-1884 (infobox pairing by order, not stated by the page).
- west-indies-federation names british-colonial-empire (Cliopatria aggregate) as predecessor and successor for the nine smaller colonies: no separate dossiers exist for them yet.
- falkland-islands names rio-de-la-plata (Cliopatria 1811-1833) as predecessor and argentine-republic (Cliopatria 1930-) for the 1982 occupation; the Falkland Islands do not appear in the pages opened for rio-de-la-plata.

## mapuche (Mapuche, 1810-1884)
Cliopatria has no Mapuche row (checked data/polities.csv). Slug 'mapuche' is the one that captaincy-general-of-chile.json and republic-of-chile.json already use as counterpart.
Thin pages: none of the pages opened dates the Biobío as the frontier in 1810 or gives the terms of the 1825 Parlamento de Tapihue (the Arauco War page and the Spanish Tapihue page are stubs for this); the 1810 start is the project's scope, not a founding, and the `found` event is confidence 1. A gap-filler should open the Spanish-language 'Guerra de Arauco' or 'Pacificación de la Araucanía' pages.
Puelmapu: included because the Mapuche page names it and says Argentina conquered it; Argentine provinces La Pampa, Neuquén and Río Negro (and Chubut for 1879-1884) are the researcher's reading and the pampas were also held by other peoples (Ranquel, Tehuelche) that the pages do not separate. If the reviewer prefers, drop the Argentine half to a separate dossier 'Puelmapu'.
Orélie-Antoine's Kingdom of Araucanía and Patagonia (17-20 November 1860 to 5 January 1862) held no recorded ground and is a war entry only; if wanted it can be its own dossier (capital Perquenco, claimed).
Capitals: none recorded on the pages opened. Chile's 1852 province of Arauco ('to administer all territories south of the Bio-Bío River') was an administrative claim and is not drawn.
Area: the Occupation page gives no area for the free territory (only about 500,000 ha confined to reductions, nearly 3,000 títulos de merced); the infobox comparison could not be made.
Gazetteer: Cunco, Curacautín and Traiguén are not in places.jsonl (Angol, Temuco, Lonquimay, Villarrica are); the region 'Araucania' is used for the whole Mapuche country south of the Biobío.
