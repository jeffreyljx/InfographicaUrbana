## Fixes applied by the reviewer (all re-validated, 0 errors)

### Both Polish dossiers: the region "Lesser Poland" overdraws Kraków
The gazetteer region "Lesser Poland" is the union of four voivodeships: Lesser Poland, Świętokrzyskie, Subcarpathian and Lublin. Every use of it in these dossiers means Kraków (texts: "Kraków", "Kraków and the Seniorate", "Kraków and Lesser Poland, the High Duke's seat"). As written it gave Kraków's holder the Subcarpathian voivodeship (Przemyśl, Sanok: Rus' land, which the Sandomierz ledger itself loses in 1215) and the Lublin voivodeship to the Bug (Chełm, Daniel of Galicia's seat), and in the Silesia ledger it also drew Sandomierz, Bolesław V's own duchy, as Henry the Bearded's in 1232-1240. Replaced by `{"admin1": "Lesser Poland", "country": "Poland"}` (the Kraków land), text kept: Silesia 7 places (/ledger/13, /ledger/23, /ledger/28, /ledger/31, /keyframe_review/11/correction, /wars/2/zones/0, /wars/5/zones/0); Sandomierz 24 places (all Kraków gains, losses and contests, the corrections of keyframes 1177-1220 and 1250, war zones 0 and 3) plus /wars/7/zones/0 ("Kraków and Sandomierz") rewritten as Lesser Poland voivodeship + the Sandomierz 1285 shape.

Now a loss to "Władysław I the Elbow-high".

### pomerania
- /ledger/1 (1169 loss of the Demmin-Tribsees strip to holy-roman-empire, confidence 1) removed and /keyframe_review/2 and /3 (1169, 1177) changed from ok to adjust with the 1139 outline.
