# am-06-x: research notes

Batch am-06-x (Spanish and Portuguese America, 1492-1825, states Cliopatria lacks). Twelve new dossiers, all validating with 0 errors; 27 Wikipedia pages opened with WebFetch in this run, none failed in the end (four wrong titles were retried). Access date 2026-10-19.

## Cross-cutting items

- Method: no Cliopatria row exists for any of these states, so each shape is Cliopatria's Spanish Empire or Portuguese Empire shape intersected with modern countries or gazetteer regions, minus lands that are separately dossiered, plus what a page dates. The pages give the dated changes; the bridging between them is recorded at confidence 1 or 2.
- Overlap policy: subordinate units (Guatemala, Chile, Louisiana, Florida, Maranhao-Grao-Para, Saint-Domingue) have their own dossiers and are subtracted from their nominal parent (New Spain, Peru, Portuguese Brazil), so the map never draws two colours on one cell.
- Page conflicts: Peru page (1724, 1740) vs New Granada page (5 Nov 1723, 20 Aug 1739): the latter used in both dossiers. Provincias Internas 1776 vs Santa Fe 1777. Spanish Florida cession: 17 July 1821 (used) vs 22 Feb 1821 (Adams-Onis page). Pensacola 1696 (body, used) vs 1698 (lead). Maranhao capital 1737 vs 1751; end 1772 vs 1775.
- Cliopatria conflict: Haiti (1792-1802, group am-08) covers the same land as Saint-Domingue 1792-1803; saint-domingue ends 1791-08.
- Spanish Louisiana ends 1801 (infobox) while administration lasted to 1803/1804; french-louisiana (am-06-c) draws the interval as an occupation.
- Time spans differ from Wikipedia's: captaincy-general-of-chile runs to 1826 (Chiloe), colonial-brazil runs to 1822.
- Infobox areas are not comparable with the shapes: Peru 1.34 M km2 (the modern country) vs shapes of about 2.2-3.6 M km2; the New Spain, Guatemala, Chile, Florida, Brazil (late extent only) and Maranhao pages give no usable area.
- Rulers are omitted in every dossier (no page opened gives a full list).
