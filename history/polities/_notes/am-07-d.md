## Vermont Republic (vermont-republic)

Cliopatria's two Vermont shapes (1783-1787, 1788-1790) are the same 28,159 km2 outline. The page does not give an infobox area, so the area check was made against the gazetteer only (87% of present Vermont, 3% of New York and 5% of Massachusetts at the cell edge). Cliopatria starts the row in 1783 and ends it in 1790; the Republic ran 15 January 1777 to 4 March 1791, so this dossier adds a 1777-1782 keyframe (same outline) and ends in 1791.

One page opened (budget 1-2). Not on it: the day of the 1777 constitution, the townships of the 1781 unions, any fighting between Vermont and New York. A gap-filler could open 'New Hampshire Grants' and 'Haldimand affair'. The Eastern and Western Unions are drawn as a one-cell band along the frontier because the page names no towns.

Counterparts: united-states-of-america (am-07-c; its ledger has the 1777 loss to Vermont and the 4 March 1791 gain). New York and New Hampshire are US states without dossiers of their own.

## California Republic (california-republic)

Rebel republic of 25 days (14 June to 9 July 1846). Cliopatria's single shape (1846-1847, 15,546 km2, 49 cells) is too large in space (it covers the whole Bay Area down to Monterey Bay) and in time (the 1847 year is wrong); the correction is a 40 km circle around Sonoma and the year 1846 alone. Typed 'rebel' because the page says the 'California Republic' name 'appeared only on the flag' and never had a formal government (infobox 'Capital: None').

Pages opened (2 of 1-2): California Republic and Bear Flag Revolt. Not on them: the Treaty of Cahuenga (13 January 1847), the Treaty of Guadalupe Hidalgo and statehood; those belong to the USA dossier. Neither page gives an area.

Counterparts: federated-republic-of-mexico (am-08) and united-states-of-america (am-07-c; its ledger has the 14 June 1846 Bear Flag contest against Mexico).

Gazetteer: 'Sonoma' has no place entry (Santa Rosa, Vallejo and San Francisco do); the area is a lon/lat circle at 38.292N, 122.458W and Sonoma is listed for the gazetteer agent.

## Canada (canada)

Cliopatria's Canada row begins in 1932 (after the Statute of Westminster, 11 December 1931), but the Canadian state begins on 1 July 1867: this dossier starts in 1867 and adds five new keyframes for 1867-1931, drawn as regions (Ontario and Quebec as the Upper Canada and Canada (St Lawrence) regions, the Arctic Islands as an approximate polygon). From 1867 to 1931 the same land lies inside Cliopatria's British Colonial Empire row (1706-1999; by the gazetteer that row holds 98-100% of the Maritime provinces in 1867, almost all of Canada in 1900 and 1930, and in 1935 only Newfoundland and Labrador, 90%); the British dossier (eu-11) should either carry the Dominion as a vassal of the Empire (counterpart 'canada', 1867-1931) or the renderer should let this dossier win the overlap. No event is written for the Statute of Westminster because no ledger type fits a change of status.

Sanity check: infobox area 9,984,670 km2 against the shapes (9.63 M without Newfoundland and Labrador; 10.03 M with, ratio 1.005). The 1973-1975 shape (10.139 M) carries 418 extra lake cells and is adjusted to the 1976 shape. The 1949 gain of 1,285 cells is Newfoundland and Labrador.

Page budget used (5 of 5): Canada, Territorial evolution of Canada, Dominion of Newfoundland, Alaska boundary dispute, Statute of Westminster 1931. Not opened: Rupert's Land, North-West Rebellion, Canadian Confederation, Nunavut, Seshat Canada (ca_canada_dominion). Internal changes (Manitoba 1870, Keewatin 1876, Ontario's boundary 1874/1889, Yukon 1898, Alberta and Saskatchewan 1905, the 1912 extensions, Nunavut 1999) change no outer border and carry no ledger event; the Territorial evolution page dates all of them for a provincial-growth layer if wanted.

Page disagreements: Labrador decision 11 March 1927 (Territorial evolution) or 1 April 1927 (Dominion of Newfoundland).

Counterparts needing dossiers: the Hudson's Bay Company lands (Rupert's Land, group am-07-x; used here as 'hudson-s-bay-company-lands'), and the Dominion of Newfoundland (1907-1949, used as 'dominion-of-newfoundland'; Cliopatria has no Newfoundland row, am-07-x). The Colony of British Columbia and Prince Edward Island are drawn as British Colonial Empire land (the aggregate row).

Rulers: none given; the monarchy and prime ministers are not on the pages opened.

Gazetteer: 'Upper Canada' and 'Canada (St Lawrence)' are existing regions used as the 1867 Ontario and Quebec; the Arctic Islands are a polygon (the gazetteer has no Arctic Archipelago, Baffin or Ellesmere); 'Skagway' is a place with country USA.
