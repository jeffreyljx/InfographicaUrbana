The validator warns that the slug does not exist yet (4 warnings per dossier); if x names it otherwise, the 8 events need the new slug.
2. eu-22 (russian-federation): Cliopatria's Russian Federation row contains Chechnya in 1996-1999 (16,926,240 km2) and leaves it out in 1992-1995 and 2000-2005 (16,908,853 and 16,908,806 km2). The pages say Chechnya was de facto independent 1991-2000 (First Chechen War: Russian troops fully withdrawn, 'de facto independence' kept) and Russian from May 2000 (direct rule). So the Russian shape should exclude Chechnya 1996-1999 and include it from 2000; chechen-republic's ledger carries the matching loss/gain (contest 1994-12 to 1996-08, gain 1996-12, contest 1999-10 to 2000-05, loss 2000-05).
3. Cliopatria's Republic of Armenia and Republic of Azerbaijan rows put Nagorno-Karabakh and the seven districts in Armenia (41,382 km2 in 1996-2024) and cut them from Azerbaijan (70,479 km2). Both 2024 shapes are stale (verdict wrong). The dossiers keep Armenia = Armenia proper and Azerbaijan = the whole de jure country, and draw the Karabakh land through contest, occupation and vassal events. This is a modelling choice: if the rendering agents prefer Cliopatria's de facto outlines, they can use the events' regions as the diff.
4. Same state or not (Q399 and Q227): not. Cliopatria's 'Armenia' (1918-1919) and 'Azerbaijan Democratic Republic' (1918-1919) are separate states from the republics since 1991, with the Soviet republics (armenian-soviet-socialist-republic, azerbaijan-soviet-socialist-republic, both already written) between them. Predecessor links point to the SSR dossiers.
