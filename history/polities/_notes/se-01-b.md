## Chenla, Land Chenla, Water Chenla (Wikidata Q1057118)
- Judged one state that divided c. 707, written as three dossiers (chenla 550-707, land-chenla 707-802, water-chenla 707-790). Reason: the Chinese sources treat the halves as separate realms (separate tribute in 717) and Cliopatria's chenla_641 shape equals Land plus Water of 718 (426,926 against 427,021 km2). The Chenla page itself says most modern historians reject a unified Chenla; Vickery calls the Land/Water division 'misleading and meaningless'. If the reviewers prefer one state, the three can be merged under `chenla` with the three Names in cliopatria_names.
- Dates moved: start 550 (Cliopatria 555); Funan annexed from 627 (Cliopatria grows the shape only in 641; the page's span is 627-649); split c. 707 (Cliopatria 718); Land Chenla ends 802, the Khmer Empire's conventional year (Cliopatria 805); Water Chenla ends c. 790 (Cliopatria 805).
- Unsettled: Isanavarman's reign and Isanapura's founder (the page contradicts itself); the capitals of Land Chenla (Pelliot Sambhupura, Coedes Wen Tan, Hoshino Kalasin/Maha Sarakham; Hoshino's drawn) and Water Chenla (Vyadhapura or Baladityapura, unlocated; the dot is the centre of the Cliopatria shape); whether Water Chenla ended c. 790 (Chenla page) or lived on to 802 as a Javanese vassal under Jayavarman II (Jayavarman II page); who the Javanese power is (Shailendra of Srivijaya per the Jayavarman II page; the Chenla page says 'the Javanese monarchy'); no frontier is given by any page (Chenla stays soft throughout).
- The Champa page is silent on any Cham-Chenla war or land change 550-805 (it records only the Javanese raids of 774 and 787 on Champa).
- Needs from others: funan (se-01-a, matching loss, contest, dissolve already written); srivijaya (Cliopatria row, another group) vassal_start/gain for Water Chenla c. 780-790, ended 802; khmer-empire the gains of Land Chenla and Water Chenla's land in 802; a Dvaravati dossier for the war notes.

## Lavo Kingdom
- Kept as one state (not split): the page treats Lavo as one mandala 'from the 7th century until 1388' with changing seats, dynasties and overlords. The two Cliopatria gaps (1028-1055, 1188-1240) are Khmer-held in Cliopatria; Lavo's own keyframes fill 1052-1055 (Haripunjaya's prince) and 1218-1240, and the Khmer dossier is left to show Angkor's governorship 1006-1051 and 1181-1217.
- Dates moved: start 648 (Cliopatria 638, the 638-647 shape is Dvaravati's land); end 1388 (Cliopatria 1394).
- The page gives five dates for Angkor's conquest (946 vassal, 1001, c. 1005, 1006, 1011-1014); 1006 is used. The Khmer Empire page is silent on it.
- The page's infobox and succession table are garbled (Narai I appears in two columns with different end dates; seat rows overlap). Capitals follow the infobox and the Ayutthaya page.
- Unsettled: the 1056/1066 bulge to 10.1 N (Khmer land in Cliopatria's 1028 shape; no page names a southern conquest); the 1375 strip (conf 1, to Ayutthaya); the extent of Lavo in 1241-1351 (42,717 km2 against the infobox's 170,000 undated); the 1155 'independence'; the end of Haripunjaya's hold (conf 1 in the Haripunjaya dossier); Siamese chronicle claim that Anawrahta took the whole Menam valley (Pagan page: undated claim, not drawn).
- Counterparts without a dossier: dvaravati, tambralinga, suphannaphum (Suphannabhum). Others' dossiers to add matching events: khmer-empire (gain 1006, loss 1052, gain 1181, loss 1218, vassal 946-1052), ayutthaya-kingdom (gains 1351, 1375, 1388).
