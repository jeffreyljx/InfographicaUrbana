# Notes for group eu-13-x (Italy, additions batch)

Written 2026-10-31. Cliopatria has no row for any state here, so every keyframe is new (verdict adjust) and no Cliopatria area could be compared; infobox areas are compared where a page gave one. 13 dossiers, all validate with `--resolve` and no gazetteer warnings (places Cliopatria-style names were avoided in favour of provinces and coordinates).

## What each dossier rests on
- republic-of-siena (1125-1555): Republic of Siena, Italian War of 1551-1559, History of Siena, Massa Marittima, Montalcino. Extent 1335-1555 = Siena + Grosseto provinces (7,840 km2 on the gazetteer vs the page's 8,000 km2). Unsettled: growth before the 16th century (Grosseto, ports, Montepulciano, Chiusi, Orbetello undated), day of the 1555 surrender (15/17/21 April), the 1547 Spanish governor vs an earlier garrison. The 1552-1555 French protectorate belongs in France's dossier as vassal_start with counterpart republic-of-siena. Hand-over: Siena surrenders to Spain (1555), the Florence ledger (eu-13-i) records the occupation to July 1557.
- republic-of-siena-in-montalcino (1555-1559): same pages; a rump kept as its own short dossier because the Florence ledger names it as a counterpart. End 3 April 1559 (war page) vs 31 May 1559 (History of Siena).
- duchy-of-ferrara (1208-1597): Duchy of Ferrara, War of Ferrara (1482-1484), Cesare d'Este, Rovigo, Republic of Venice. Matches the Papal States vassal events (1208, 1597) and the Venice ledger's Rovigo events. Ferrara only: Este Modena and Reggio (1288/89-1814) are covered by nobody; the slug duchy-of-modena-and-reggio is eu-20's Cliopatria row (1815-1859) and should be extended back. Rovigo 1438-1481 is a bridge; the 1509 recovery is undated on the Rovigo page.
- marquisate-of-montferrat (967-1708): Marquisate of Montferrat, Montferrat, War of the Mantuan Succession; repaired 2026-10-31 with Lordship of Milan, Visconti of Milan, Casale Monferrato, Asti, Alba, Alessandria, Matteo I Visconti, Chivasso, Trino (see the repair pass below). The extent is no longer the Alessandria and Asti provinces (the region the Montferrat page describes) but circles at the towns the pages name, with dated gains and losses (Casale 1292/1370/1404, the war of 1356-1358, Asti 1356-1372, Pavia 1356-1359). The Mantua page says Mantua got Montferrat in 1531, the Montferrat page 1536 (Spanish seizure 1533-1536).
- duchy-of-mantua (1328-1708): Duchy of Mantua, Marquisate of Mantua, War of the Mantuan Succession, plus the Montferrat pages. Montferrat is drawn as a vassal 1536-1708 (personal union, a modelling choice). No area on either page. Austrian Mantua 1708-1797 belongs to the Habsburg dossier.
- lordship-of-milan (1259-1395): Lordship of Milan, Visconti of Milan, Gian Galeazzo Visconti, Scaliger, Mastino II; repaired 2026-10-31 with Matteo I Visconti, Asti, Casale Monferrato, Alessandria. Province-level readings of dated city acquisitions, now without Cuneo province, without the 1259-1270 cities no page dates, with Pavia out 1356-1358 and Asti, Alba, Novara and Casale dated, and drawn without the Montferrat holdings of the same year. Unsettled: years after 1313 per city, Alessandria (1313 on the Visconti page, 1345 on the Alessandria page), Lodi after 1271, Bologna after 1356, end of the 1353 Genoa lordship (1356), 1395 day (5 September here, 11 May in the Duchy dossier).
- scaliger-lordship-of-verona (1262-1387): Scaliger, Cangrande I, Mastino II, Visconti of Milan (Brescia 1337 only). Provinces by stage; Parma and Lucca after 1339 and Treviso/Belluno taker are bridges (confidence 1). Slug and the string "Scaliger rulers at Verona" match the Venice ledger.
- carrara-lordship-of-padua (1318-1405): House of Carrara, Cangrande, Mastino II, Gian Galeazzo. Gaps in life 1328-1336 and 1388-1389 (no keyframe). Carrara page says "in 1338 they ousted the Veronese della Scala", the Mastino infobox 1337.
- viceroyalty-of-naples (1504-1734, colony): Kingdom of Naples, Viceroyalty of Naples (a list of viceroys). Reuses the Cliopatria Kingdom of Naples 1501 shape. Needs vassal events from the Spanish and Habsburg dossiers; check double drawing with Habsburg Monarchy 1714-1734.
- state-of-the-presidi (1557-1801, colony): State of the Presidi, Republic of Siena. About 300 km2 on the page; circles come to about 430 km2.
- republic-of-lucca (1160-1805): Republic of Lucca, Mastino II. Lucca province as reading. No keyframe 1335-1369 (Verona then Pisa; the Pisan years are undated on the page).
- duchy-of-urbino (1443-1631): Duchy of Urbino, Federico da Montefeltro. 5,556 km2 on the page vs about 3,300 km2 for the province plus circles at Gubbio and Senigallia. The 1625 vs 1631 devolution date is unreconciled.
- state-of-milan (1535-1796, colony): State of Milan (one page, same article as Duchy of Milan); repaired 2026-10-31 with Peace of Utrecht, War of the Polish Succession, War of the Austrian Succession, Aix-la-Chapelle, Lomellina. Reuses the Duchy of Milan 1535 shape to 1712, then loses the Savoyard districts (11 April 1713), Novara and Tortona (November 1738) and the Worms cession (13 September 1743); the Franco-Sardinian occupation 1733-1738 is booked as occupation. The double drawing with kingdom-of-spain and habsburg-monarchy is an open orchestrator decision.

## Repair pass 2026-10-31 (after the review of eu-13-x)

### marquisate-of-montferrat
- Reading chosen (finding 1): the state is circles at the towns the pages name, not the provinces. Evidence: the Montferrat page's 'modern provinces of Alessandria and Asti' is the region ('its extent has varied over time'); the Marquisate page has Montferrat fighting the independent communes of Asti and Alessandria; the Alessandria page has Alessandria a fief of Boniface in 1193 and under the Visconti protection from 1345. Chivasso and Trino from 967, Casale from 1292, then the dated 1356-1372 holdings; Alba and Nizza Monferrato from 1536 as a bridge (confidence 1). Instead of subtracting Milan from Montferrat I did the reverse: lordship-of-milan is drawn without Montferrat's holdings of each year (same effect, one place to maintain).
- Mirrors (finding 2): gains of 1356 (Asti 23 January; Alba, Mondovi, Chieri; Pavia), losses of Alba (8 June 1358) and Pavia (1359), Casale lost 1370 and regained 1404 (Casale page), Asti lost 1372 (Asti page). All have the matching event in lordship-of-milan. Mondovi and Chieri are dropped on 8 June 1358 as a bridge (the peace names only Novara, Alba, Asti and Pavia); Cuneo is not drawn for Montferrat (the Lordship page gives it both to Montferrat and to Philip II of Taranto, June 1356).
- Vassal events (finding 3): vassal_start 1536 and vassal_end 1708, counterpart duchy-of-mantua, region the shape of the year.
- Capitals (finding 4): Chivasso 1200-1299 lies inside the shape (the circle runs to 1304); Casale from 1471 (Casale page: William VIII chose it 'as the permanent location of the marquisate court'). Nothing for 1300-1470.
- Trino and Alba (finding 5): Trino is in the first keyframe (undated, first attested 1628); Alba gains at 1536 (confidence 1) and goes with Trino in 1631.
- Disagreements and bridges: Chivasso is dropped at 1305 as a bridge (confidence 1; no page dates its loss; the Chivasso and Trino pages are silent on Montferrat). The Casale page has Casale under Montferrat in 1292 while the Matteo I Visconti page has Matteo 'taking Casale' that year. I could not fill the Palaiologan centuries (1305-1533): the shapes are minima. The 1345 'division of the Angevin possessions' between Montferrat and Milan (Alessandria page) names no place.
- Needs others: duchy-of-mantua /ledger/3 and /6 still carry the old Alessandria and Asti shape (that dossier is verdict fix, not mine to touch now); house-of-savoy /ledger/11 and /20 name duchy-of-mantua rather than this state as the loser; duchy-of-milan has no loss of Casale in 1404.

### lordship-of-milan
- ledger/14 and /17 retyped as rename (the 1354 shares are on no page and contradicted the keyframes); the Genoa end (reviewer's edit) kept.
- Pavia: a Visconti city from 1315 (Matteo I Visconti page: 'followed by the capture of Pavia'), out of their hands from 1356 (siege of April 1356; 'Asti and Pavia remained to the Marquis of Montferrat'), back in 1359 ('Pavia surrendered to Galeazzo II'). I disagree in part with the review, which wanted Pavia removed from all of K2-K7: the Visconti page has Galeazzo II holding court at Pavia after 1355, so only 1356-1358 is outside.

- First keyframe: Milan only 1259-1262, Como from December 1263, Lodi in 1270, Milan only again from 1271 (the 1271 loss now books Como and Lodi). Brescia, Cremona and Novara are not drawn before 1271 because no page dates their gain.
- Also added: Asti (1340, lost 23 January 1356, back 1379, dowry 1389; Asti page), Novara out 1356 and back 8 June 1358 ('returned to the Visconti', taker not named), Casale from 1370, the 1313 gain split by certainty (the Visconti page's list, Como and Novara undated, Pavia 1315). The thin finding on 1313 is only partly answered: the Matteo I Visconti page dates Pavia (1315) and Casale/Novara/Vercelli/Alessandria influence to 1292 but not Bergamo, Tortona, Piacenza.
