## nawabs-of-bengal (Cliopatria "Nawabs of Bengal" 1718-1761, 5 shapes)
- Life span: 1717 (Farrukhsiyar recognises Murshid Quli Khan) to 23 June 1757 (Plassey: "brought an end to the independence of the nawabs"). Cliopatria runs to 1761 and hands Bengal to its British Empire shape in 1762; the 1757-1761 shape is kept (verdict adjust) as a British client under puppet nawabs. The vassal_start belongs in the british-empire dossier.
- Cliopatria draws Bihar from 1718 and omits Odisha; the Nawabs page puts Orissa in the 1717 jurisdiction and dates "Annexation of the Bihar Subah" to 1733. Bihar is removed 1718-1732, Odisha added 1717-1751 (contest with the Marathas 1742-1751, formal loss 1752). The page's prose also names Bihar for 1717: disagreement recorded, the dated table entry followed.
- Page silent (nothing filled from memory): Mir Qasim's 1760 cession of Burdwan, Midnapore, Chittagong; the retaking of Calcutta in 1757; the date of the post-Plassey treaty; the 24 Parganas. Orissa "ceded January 1747" (Nawabs page) conflicts with the 1749 recovery and the 1751-52 dates and is not used.
- Needs a dossier: the Bhonsles of Nagpur (nagpur-kingdom), taker of Orissa; counterpart given as maratha-empire.

## sikh-confederacy (Cliopatria "Sikh Confederacy" 1762-1806, 7 shapes; 1763-1768 missing)
- Life span 1748 (Gurmata at Amritsar; Misl infobox 1748-1799) to 1799 (Ranjit Singh unites it into the Sikh Empire). Cliopatria's 1800-1806 shapes (5,631 and 3,082 km2) are remnant misls (the Amritsar patch of the Bhangi misl, annexed 1802; the 1803-1806 patch is unidentified on the pages opened), kept as adjust/ok with a status change. sa-05-f's sikh-empire records gains from sikh-confederacy in 1800 and 1802; this dossier hands the core over in its 1799 dissolve.
- Cliopatria's shape is a set of city patches (Lahore, Amritsar, Kasur, Gujrat, Sialkot, Gujranwala, Jullundur, Ludhiana), none in Haryana, none at Multan: the Cis-Sutlej Phulkian lands are added from 14 January 1764 (Sirhind). Detail of 1772-1798 unverified: page budget. The 1748-1761 core (Amritsar and Kasur discs) is the researcher's minimal reading.
- Inference flagged: Durrani as the prior holder of Lahore (1761, 1765) is not named on any page. Kasur 1761: from whom not named.
- Needs dossiers: bhangi-misl, sukerchakia-misl, the Phulkian states (Patiala from 1763-64).

## nawab-of-awadh (Cliopatria "Nawab of Awadh" 1727-1802, 13 shapes)
- Life span 1722 (infobox formation 26 January 1722; the text says 1724; appointment as governor 9 September 1722) to the annexation of 11 February 1856 (the Wajid Ali Shah infobox says 7 February). The Nawab page's abolition 3 March 1858 is the end of the 1857-58 rebel government (Hazrat Mahal, Birjis Qadr), which needs a rebel dossier.
- Cliopatria ends the Nawabs in 1802 and gives all of Awadh to its British Empire shape from 1803 (116,334 km2), yet the 1801 cession left the Nawab about half (new keyframe 1801-1856, about 47,000 km2 by cells; the historical rump is larger).
- Cliopatria dates Rohilkhand to 1794 and the Rampur carve-out to 1796; the Rohilla War page gives 23 April and 7 October 1774. Allahabad district out from 16 August 1765 (Shuja page; Shah Alam page). Doab (Kora) regained: the year is not on the page (confidence 1, Cliopatria 1775). Benares country: Awadh zamindar-vassal from 1737, lost 1775 (Benares page: "Between 1775 and 1795"; the same page also says the Company annexed Benares "in the later 1760s", and the Shuja page lists Chunar, Benares, Ghazipur, Allahabad among payments without years).
- Unverified: the 1788 loss of 4,300 km2 in the north to Cliopatria's Nepal 1788 shape (no page mentions it; probably the Tarai); pre-1737 inclusion of the Benares country.
- "Annexation_of_Oudh" returned url_not_found and was replaced by Wajid Ali Shah.
- Needs dossiers: benares-state, rampur-state (rohilkhand-state names it), the 1857-58 Awadh rebel government.

## hyderabad-state (Cliopatria "Hyderabad State" 1727-1798 and 1947, 13 shapes; gap 1799-1946)
- Life span 1724 (infobox "Established 31 July 1724"; Asaf Jah I page "11 October 1724") to 18 September 1948 (Operation Polo 13-17 September). Gap 1799-1946 filled with new keyframes: 1795 (Kharda cession), 1800 (Ceded Districts out), 1804 (Berar), 1805-1852, 1853-1946 (Berar assigned, then leased 1902; drawn occupied), 1948.
- Cliopatria leaves out the Aurangabad country (capital 1724-1763; its Mughal row in 1727, Maratha row 1794-1804, British row 1805): a 50 km disc plus Solapur is added to 1724-1794 and removed in April 1795 (Kharda). The date the rest of Marathwada joined is not on any page (bridged 1805, confidence 1).
- Northern Circars lost 1 March 1768 (Cliopatria 1769), Guntur kept to 1788. The Ceded Districts sit in Cliopatria's Nizam shape from 1727 although the Seringapatam page has Mysore hand the northern land over only in 1792: open question, left as drawn, unverified: page budget.
- Areas: 1727 shape 261,170 km2 against 283,250 km2 for the Hyderabad Subah in 1707 (0.92); 1947 shape 211,298 km2 against 214,187 km2 for 1941 (0.99); Berar 29,340 km2 (1881) against the 27,566 km2 disc.
- Not on any page: the 1853-60 Raichur Doab and Dharaseo assignment, Palkhed's terms, the 1799 Mysore share, the end date of British administration of Berar (occupation_start left open).
- Subsidiary alliance 1796 (Ceded Districts) or 1798 (Hyderabad State). British-client tint from 1798 belongs in british-empire.
- Needs a dossier: nagpur-kingdom (Berar's previous holder).
