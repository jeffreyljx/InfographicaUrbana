Written 2026-11-01. Thirteen new dossiers, none owning a Cliopatria row (cliopatria_names []). Every dossier cites only pages opened with WebFetch in this run. Pages that would not load: none. Wikidata ids are null throughout (wikidata.org is unreachable).

Type choices for the colour and rendering agents: the Habsburg units (habsburg-military-frontier, banat-of-temeswar, kingdom-of-serbia-1718-1739) and venetian-dalmatia are type colony (a dependency ruled from elsewhere); the Ottoman units (pashaliks and eyalets) are type state with family islam-west; the Ottoman Empire row of Cliopatria already covers most of this land, so drawing both will overlap unless the renderer treats them as sub-units.

## Fixes after the adversarial review (2026-11-01)

The reviewer failed nine dossiers (history/polities/_notes/review-eu-18-x.md). Every fix below was made after re-opening the page it rests on (Military Frontier, Sanjak of Scutari, Stato da Màr, Venetian Dalmatia, Herzegovina Eyalet, Eastern Rumelia, Mustafa Pasha Bushati, Kingdom of Albania (medieval)); the reviewer's own edits (Parga circle, counterparts, months) were left as they were. All nine validate with --resolve. The "thin" items were not worked unless a re-opened page settled them.

Pashalik of Scutari. Added the missing 1796/9 loss to prince-bishopric-of-montenegro with the same region as Montenegro's gain (Danilovgrad and the Piperi circle); the keyframes of 1796-1831 subtract it and the Cetinje circle, so the overlap with Montenegro fell from 3 cells to 1 (a Bar/Budva cell in 1813-1814, when Montenegro's keyframe adds Budva). Bar and Podgorica are now in the found region and the keyframes from 1757 on the strength of the Sanjak of Scutari page ("ownership of the cities of Bar, Podgorica, Spuž and Ulcinj was transferred from the Sanjak of Scutari" in 1876-78); the first keyframe is split at 1772 so that Ulcinj enters where the ledger gains it. The dissolve region and the successor land are the 1824-1831 union (15 districts) minus the Montenegrin land. The 1820 Berat gain keeps its event; the loss is now in Yanina. Agreed with the reviewer's edit of the Durrës event (month dropped, confidence 2). Not settled: the haraç (no dates on the pages), the Diber-Debar-Tetovo date, "most of Montenegro".

Pashalik of Yanina.
