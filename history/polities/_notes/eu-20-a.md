## Cliopatria rows that are wrong or need a decision

- Grand Duchy of Tuscany 1797..1848: five of the six shapes (985-1,032 km2) lie near Lucca and the Garfagnana, not in Tuscany (grand duchy about 21,700 km2); only the 1848 shape (24,632 km2) is the grand duchy. They look like a mislabelled Lucca polity. The dossier marks them wrong, starts at 1737 (where republic-of-florence hands over) and adds new keyframes 1737-1796, 1814 and 1849-1859. The state was abolished 21 March 1801 (Kingdom of Etruria 1801-1807, French 1808-1814) and re-established 30 May 1814: one dossier with a gap.
- Batavian Republic 1796..1810: the 1796-1802 shape (259,681 km2) contains Indonesia (VOC lands, drawn by dutch-east-india-company to 1799 and dutch-east-indies from 1800); replaced by the 1803 Netherlands shape. The 1807-1810 shapes are the Kingdom of Holland (5 June 1806 to 9 July 1810), kept in this dossier as rename events; the Batavian page itself names the kingdom its successor, so a split into batavian-republic (1795-1806) and kingdom-of-holland (1806-1810) is a legitimate alternative.
- Ligurian Republic 1797..1814: all four shapes (2,292-3,461 km2) are smaller than the Liguria region (6,070 km2) although the page says the republic covered 'most of the Ligurian region'; all marked adjust to the region. The republic has a gap 1805-1813 (French) and a restoration 28 April to 28 July 1814.
- Cisalpine Republic 1797..1799: shapes agree with the infobox (42,500 km2); the dossier extends to 26 January 1802 (rename to Italian Republic; the italian-republic dossier names it as predecessor) with a new keyframe 1800-1802. Cliopatria's 'Cispadane Republic' row (1800-1802, 7,095 km2) lies in these years and does not match the 1796-1797 Cispadane Republic; the cispadane-republic dossier should say what it is. The 1797 shape also holds Verona, Rovigo and northern Marche (Pesaro e Urbino) cells that no opened page assigns to the republic.
- French First Republic 1792..1795, French Directory 1796..1799: the Wikipedia page treats Convention, Directory and Consulate as one republic (to 18 May 1804); Cliopatria and this project keep three dossiers in colour family 'france'. The Directory starts 26 October 1795 (its page), the Republic ends 3 November 1795 (its page): a two-month overlap and a one-week disagreement. The 1792 shape lacks Savoy and Nice; the 1794 shape draws Belgium, Liege and the Rhineland as plain territory although they were occupied until the annexations of 1795 (Belgium, 1 October) and 1797 (Rhineland, Campo Formio); Corsica is rightly absent 1794-1796.
