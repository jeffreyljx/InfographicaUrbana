All five validate with 0 errors and 0 warnings, also with --resolve (no unresolved region names). Decision common to the five: the starts follow Yaroslav's division, 20 February 1054 (the rus dossier of eu-16-a ends then), with a new keyframe for 1054-1055 (Cliopatria's first shapes are 1056). The pages' own founding years (Novgorod 1136, Kiev 1132, Chernigov 1024, Pereyaslavl 988) are rename or found events, not starts, except Chernigov which starts 1024 because the rus dossier already names it as counterpart for 1024 and 1036. The ends follow Cliopatria (1240) for Kiev, Chernigov and Pereyaslavl, with the Mongol sack as the last dated event (Chernigov and Pereyaslavl in 1239 at confidence 3 as contest events; dissolve at confidence 1). The map therefore draws these three beside the mongol-empire dossier's vassal_start events of 1239-1240.

## novgorod-republic (1054-1478; 22 Cliopatria shapes, 27 keyframe entries, 27 events)
- The largest effort: every shape step is either explained by a dated page (1136, 1293, 1300-1301, 1314 Karelian rising, 12 August 1323, 1397-1398, 1471, January 1478) or flagged as Cliopatria's year at confidence 1 (1126, 1236, 1294, 1314 north, 1344, 1463, 1468).
- Cliopatria rows with no page: the northern expansion (1126 +163,000 km2, 1314 +3,668 cells to the Urals, 1468 Kola). A gap-filler should open Zavolochye (try 'Zavolochie'), Great Perm, Yugra, Kola, 'Muscovite-Novgorodian Wars'.
- 1475-1481: shape marked wrong. Pages: Korostyn 1471 (patrimony of the grand prince, no land named), direct control January 1478. The full 1468 shape holds 1475-1477. grand-principality-of-moscow (eu-16-g) must start the northern 5,900 cells in 1478, not 1475, and should carry a vassal_start for Novgorod at 1471 (Novgorod's side has no such event type).
- 1241-1284: Cliopatria has no Novgorod shape; filled with the 1236 shape (Pskov included, as the Battle on the Ice page has Alexander retaking Pskov and Votia in 1241). Pskov splits in 1285 (Cliopatria; confidence 1) although the Novgorod page gives its formal independence as 1348 (Treaty of Bolotovo); pskov-republic (eu-16-e) agrees with 1285.
- The mongol-empire dossier (ia-02) has a vassal_start for Novgorod in 1241 from the overlap of Cliopatria's Mongol shape with 835 Novgorod cells; the Alexander Nevsky page has Novgorod forced to pay tribute to the Golden Horde in 1259 and says nothing of conquest.
- Karelia: the karelians dossier draws Karelian land as a separate state 1143-1322, overlapping Cliopatria's Novgorod shape in 1143-1235 and 1294-1322; the Swedish-Novgorodian Wars page has Karelia under Novgorod's authority in 1314. The 1236 loss and 1294 gain against karelians are confidence 1.
- Counterpart named by text, no dossier: "Bishopric of Ösel-Wiek" (1240-1241 occupation of Votia). teutonic-order stands for the Livonian Order in the 1240-1241 Pskov occupation (the Livonian Brothers of the Sword merged into it in 1237). grand-principality-of-moscow was not yet on disk; named by slug.
- Not entered (no page opened): the 1259 tribute; the 1348-1349 Swedish occupation of Oreshek (kingdom-of-sweden has it; this page says only 'set it ablaze, but Novgorod soon regained control'); 1278 war on the Karelians (karelians has it); the Lithuanian frontier; Velikiye Luki and Toropets; Torzhok.
- No identity.rulers: the Novgorod page lists only a few princes.

## principality-of-chernigov (1024-1240; 4 shapes, 7 keyframe entries, 7 events)
- Murom-Ryazan: the Murom page makes Murom part of Chernigov's 1054 inheritance and a separate principality only in 1127 (Ryazan after 1129); Cliopatria adds the land in 1072 and cuts it in 1094 where its Ryazan row starts. Followed the pages: the Murom-Ryazan lands are drawn as Chernigov's for 1054-1126 and the loss to principality-of-ryazan is dated 1127. principality-of-ryazan (eu-16-c) starts in 1094 and so overlaps by 264 cells for 1094-1126: move its start to 1127 or drop the three Chernigov keyframes _1054, _1056, _1094.
- The Chernigov page puts the Council of Liubech after Sviatoslav's death in 1076; the Council of Liubech page dates it 19 October 1097. 1097 used in the notes of the keyframes.
- Tmutarakan: Chernigov's from 1054 (Kievan Rus' page); the page says it was 'eventually overtaken', undated. Not drawn. Suggested dossier: tmutarakan (a state Cliopatria lacks; the rus dossier of eu-16-a also used the Tmutarakan page).
- Novgorod-Seversk, named by the Chernigov page as one of three apanage principalities (undated), has no Cliopatria row.
- No infobox area, so the 224,000-266,000 km2 shapes could not be compared. Lithuania's takeover (c. 1357) and the Golden Horde's vassalage (1245-1362) belong to other dossiers.
