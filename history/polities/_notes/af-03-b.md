## Cross-dossier overlaps to settle
- **Diafunu (mali-empire / songhai-empire).** Cliopatria's Songhai 1475 shape holds the whole Nioro du Sahel proxy (195 of 195 cells), but the Askia page dates Diafunu's capture to 1501 (the mali-empire dossier: 1502). songhai-empire withdraws it for 1475-1500; the mali-empire dossier's own 1475 shape also lacks it. A gap-filler should hold Diafunu in Mali's shape until 1501.
- **Ségou remnant (bamana-empire / massina-empire).** Cliopatria's Bamana row ends 1821 and relabels the 104,716 km2 outline as Massina (1822-1860), but the Bamana page runs Ségou to 10 March 1861. bamana-empire adds a 1818-1861 remnant (a 100 km disc at Ségou, extent not on any page); massina-empire removes the same disc from its shapes. Cliopatria's Massina 1820-21 shape (41k km2) has 87 of 195 cells in the Ségou region.
- **Bornu borderlands (sokoto-caliphate / empire-of-kanem).** sokoto-caliphate mirrors the empire-of-kanem loss of 1809 (difference of the Bornu 1808 and 1809 shapes) and its contest of 1808-1827 (the end year is that dossier's bridge).
- **Taghaza (songhai-empire / saadi-sultanate).** The saadi dossier has the Saadi occupations of 1556-57 and 1586; the pages opened here do not date them, so songhai-empire only records Taghaza as an undated Askia gain (confidence 1) and its Moroccan loss is not written. Timbuktu/Gao 1591 and Djenné 1592 mirror the saadi dossier (the latter at confidence 1).
- **Darfur in 1883/1885 (sultanate-of-darfur / mahdist-state).** The Darfur page has the Mahdists conquering Darfur in 1885, the mahdist-state dossier 1883. sultanate-of-darfur records only the 1874 loss to khedivate-of-egypt and the September 1898 regain from mahdist-state.
- **Ilorin and Oyo-Ile (sokoto-caliphate / oyo-empire).** sokoto-caliphate gains Ilorin (1824) and the old Oyo core (1836, confidence 1) from oyo-empire; af-04 should carry the matching losses.

## songhai-empire
- Start 1435 (infobox "Independence from Mali Empire: c. 1430s"; matches the mali-empire loss of Gao); Cliopatria begins 1463. End 1599 (infobox "Saadian invasion of the Songhai Empire: 1599"; Nuh deposed that year) although Tondibi (13 March 1591) ended the empire's effective power. The Askia line continues in Dendi (Lulami) to 1901: needs a dendi-kingdom dossier (af-03-x), and the 1600-1608 Cliopatria specks are marked wrong.
- Cliopatria's 1463-1594 shapes stop at 1.7 E and omit Dendi, which the Sunni Ali page counts in the empire at his accession ("from Dendi to Mema"); every shape is unioned with Dendi (Tillabéri admin1 and a disc at Gaya). The 1595-1608 shapes are a 112-224 km2 speck at 3.7 W, 15.8 N.
- Infobox 1,400,000 km2 (1528) against Cliopatria 629,260: ratio 0.45, not reconciled; the 1515 keyframe adds only Aïr (campaign against Agadez, 1515) and Taghaza (undated). 800,000 km2 (1550) is within range. The 1564 western strip (to the Senegal coast) is undated on every page (confidence 1).
- Pages disagree: Timbuktu 1468 (Songhai page) vs the protection request of 1469 (Sunni Ali page); Djenné 1473 (text), 1475 (lead); Daoud d. 1582 (table) vs 1583 (text); Tondibi force sent 1590 (lead) or 1591 (text).
- Not mapped (no located, dated page): Futa Kingi and Diarra (1512), Kebbi, Katsina, Zaria and Kano (Askia page: Hausa conquests undated; Songhai page silent), Walata (1504, not held), Borgu (1504 disaster). Mema is dated only "1460s" (Songhai page) / at accession (Sunni Ali page) against 1465 in the mali-empire dossier.
- Gazetteer lacks Dendi, Mema, Diafunu, Taghaza (a coordinate is used), Lulami.
