## transcaucasian-democratic-federative-republic: thin
Spot checks (TDFR page and Caucasus campaign page): 22 April 1918 proclamation (infobox 'Federation proclaimed: 22 April 1918'), Batum 'occupied ... on 14 April, with little resistance', war declared 14 April, Kars 'held ... until it was evacuated on 25 April' (siege 23 April, surrender 25 April on the campaign page), Jordania's Georgian declaration (26 May), Armenia and Azerbaijan 'each declared themselves independent on 28 May', infobox 'Dissolution: 28 May 1918'. All confirmed. No infobox area; the page: 'included most of the territory of present-day Armenia, Azerbaijan and Georgia'.
Findings:
1. /keyframe_review/0/correction (not applied): the shape misses the western Erivan Governorate (Alexandropol/Shirak, Surmalu/Igdir), which lies outside all three successor 1918 shapes (armenia_1918 bbox west edge 44.04 E, Gyumri 43.85 E; armenia_1918 is 16,537 km2 against present-day Armenia's 29,743 km2, the post-Batum rump). The federation held these lands until the Ottoman May offensive; the armenia dossier (ledger 3) gains about 16,000 km2 back from the Ottomans in November 1918, so the Ottomans took them in 1918, from the federation. Fix: add {"admin1": "Shirak", "country": "Armenia"} and {"admin1": "Iğdir", "country": "Turkey"} (gazetteer spelling) to the keyframe union and record a May 1918 loss to ottoman-empire (Alexandropol fell in mid-May 1918; page to try: Battle of Sardarabad, First Republic of Armenia).
2. /ledger/0/counterpart and /identity/predecessors/0 (not applied, minor): russian-republic's dossier ends in November 1917 (its ledger 1, dissolve) and lists no Transcaucasian successor, so the found event names a state that no longer exists in April 1918. Fix: name 'nobody' (the Transcaucasian Commissariat's government, no dossier) or keep russian-republic and say in the note that it is the last state the land had.
Cross-dossier: the three splits match the georgia (26 May), armenia (28 May) and azerbaijan-democratic-republic (28 May) found events. The Kars loss (April 1918) has no mirror: ottoman-empire ledger 227 records one 1918 gain of the north-east from russian-republic (Treaty of Batum). Route to the Ottoman dossier's owner: Kars (25 April) and the western Erivan lands (May) from transcaucasian-democratic-federative-republic; Batum (14 April) from nobody, before the federation.

## kathiri-sultanate: thin
Spot checks (Kathiri page; Qu'aiti page): founding 1395 by Badr as-Sahab (lead 1379), 'conquered Ash-Shihr in the 1460s', Shibam taken by the Qu'aiti 'in 1858', 1866 Ghalib bin Muhsin expels Ali bin Naji from Shihr, April 1867 landing near Shihr and the December repulse, 1895 'captured the port at Dhufar, driving out the Governor, who retired to Mirbat', 'In 1897 the port was recaptured', overthrow October 1967 and South Yemen the following month. All confirmed. No infobox area (Kathiri page and Eastern Aden Protectorate page both give none).
Findings (not applied):
1. /keyframe_review/1/correction: the 1858-1967 shape leaves out Shihr while the ledger holds it until April 1867 (ledger 4). The compiler applies the ledger and then snaps to the keyframe in 1858 (Shihr is well over 5 percent of the state), so Shihr leaves the map in 1858 with no event, returns from nobody with the 1866 gain and leaves again in 1867. Fix: either record, with a page, the loss of Shihr before 1866 (the Qu'aiti page shows Ali bin Naji holding it), or split the keyframe at 1867 so that Shihr is in the 1858-1866 shape.
2. Missing protection relation: the infobox has 'Informal protection treaty with the British signed' (no year) and the text 'In the mid-1950s, the Kathiri state was forced to join the British Protectorate of South Arabia'; the Eastern Aden Protectorate page names the Kathiri state in the protectorate. No vassal_start exists. Page to try: Aden Protectorate, Protectorate of South Arabia.
3. The extent: the lead says the state 'ruled Hadhramaut from Dhofar in the east' and later 'lost its eastern regions to the Omani Empire and the Mahra Sultanate'; neither the eastern reach nor its loss is drawn or dated. The 120 km disc (about 45,000 km2) is unsourced and also covers Qu'aiti land in the western wadi.
Cross-dossier: the 1465 Shihr gain against tahirid-sultanate ledger 5 (loss 1499, confidence 1) is a 34-year mismatch, both sides citing pages that disagree (Kathiri page '1460s'; Tahirid page 'end of the fifteenth century'). The 1895 gain and 1897 loss against sultanate-of-oman have no mirror in the Oman dossier (it holds Dhofar from 1879 with no 1895-1897 events). The Qu'aiti counterpart (1858, 1867) needs a dossier (exception, listed by the author).
