Its Wikipedia field ("Berga") is the town's page, which never names a County of Berga. By the pages Berga had counts of its own only 988-1002 and was annexed to Cerdanya in 1002. A `county-of-cerdanya` dossier (Cerdanya page: counts 798-1403) would be the right home; until then county-of-berga carries it (alt name "County of Cerdanya"), ends in 1242 (Roussillon page) not 1235, and records 1002 as a rename.

The dossier takes Vic out, moves the Cerdanya-Berga part to county-of-berga (new keyframe county-of-berga_960) and adds Besalú keyframes for 878-897, 927-935 and 988-1110 (the county lived to 1111; Cliopatria stops in 989). Cliopatria's County of Osona row (990-1110, 2,329 km2) repeats the 898 polygon, so it overlaps the new Besalú keyframes.
- **County of Barcelona**: Cliopatria's Wikipedia field is "Girona" and its Wikidata id Q7038; the county's article is "County of Barcelona" and Q7038 is probably Girona's id (Wikidata unreachable), kept as given. No page opened says Sancho ruled Barcelona or Cerdanya; both dossiers use a stand-in keyframe and leave the question to the Ribagorza and Navarre owners.
