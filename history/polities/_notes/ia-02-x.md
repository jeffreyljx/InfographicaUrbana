- The four pre-1294 uluses (`ulus-of-jochi`, `ulus-of-hulegu`, `ulus-of-chagatai`, `ogedeid-ulus`) and `ariq-boke` lie inside Cliopatria's Mongol Empire row (1206..1293). It already carries the Rus' (1237-40), Baghdad (1258), Syria (1260), Ismaili (1257), Rum and Georgia (1243) events as the empire's own.
- `ulus-of-chagatai` ends 1293 and hands over to `chagatai-khanate` (Cliopatria from 1294); `ogedeid-ulus` ends 1306 and hands over to `chagatai-khanate` as well. The chagatai-khanate dossier should name both as its predecessors.

- `turpan-khanate` lies inside the Moghulistan row (1492-1518) and Eastern Moghulistan (1519-1686). `kara-del` (Hami) lies inside the Ming and Northern Yuan rows at times.
- `sufi-dynasty-of-khwarazm` is the counterpart of the `golden-horde` 1383-88 events and the `timurid-empire` 1402 event; both use the string 'Sufi dynasty of Khwarazm'.
- `amirate-of-qazaghan` mirrors the 1346 split, 1360-61 contest and 1363 loss already in `chagatai-khanate` (counterpart slug used there), and ends in April 1370 where `timurid-empire` starts.
