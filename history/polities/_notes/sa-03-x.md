## Per state
- eastern-chalukyas: the Eastern Chalukyas article gives no war dates and no area. End year 1077 follows the Kulottunga I page (Vengi under direct Chola rule); the infobox says 1189 and the lead "until c. 1001". Capital-move dates (Pishtapura, Vengi, Rajamahendravaram) are placeholders. Loss and recovery of Vengi to the Rashtrakutas, the date of independence from Badami, the Chola conquest year (late 10th century; restoration of Saktivarman "in 1002 CE under the overlordship of the Cholas") are undated. The Chola protectorate 1002-1077 and the 1118-1126 northern loss belong in the chola-empire dossier (vassal_start counterpart eastern-chalukyas).
- hindu-shahis: starts 870 (not c. 822) to follow the turk-shahis dossier, which carries the Kabul kingdom 665-870 with a rename in 822. Peshawar is 27 November 1001 (infobox and body of the battle page, Hindu Shahis infobox) or 27 September (bodies of the Hindu Shahis and Jayapala pages); month 11 kept. Repair pass: the Salt Range and Jhelum country (70 km round Nandana, 32.7 N 72.9 E) is drawn from 878 on the strength of the Jayapala page (Bharat of Lahore contested 'Nandana, Jailam and Takeshar' c. 990-991), so the capital of 1002-1014 lies inside the state; the rest of the Punjab before 999 is not drawn (Lahore came 998-999, Multan was Daud's, no page places Sirhind or the Chenab). identity.rulers added from the Hindu Shahis infobox accession years (a convention: each reign ends where the next begins). The Ghaznavid dossier should carry vassal_start (1009) for Anandapala's tributary status and the gains of 991, 1001, 1014, 1026.
- kalachuris-of-tripuri: no founding or end year on the page (state drawn 850-1212, "at least until 1212"). Not on any page opened: the Lata country (shown as Kalachuri 1056-1074 in the paramara-dynasty and chaulukya-dynasty dossiers), the Kalinjar loss before 954 and Gangeya's eastern gains from the Chandelas c. 1035 (both in chandela-kingdom), Karna's Magadha and Gauda gains. Years of the 1085 (Gahadavala) and 1152 (Chandela) losses are bridges matched to those dossiers.
- licchavi-kingdom: the intro says c. 450-c. 750 but the list runs to Mandeva IV (875-879) and the Thakuri page begins in 879; dossier runs to 879. No page gives an area, a frontier, a capital or a Tibetan overlordship; the 35 km valley radius is a reading. Cliopatria's Tibetan Empire shape does not touch Kathmandu (checked 640-800).
- thakuri-dynasty: one page; the dynasty's existence "is disputed by some historians"; end c. 1200 (infobox) or the mid-12th century (text); Nuwakot versus "southern Nepal". The Karnat Nanyadeva's attack on Nuwakot is undated on the page (no war entry).
- tomara-dynasty: 736 rests on a footnote; Anangpal II page says Delhi was founded in 1052. End 1152 (Vigraharaja IV page, manuscript date); the Chauhan dossier has c. 1151. Tomaras survived as Chahamana vassals until 1192 (Chauhan dossier should carry the tint). The pages are silent on the 1037 Hansi, 1043 Lahore and Tarain events.
- guhilas-of-mewar: infobox 'Founded: 566'; Majumdar and Somani date Guhadatta earlier; Bappa Rawal may be an epithet. Repair pass: the Paramara conquest of eastern Mewar with Chittorgarh under Munja ('later 10th century', year 975 a confidence-1 bridge) is now a loss to paramara-dynasty, the shape 975-1212 is western Mewar (60 km round Udaipur), the 1151 loss to the Chaulukyas is dropped (the 1151 change is Paramara to Chaulukya), the capital stays Nagda/Ahar to 1212 and Chittor starts 1213, and the Pratihara feudatory status (8th-9th century) is left to gurjara-pratihara-dynasty (vassal_start c. 750, vassal_end c. 900). Still undated: the Rashtrakuta refuge, Iltutmish's destruction of Nagda, and Chittor's return before 1213 (the Chittorgarh page is silent). Ends August 1303 with the Rawal branch; the Sisodia line to 1948 needs kingdom-of-mewar.
- chera-perumal-kingdom: infobox c. 844-c. 1124 (not c. 800-1102). No regnal years and no war dates on the main page; the only dated war is the Chola advance to Kollam c. 1097 and recovery by 1102. Venad, Kolathunadu, Kozhikode need dossiers.
- emirate-of-multan: ends 1010 (Ghaznavid conquest); the brief's "to 1175" is not supported by the pages (Ismaili rebellion 1041 is the only later date). Ghaznavid dossier should carry vassal_start 1006.
- bhauma-kara-dynasty: only the Bhauma-Kara page; 950 is a convention for "the 10th century"; era 736 (Panigrahi) or 831 (Sircar).
- somavamshi-dynasty: no founder or founding date on the page; 882 is the first reign in its table. 1114 is 'By 1114' (Eastern Ganga conquest); the Anantavarman Chodaganga page gives only the Sindurapura grant of 1118 claiming the whole of Utkala, so the end stays 1114 and eastern-ganga-kingdom's 1118 gain should be matched to it. Repair pass: 882-949 is Kosala only; the Bhauma-Kara coast is a gain of 950 (matching bhauma-kara-dynasty's loss); 1000 loses Dakshina Kosala round Bilaspur to kalachuris-of-ratnapura; 1021-1113 is Kosala and Utkala without southern Odisha (Kalinga title not drawn). The Jajpur page is silent on the capital move (1101 stays a placeholder). Not drawn: Karnadeva's kingdom confined to the coast between Balasore and Puri (c. 1100-1110) and the undated losses to the Nagas and the Gangas.
- karnat-dynasty-of-mithila: clean dates (10 July 1097, 1324). Previous holder of Mithila is not on the pages.
- kalachuris-of-ratnapura: repair pass: the state now runs 1000-1758 as the page says (1226-1739 is a confidence-1 bridge with the shape unchanged; 1740 Maratha invasion and acceptance of Nagpur's suzerainty; dissolve 1758 to maratha-empire). The 1114 gain is the Raipur plain from the Telugu Choda governors, not all of Chhattisgarh; the Chindaka Nagas are a separate war and Bastar is not drawn. The Ratnapura page gives no information on Pratapa-malla's successors; next inscriptions are of Vahara (15th-16th century).
- ayudha-dynasty: WITHDRAWN after review (2026-10-19). It duplicated kannauj-varman-dynasty (sa-03-c), which already carries the Ayudhas as a 770 rename of the Kingdom of Kannauj with the 816 loss and dissolve to gurjara-pratihara-dynasty; research brief rule 2 makes a dynasty change on the same land a rename. The file was removed from history/polities/ (a copy is in the scratch directory) and its new_slug is withdrawn. To pass on: kannauj-varman-dynasty may cite https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chakrayudha (Dharmapala installs Chakrayudha; brothers fight for Kannauj; end of the Ayudhas, c. 816) and already notes the c. 791 installation; pala-empire /ledger/4 and /ledger/8 should name kannauj-varman-dynasty as counterpart instead of the free text 'Chakrayudha (Ayudha dynasty of Kannauj)'.
