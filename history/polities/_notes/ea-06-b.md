## tibet

- **Life span.** Starts 1354 (Phagmodrupa, infobox "Established: 1354"), not 1363 as Cliopatria's first shape; the 1354-1362 keyframe overlaps the Cliopatria Yuan Dynasty row (the Yuan dossier is another group's). Ends 23 May 1951 (Seventeen Point Agreement), not 1952; the Tibetan government stayed in place to 1959 (Ganden Phodrang page: "Protectorate of the People's Republic of China (1951-1959)"), which the PRC dossier should show as a tint if the project wants it.
- **One state, four regimes.** Phagmodrupa (1354), Rinpungpa ministers (1435), Tsangpa (1565), Ganden Phodrang (1642) are `rename` events, as in Cliopatria's single row. The khoshut-khanate dossier's counterpart `tsangpa-dynasty` is this state; no separate dossier is needed.
- **Shape before 1642 (rewritten in the review fixes).** Cliopatria draws the whole plateau (1.55 M km2) for 1363-1641. The pages say the Phagmodrupa "in the first place wielded power over Central Tibet", were "reduced to U" in the 16th century, and that the Beri king still held Kham in 1640-41. Shapes 1354-1641 are therefore Ü-Tsang: the gazetteer's Central Tibet (the whole Tibet Autonomous Region, 4,342 cells) less Chamdo (Kham, 699 cells) and the Guge circle (173 cells), and until their conquest less Latö (113 cells, 1612) and Mangyül Gungthang (59 cells, 1619): 3,298 cells in 1354-1611, 3,470 from 1619. No page opened says who held Kham and Amdo then; a gap-filler or the Ming dossier should decide whether they are nobody's land. New facts from the Tsangpa page (for the gap-filler on the Tsangpa reach 1565-1620): the Tsangpa took Kyishö and Tsal in Ü only in 1618 and Nêdong yielded in 1620; Dagpo, Phanyul and Neu are "new acquisitions" without a date; the drawn shape still holds all of Ü from 1354.
- **Shape 1642-1726 (review fix F2).** Cliopatria's plateau less the 921 northern cells (Kokonor and the Qaidam Basin, 89.9-100.4 E 32.3-36.9 N), which the Khoshut Khanate page gives to the Khoshut home realm, and less the Guge circle until 1684. Kham (Chamdo and the plateau fringe, 1,581 cells) enters in 1642 as a gain from khoshut-khanate (confidence 2: the page says only that Güshi granted the Dalai Lama "all temporal powers over Tibet in 1642", that he crushed the Beri king in 1640-41 and was "king of Ü-Tsang and Kham" in 1653). The first version's 1724 Qinghai loss is deleted (not Tibetan land in this reading).
- **Status as vassal.** Khoshut protectorate 1642-1717 and Qing protectorate 1720-1912 (Ganden Phodrang infobox; Tibet under Qing rule: a fanbu, "vassal"; the Ming received tribute from 1373 and gave titles in 1372 and 1406). Vassal events belong to the suzerain's ledger, so this dossier records none. Needed elsewhere: qing-dynasty `vassal_start` 1720 / `vassal_end` 1912 counterpart `tibet`; ming-dynasty (ea-07) at its discretion; khoshut-khanate 1642-1717. The khoshut-khanate dossier books Central Tibet as its own gain in 1642 and the dzungar-khanate dossier books it as a gain in 1717; this dossier books the Dzungar years as an occupation of Tibetan land. The two views conflict on the map and need an orchestrator decision.
- **Gap 1794-1911.** Cliopatria has no Tibet shape (its Qing shape contains 4,300 of the 4,342 Central Tibet cells). New keyframe `tibet_1794` keeps the 1775 frontier.
