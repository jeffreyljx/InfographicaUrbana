## What the pages could not settle, per state

### southern-han (Cliopatria 911-979, 4 shapes)
- Cliopatria includes northern Vietnam to 946 (204 of 1,185 cells); the Bach Dang (938) page shows Jinghai was a Viet principality that Southern Han held only from October 930 to 931 (occupation events) and failed to retake in 938. The 947 Cliopatria shape equals the 911 shape without Vietnam (979 against 981 cells).
- The ngo-dynasty dossier (se-01) founds the Ngo in 939 "ending Southern Han rule" and takes land from southern-han; it should name the Jinghai circuit (Khuc clan, Duong Dinh Nghe, Kieu Cong Tien, 905-939) as predecessor instead. Suggested slug jinghai-circuit.
- The state runs 907-971, not 911-979. 907 is the Tang fall (infobox says established 917, Yue; renamed Han 918); the start is 907 so that the 908 loss of six prefectures to Ma Yin lies inside the life.
- Page disagreement: the Southern Han page puts Liu Yin's death in 917; the Ma Yin page has Liu Yan, "Liu Yin's brother and successor", asking for a Ma bride in 913. The rulers list starts at 917.
- 951: "in winter 951 Liu Sheng launched a successful invasion" (Southern Tang page, "to secure Lingnan"); no prefecture is named. The Guilin and Wuzhou discs are a reading of the Guangxi land the southern-chu dossier gives to Chu until 951.
- Not in the ledger because no page places it: Liu Yin's taking of Rong and Gao from Ma's man Yao Yanzhang in 911 (Ma Yin page).
- Budget: 7 pages opened for a budget of 5 (the Five Dynasties and Ma Chu pages are shared; the Ma Chu page turned out silent on Southern Han).

### southern-wu (Cliopatria 911-959, 2 shapes)
- The state is the Yang Wu, 907-937 (some historians 902). Its end: Yang Pu surrendered the seal in October 937, Xu Zhigao (Li Bian) acceded on 11 November 937 (Southern Tang page). Cliopatria therefore labels Southern Tang land as Southern Wu for 938-959; the 947 shape (495,453 km2) is verdict wrong here. The southern-tang dossier must carry 937-959 (the 911 shape from 938 and the 947 shape); Cliopatria's own Southern Tang row starts in 960.
- Cliopatria's Wikipedia field "Southern Wu" is a disambiguation page; identity.wikipedia is "Yang Wu".
- The pages give no territorial gain or loss for the Wu; the ledger has only the founding and the end. The Huai frontier with Later Liang and the Jiangxi and Hubei parts rest on the infobox area text ("central and southern Anhui, central and southern Jiangsu, much of Jiangxi, and eastern Hubei").
- Date disagreements: Yang Wo 905-908 (text) or 907-908 (infobox); Yang Longyan's death 921 (list) or 922 (events); the Five Dynasties page 902-937 (text) or 907-937 (list).

### wuyue (Cliopatria 911-979, 4 shapes)
- The state is 907-978 (Qian Chu surrendered in 978; the infobox's 988 is his death as a nominal king). The 124,000 km2 shape fits "Zhejiang, Shanghai, and southern Jiangsu".
- Cliopatria has no Fujian: Fuzhou came to Wuyue in 946 (Southern Tang page: Wuyue forces arrived in winter 946-spring 947 "to protect their new vassal"; Wuyue page: 13th prefecture; "northern Fujian after 945"). Years differ by a year between pages; 946 used at confidence 2. The Fuzhou disc (80 km) is the one the min dossier gives for Wuyue.
- Nominal ties not drawn (as in the southern-chu and min dossiers): overlords Later Liang and Later Tang; "In 960, Qian Chu submitted to Song" (Qian Chu page). The ea-05 Song dossier should decide whether 960-978 is a vassal tint (vassal_start 960, counterpart wuyue).
- Qingyuan Jiedushi (Quanzhou and Zhangzhou, Liu Congxiao) is paired with Wuyue in the 978 sentence ("gave up their land to Northern Song in 978"); no dossier yet (qingyuan-circuit).
- Not found on the pages opened: Wuyue's role in the Song conquest of the Southern Tang (975-976), any Wuyue gain of Changzhou or Suzhou, dates of Fuzhou beyond 945-947.

### yan-kingdom (Cliopatria 911-921, 1 shape)
- The state lasted 911-913: Yan page "established 911", "about two years", infobox 914; Jin page "in 913, conquered Yan"; Five Dynasties page "defeated Liu Shouguang in 915". 913 used (Li Cunxu page: siege of You Prefecture by summer 913). Cliopatria's 914-921 years are Jin land.
- The Yan page is a stub: no extent, no wars with Zhu Wen, Zhao, Yiwu or the Khitan; the shape (near Beijing) is unverified.

### former-jin (Cliopatria 922-925, 1 shape; plus the Jin years that Cliopatria files under "Later Jin" 911-921)
- Is the Jin of Li Keyong and Li Cunxu, a state 907-23 May 923 (Li Cunxu infobox, "Era name: Tongguang, May 13, 923"), not a state of 922-925. Cliopatria's 924-925 years are the Later Tang's (later-tang dossier, and Former Shu fell in late 925).
- The shared Wikidata id with "jin" (Spring and Autumn Jin) is a Cliopatria error; they are different states. The id is kept only because Cliopatria gives it.
- The Cliopatria "Later Jin" 911-921 shape (Shanxi, 71,612 km2) belongs here; the later-jin dossier marks it wrong. This dossier has new keyframes 907-923 instead (the shape ids former-jin_907, _912, _913, _915, _916, _922, _923).
- Mirror events present (requested by the ea-04-c notes): 915 Tianxiong, 916 north bank, 917 contest_start, 922 Wei Prefecture loss, 922 Zhao gain, spring-923 Zhaoyi loss. The 912 Jincheng gain ("In 912, Jin took over Jincheng", Jin page) has no matching later-liang-dynasty loss; that dossier could add one.
- The 917-923 contest_end here is dated 13 May 923 (the Jin ends); the later-liang-dynasty contest_end is 19 November 923 (fall of Kaifeng) and belongs to the later-tang dossier on the Tang side.
- Hezhong (one of the four commands lost to Later Liang by 907) has no gazetteer place; its 60 km disc is a coordinate reading (111.0 E, 35.03 N). The Yiwu lands (Wang Chuzhi, Dingzhou), allied with Jin, are not cut from the 916-921 shape because no opened page gives their extent; the shape may overstate Jin there. The Zhao disc is cut out of the 916-921 shape and added from the 922 keyframe.
- Not drawn: the Inner Mongolia parts held in 907 (page names none).

### jingnan (Cliopatria 926-969, 3 shapes)
- The state is 924-963 (infobox established 924, ended 963; Five Dynasties page agrees). Cliopatria starts two years late and ends six years late (964-969 is Song land).
- The three shapes are one outline (the 936 shape is 623 km2 smaller for no recorded reason). The extent is unverified: the Jingnan page is a stub with only the capital Jingzhou.
- Name collision: Jingnan (荆南, Gao family, Hubei) is not the Qi's Jingnan Circuit (靜難, Xianyang, Shaanxi) in the later-liang-dynasty dossier.
- The Jingnan page names the founder Gao Jixing, the Five Dynasties page Gao Jichang; the same man.
