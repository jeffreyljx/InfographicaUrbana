# se-03-c notes (Colonial, part c): what is not settled

Six dossiers written and validated (0 errors): riau-lingga-sultanate, sultanate-of-sambaliung, kedah-perlis-district, kingdom-of-sarawak, pahang-sultanate, french-indochina. Every event cites a page opened on 2026-10-19; page budget per state was used in full except Riau-Lingga, Sambaliung, Perlis and Pahang (2 pages each, the budget).

## riau-lingga-sultanate (2 pages)
- Life 1824..1911 (page), not Cliopatria's 1820..1856. The 1820 shape is verdict `wrong` (Johor-Riau under Dutch paramountcy); 1857..1911 is a new keyframe.
- Cliopatria's 1824 shape contains the whole Sumatran mainland of Riau province (297 cells); the page names only Igal, Gaung, Reteh and Mandah in Indragiri. Corrected to Kepulauan Riau plus a 70 km circle at 103.0E 0.4S.
- Disagreement with the Dutch East Indies dossier (eu/se-03): it takes the land in 1857; the page has the sultanate until the Dutch abolished it on 11 February 1911. The Indies should draw 1857-1911 as vassal.
- Reign years differ between infobox and sultan table; the table is used. Infobox gives no area.
- Gazetteer: Daik, Penyengat, Tembilahan (places).

## sultanate-of-sambaliung (2 pages)
- The English articles (Berau_Sultanate, Sambaliung_Sultanate) returned URL_NOT_FOUND. Sources: id.wikipedia Kesultanan Sambaliung and the Bulungan page (Dutch conquest of Berau 1834).
- Life c.1810..1960 (page) against Cliopatria 1830..1848. Extent and area are not on either page; Cliopatria's outline stays, unverified.
- Needs dossiers: Sultanate of Berau (before the split) and Sultanate of Gunung Tabur (the other half).
- The Dutch protectorate is undated on the Sambaliung page; 1834 comes from the Bulungan page's Berau conquest.

## kedah-perlis-district (2 pages)
- The row is the state of Perlis. Cliopatria's shape is 5 cells: Perlis (2) plus 3 cells on the Langkawi side and east; infobox area 819 km2 against 2,094 km2 (ratio 2.56). No opened page says whose the extra cells (Langkawi) were; the Kedah dossier expects this row to hold Langkawi. A page on Langkawi or Kedah is needed.
- Infobox puts the Siamese vassal state under Raja Long Krok in 1839, the text has the separation in 1842; 1842 used. Syed Hussain appointed 20 April (infobox) or 20 May 1843 (text).
- Life ends July 1909 (Anglo-Siamese Treaty, signed 10 March, effective 9 July 1909) as in the Kedah dossier. The Siamese return of Perlis in the Second World War is undated on the page and not modelled.

## kingdom-of-sarawak (5 pages)
- Life 24 September 1841..1 July 1946. Cliopatria has no shape for 1895-1945; covered by the 1890 and a new 1905 keyframe.
- Cliopatria dates gains 7 to 8 years late (1859, 1868) and lumps Baram (1882), Trusan (1885) and Limbang (1890) into 1890. Trusan: 1884 (History of Sarawak body) against 1885 (Limbang page and caption).
- Not dated by any page: the inland strip added in 1870 (95 cells) and the interior (177 cells, 1946 minus 1890); read as 1870 and 1905 at confidence 1.
- Lawas cedant not on any page: 'british-north-borneo-company' is the researcher's inference; the North Borneo page is silent on Lawas and the Sarawak boundary.
- Gazetteer: Limbang, Lawas, Trusan, Miri.

## pahang-sultanate (2 pages)
- The Pahang Sultanate article tells only the old state (to 1623); the Pahang article supplies 1853, 1857-1863, 1881, 1887, 1888 and 1895. Johor dossier dates the separation to the 1861 treaty (its page), the Pahang page to 1853.
- Life ends October 1888 (first Resident), not 1889. Infobox 1882 against text 1881 for the Sultanate.
- Succession war 1857-1863 recorded in wars with no zones (no land named).

## french-indochina (9 pages)
- Life 17 February 1859..21 July 1954. Cliopatria 1860..1957: the 1956-1957 shape holds no Indochina; the six Cochinchina provinces are drawn from 1860 but were taken in 1862 (three) and 1867 (three).
- Every shape holds 72-106 cells that are not Indochina (Réunion, French India, Tianjin concession, New Caledonia, Kerguelen from 1895); cut by intersection with Vietnam, Cambodia, Laos (and Guangzhouwan, Trat where dated). The French colonial dossiers should take those cells.
- 1941-1945: Cliopatria gives a 105-cell block round Hanoi to Japan and omits the Franco-Thai cession (Battambang, Sisophon, Siem Reap, Preah Vihear; Champasak, Xaignabouli). Recorded as occupation (extent Cliopatria's, confidence 2) and as a loss to Thailand 9 May 1941, returned November 1946.
- Page disagreements: Laos protectorate 27 March 1889 / 3 October 1893 / 1896; Laos into the Union 1898 or 19 April 1899; Japanese entry into the south 28 or 29 July 1941; the Franco-Thai War ends Jan or May 1941 by infobox.
- Protectorate monarchies without dossiers for their protectorate years: Cambodia 1863-1953, Annam/Tonkin 1884-1945, Laos 1893-1953; State of Vietnam 1949-1955 needs one; the short-lived Japanese-backed Empire of Vietnam, Kingdom of Kampuchea and Lao state of 1945 too.
- Thin or silent: Patenôtre treaty of 6 June 1884 (Tonkin page silent), Guangzhouwan lease date, the 1890 and 1904 Cliopatria steps (confidence 1), Chinese occupation limits of 1945-46, Viet Minh zones.
- Gazetteer: Guangzhouwan, Côn Đảo, Việt Bắc, Sisophon.
