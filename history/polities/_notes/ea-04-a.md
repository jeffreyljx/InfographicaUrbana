Where I disagree with, or went beyond, the review:
- Semirechye loss date: the review offered 719 or 703. The Turgesh page says 'In 703 he captured Suyab and set up his authority on the territory from Chach to Turfan and Beshbaliq'; the Anxi page says 'In 719 the Turgesh captured Suiye'. I used 719 because the 703 sentence also puts Turfan and Beshbaliq under the Turgesh, while the Anxi page has the Beiting Protectorate set up at Ting Prefecture in 702 and Chinese-commanded in 789, and the Turgesh page itself has the Turgesh attacking Ting Prefecture in 735. turgesh.json /ledger/1 (703, Semirechye, Dzungaria, Chach, Turpan) therefore does not match this ledger; ia-01 should either move it to 719 or reduce it to Suyab and Chach.
- Pyongyang: the review read the 682 shape as dropping Pyongyang with no event. A cell test shows the 681 to 682 drop in Korea is mostly Hwanghae (the 676 loss to Silla) plus about 9,900 km2 around Pyongyang; the Silla-Tang War page says the Tang 'held control over former Goguryeo territory north of Taedong River', so I did not put a Pyongyang loss in 676 (when only the Andong seat left: 'the seat of Andong was moved to the Old City of Liaodong' in the second month of 676). The Pyongyang district loss is dated 682 (the year of Cliopatria's shape), confidence 1.
- Liaodong: the review expected the Manchurian losses c. 698-705. The rest of the Cliopatria 704 to 705 difference in Manchuria and North Korea is the 705 loss. About 37 cells (9,000 km2) of the 704 to 705 difference lie elsewhere at the shape's edges and are left as drawn.
