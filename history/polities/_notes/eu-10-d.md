## Split and merge decisions

The brief asks whether the 801-year row is successive states: it is one state. The Kingdom of France page treats Capetian, Valois and Bourbon rule as one kingdom with one capital history, and rule 2 makes a dynasty change a rename event, so no split was made. The row's umbrella shapes of 1260-1375 (invalid geometry, repaired upstream) were not used; the leaf row's 96 shapes are reviewed one by one. Cliopatria's "Bourbon Kingdom of France" (slug bourbon-kingdom-of-france, 1815..1847, 8 shapes) shares Wikidata Q207162 with this row, and the Kingdom of France page's infobox carries "restorations" of 1814-1848 inside the same state. It is a separate row and slug owned by another group, and in time it follows the First Republic, the Consulate and the First Empire, so folding it in would put a found event after a dissolve. This dossier therefore ends on 21 September 1792 (the page's date) with the First Republic as successor. If the orchestrator decides the Restoration is the same state, the merge belongs to the dossier of bourbon-kingdom-of-france (a found event naming this slug as predecessor) and not here. Dynasty changes inside the state are rename events (rule 2): Capetian to Valois 1328, Valois to Bourbon 1589, absolute to constitutional monarchy 1791.

## How the Cliopatria shape is read

The Cliopatria France shape is the royal domain plus the lands the crown held directly, but it also contains lands the pages call separate: Navarre inside the shape 1285-1332 (personal union 1284-1328, removed from the solid shape and drawn as vassal_start/vassal_end, following the Tang example), Brittany from 1492 (the Crown lands page dates the union to 1532; the Kingdom of France page gives 1453/1547 for Brittany; the dossier removes it until a new keyframe of 1532-1538), Bearn throughout (cut from the solid shape in 1285-1314 and 1453-1619 after the repair, and from 1595; the Bearn page has it a de facto sovereign principality from 1356 and French personal land only from 1589, the Navarre page has Bearn and Lower Navarre incorporated in 1620), Naples through 1506 (the Italian Wars page has the French expelled after Garigliano in December 1503), Catalonia 1642-1652 (the Franco-Spanish War page has the Catalan Courts recognise Louis XIII in 1641 and Spain retake Barcelona in 1652; drawn as a vassal span), and the Duchy of Lorraine from 1700 although Ryswick returned it in 1697 and the page dates the acquisition to 1766. Colonies in the 1791 shape (Haiti 130 cells, India, Senegal, Mauritius) are cut from the metropolitan shape because Cliopatria carries them as rows of their own. England in the 1216-1219 shape is Prince Louis's brief foothold in 1216-1217; it is removed and drawn as a contest zone (1216-1217). A stray cell in central Africa in the 1220 shape is dropped as a geometry artefact.

## Page disagreements

(1) Champagne: the Crown lands page says the county was reunited with the domain at the 1284 marriage (made official in 1361); the Kingdom of France page says 1316; the dossier follows the Crown lands page and notes the other. (2) Toulouse: the Crown lands page says "the king annexed the County of Toulouse" in 1229, but the same page has the lands pass to Alfonso, Count of Poitou, and revert in 1271; the ledger shows 1229 for the Nimes sénéchaussées only, 1250 as the apanage and 1271 as the reversion. (3) Strasbourg: the Nine Years' War page gives 30 September 1681 in one place and 1685 in another; 1681 is used. (4) Cliopatria draws Toulouse and Poitou inside the shape from 1250, before the reversion, so most of the 1271 gain is a change of tenure and not of colour.
