## republic-of-venice

ONE STATE. Cliopatria's 'Republic of Venice' row (705-1796, 69 shapes, one Wikipedia article, one Wikidata id Q4948) is the continuous Venetian state from the lagoon duchy of Venetia to the fall of the republic, so it is kept as one dossier and not split; the 'spans 1091 years' flag does not apply (the doges' duchy and the republic are one state; the main page has no date for a change of constitution).
