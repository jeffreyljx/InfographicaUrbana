## Start and end
- The Qing is the Later Jin renamed Great Qing in 1636. Cliopatria keeps 'Later Jin Dynasty' to 1644 and starts 'Qing Dynasty' in 1645. The Later Jin dossier (ea-08-a) ends in 1644 and asks this dossier to take over the colour and the Joseon tributary relation. This dossier starts June 1644 (a new keyframe qing-dynasty_1644) and ends 12 February 1912 (Cliopatria's last shape is 1911; the Republic of China row begins 1912).
