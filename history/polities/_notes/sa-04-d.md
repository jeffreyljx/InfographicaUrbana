The pages contradict it: Vijayanagara won at Raichur on 19 May 1520 (Battle of Raichur) and Golconda fought Vijayanagara at Pangal in 1513. The vijayanagara-empire dossier (sa-04) should be checked against this.
- Mughal side (sa-05) should carry: Berar ceded to Murad 23 February 1596 (Cliopatria has it in 1600); Ahmadnagar fort taken 18 August 1600; Daulatabad 1633; Mahuli and Junnar October 1636; the 1636 tribute of Bijapur and Golconda (vassal_start 1636; ends 1657/1686/1687); Bidar and other forts from Bijapur in 1657; Golconda's northern strip 1656-1659; Bijapur annexed 12 September 1686; Golconda 21-22 September 1687. - Cliopatria's Aravidu/Vijayanagara row ends 1571, but Bijapur's gains of 1638-1648 (Bangalore, Vellore, Gingee) and Golconda's of 1642-1650 are from the Aravidu remnant ('Kingdom of Chandragiri', Sriranga III). Counterparts are named `vijayanagara-empire` / 'Kingdom of Chandragiri'.
