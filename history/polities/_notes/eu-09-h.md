In those gaps the land is drawn as House of Valois-Anjou (1440-1452, 43 of 44 cells in 1445), Duchy of Burgundy (1475-1481) and Kingdom of France (the rest, 71 of 72 cells). New keyframes keep the neighbouring Lorraine outline and the ledger hatches French/Burgundian occupation (1475-76, 1633-1661, 1670-1697, 1702-1713). The kingdom-of-france, house-of-valois-anjou and duchy-of-burgundy dossiers need to take these cells out.
