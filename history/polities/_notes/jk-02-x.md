The dossiers follow the Four Commanderies chain and draw only the Wonsan-Anbyon remnant of Lintun (80 km radius); the rest of the coast is the Ye's (dongye starts -82). - **Xuantu.** Its page has the 75 BCE move both to Gaogouli County and 'to Liaodong'; the first seat is only 'the northern Korean Peninsula' (Bowman via the Lintun page: east coast of northern Korea; a Shenyang theory is on the page). The retreat to Fushun is undated ('afterward' 238).

- **Daifang founding:** 204 (Four Commanderies), 'between 204 and 220' (Lelang, Daifang), 'around this time' after 204 (Gongsun Kang). End 314 (Daifang) against 314-315 (Four Commanderies).

- **Buyeo:** surrender in 494 (Buyeo page) against 491 (the goguryeo dossier's Munja bridge) and 504 (the Samguk sagi's Baekje absorption).
