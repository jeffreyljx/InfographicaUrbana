# data/ : polity and shape tables from Cliopatria v0.2.1

Generated by `tools/build_tables.py` (re-run it to rebuild; it reads the Cliopatria GeoJSON once and Natural Earth 10m admin-0 countries). Shapes of record are Cliopatria's (Seshat Global History Databank, CC BY 4.0); nothing here is corrected, only measured. Years are integers, negative for BCE; ToYear is inclusive.

## Files

### polities.csv (one row per distinct Name, sorted by first_year then slug)

| column | meaning |
| --- | --- |
| slug | `slugify(Name)` from tools/slug.py; see the collision note below |
| name | the Cliopatria Name verbatim |
| umbrella | 1 if the Name starts with "(" (umbrella or relation entity, not territory of its own) |
| relation_kind | none, umbrella, allegiance, alliance, vassalage or personal_union, parsed from the Name |
| first_year, last_year | min FromYear and max ToYear over the Name's shapes |
| n_shapes | number of features with this Name |
| max_area_km2 | Cliopatria's Area of the largest shape |
| centroid_lon, centroid_lat | lon/lat centroid of the largest shape (plain planar centroid; for shapes that straddle the antimeridian it can fall in the gap) |
| modern_countries | Natural Earth ADMIN names of the countries overlapping the union of the Name's shapes, top 3 by overlap area, ";"-joined; overlaps under 1 percent of the shape (and under 1000 km2) are ignored |
| wikipedia, wikidata | distinct values over the shapes, ";"-joined (a few Names carry more than one, listed below) |
| seshat_ids | distinct SeshatID values, ";"-joined |
| member_of | distinct MemberOf values (umbrella Names, verbatim), ";"-joined |
| components | distinct Components values (Names, verbatim), ";"-joined |

### shapes.csv (one row per feature, sorted by slug then from_year)

| column | meaning |
| --- | --- |
| shape_id | slug + "_" + FromYear, plus a letter suffix b, c, ... when two features of one Name share a FromYear (file order) |
| slug, from_year, to_year | as above |
| area_km2 | Cliopatria's Area field (spherical km2; recomputing it from the geometry agrees to 0.5 percent) |
| bbox | "minlon,minlat,maxlon,maxlat" in one quoted field, 4 decimals |
| n_vertices | vertex count of the original geometry |
| modern_countries | top 3 Natural Earth ADMIN names by overlap with this shape, ";"-joined |

### polities/<slug>.json (one per leaf polity)

`{slug, name, first_year, last_year, wikipedia, wikidata, seshat_ids[], modern_countries[], shapes[], relations[], neighbours[]}`

- `shapes[]`: `{shape_id, from, to, area_km2, bbox[4], centroid[2], modern_countries[]}` in year order.
- `relations[]`: `{kind, role, other, other_name, from, to, source}`. `kind` is allegiance, vassalage, alliance, personal_union or umbrella; `role` is this polity's side: vassal or suzerain (allegiance, vassalage), ally (alliance), partner (personal_union), member (umbrella). `other` is the counterpart's slug (a leaf slug when a leaf polity of that name exists, otherwise the rel- slug of the umbrella), `other_name` its Name, `source` the slug of the Cliopatria entity the relation came from. Years are the relation entity's own FromYear..ToYear, merged over its features; umbrella membership comes from the umbrella's Components and from the member's MemberOf, merged.
- `neighbours[]`: `{slug, from, to}`: other leaf polities with a shape alive in an overlapping year whose geometry comes within 25 km (shapely.dwithin in degrees with the tolerance scaled by cos(latitude): exact east-west, lenient north-south, so nothing is missed). from..to is the merged span of the overlapping years of the qualifying shape pairs; a neighbour with a gap in the record appears once per span. Umbrella entities are excluded from both sides.

## Conventions other builders rely on

- Slug: `tools/slug.py` `slugify(Name)`. Two Names that collide after slugifying are disambiguated here by first_year: the earlier keeps the bare slug, later ones get `-2`, `-3`. **Anyone deriving a slug from a Name must check polities.csv for these.**
- `han`: "Han" (-404..-203) -> `han`; "Hán" (-206..-204) -> `han-2`
- Modern country names are Natural Earth 10m admin-0 **ADMIN** ("United States of America", "Ivory Coast", "Czechia", "Russia", "Republic of Serbia", "United Republic of Tanzania", "eSwatini", "East Timor", "The Bahamas", "Hong Kong S.A.R."); NAME differs from ADMIN for 64 of the 258 features ("Côte d'Ivoire", "Dem. Rep. Congo", "S. Sudan").
- Relation entities (Type RELATION) carry Components that list the whole membership of the larger party (e.g. the 27 components of "(Allegiance of Swiss Confederation to Holy Roman Empire)"), so the two parties are taken from the Name, not from Components; Components are used only for umbrella entities.

### Modern-country join caveats
The polity-level modern_countries column ranks countries by overlap with the **union of every shape**, so for a state that lost a large territory the former land wins: French Fifth Republic -> Algeria, State of Israel -> Egypt (Sinai 1967..1982), Kingdom of Great Britain -> Oman and Yemen. Use the per-shape modern_countries (shapes.csv, or the dossier's shapes[]) for a given year. Polities alive in 2024 whose union-based top country differs from their 2024 shape's top country (7):
- French Fifth Republic: union -> Algeria, 2024 shape -> France (680260 km2)

## hierarchy.csv (1 Nov 2026)

Which state sat inside which, for the overlapping pairs the Phase C compiler found (`notes/phase-c/conflicts.csv`, the 408 nested, same-state and colony pairs of 500 cell-years or more). One row per child, parent and span: `child,parent,from,to,kind,confidence,evidence`. `kind` is `constituent` (a member of the parent: an Imperial Estate of the Holy Roman Empire, a Soviet republic, a province held by a satrapy), `vassal` (its own ruler under the parent's overlordship), or `same_state` (two dossiers drawing one state over these years: the parent's dossier is the one drawn, the child's cells yield). `confidence` 3 = both dossiers say so, 2 = one dossier or well established, 1 = a judgement. Written by eight Sonnet 5.5 classifiers from the dossiers (no new pages opened); their raw rows, including the `independent` and `unclear` pairs, are in `notes/phase-c/hierarchy-raw/`, and the dossier fixes they found are in `notes/phase-c/hierarchy-fixes.csv`. The compiler reads this file (rule R3): in a constituent or vassal span the child owns its cells, with status constituent or vassal and the parent as suzerain. Five rows were added by hand on 1 Nov after the colour run found them missing: the RSFSR, Ukrainian and Byelorussian SSRs (1922-1991), the Moldavian SSR (1940-1991) and the Transcaucasian SFSR (1922-1936), each a constituent of the USSR. A membership sweep the same day (two Sonnet 5.5 agents, briefs and outputs in `notes/phase-c/membership/`) added 262 rows the overlap pairs had missed, for parents whose members period maps draw inside them: the Holy Roman Empire (102 rows over its two dossiers), the North German Confederation and German Empire, the Commonwealth, the Crown of Aragon, the Ottoman vassals, Rome's client kingdoms and Italian allies, the Mongol uluses, the Maratha houses, the Mughal vassals, the princely states under the East India Company and the Raj, the Qing's Tibet and Kumul, and Achaemenid, Neo-Assyrian, Han and Tang dependants. Children that held much land outside the parent (the Habsburg monarchy, Brandenburg-Prussia) were left out, since a row tints all the child's land; tribute-only and nominal ties were left out too. The orchestrator dropped one row (Galicia-Volhynia under the Mongol Empire after 1259) and checked that no child has two parents in the same years and that no row makes a cycle.
