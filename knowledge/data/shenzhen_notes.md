# Shenzhen 150–199.9 m band: Futian vs Nanshan — research notes (2026-09-05)

## Bottom line
No accessible source gives a per-district count of completed buildings >= 150 m for Shenzhen. The only per-district figures found anywhere are for 200 m+ (Skyscraper Atlas: Futian 54, Nanshan 61, Luohu 14) and one 2021 figure for Futian at 100 m+ / 200 m+ (172 / 34). Everything below the 200 m line has to be estimated. The 150 m+ complete list exists only on Chinese Wikipedia (深圳摩天大楼列表), which is blocked in this sandbox in every form tried (zh.wikipedia, zh.m, wikiwand, translate.goog proxy, wikiless, zyhorg mirror).

## (b) Estimate of >= 150 m completed totals

Known anchors: Futian 200 m+ = 54; Nanshan 200 m+ = 60–61 (CTBUH-based Wikipedia table / Skyscraper Atlas). City-wide the 150–199.9 m band holds 271 (CTBUH: 471−200) to 282 (Atlas: 501−219) buildings, i.e. the band is 1.29–1.36 × the 200 m+ count city-wide.

Method 1 — uniform city ratio (band = 1.3 × 200 m+):
- Futian ≈ 54 + 70 ≈ 124
- Nanshan ≈ 61 + 79 ≈ 140

Method 2 — district-adjusted reasoning:
- The city ratio is inflated by outer districts (Bao'an, Longhua, Longgang), which have many 150–199 m residential towers but few 200 m+; the three core districts hold 129 of the ~219 200 m+ towers, so ~90 200 m+ towers are outside the core and the outer districts' band ratio is probably > 1.3, implying the core districts' ratio is somewhat < 1.3 (plausibly 1.0–1.3).
- Futian, however, is the oldest CBD: its 1990s–2000s office stock (Futian CBD, Chegongmiao/Tairan, Huaqiangbei, Shangbu, Bagualing, Xiangmihu, Meilin) contains an unusually large share of 150–199 m towers (e.g. the three verified above, Special Zone Press Tower, Development Center, Phoenix Building, Jiangsu Building, Investment Building, Duty Free Building, Lianhe Plaza, Times Financial Center, Rongchao Center, Tianan Cyber Park towers, plus large numbers of 150–180 m residential towers). This pushes Futian's ratio toward the upper end.
- Nanshan's 150–199 m stock is younger (Science Park, Nanshan Centre, Houhai, Qianhai, Shekou, Dachong/CR City, Liuxiandong) and the 2019–2025 pipeline in this band is Nanshan-dominated (see U/C table). Qianhai alone has added many 150–190 m office towers since 2018.

Best estimate (completed, architectural height >= 150 m, as of 2025/26):
- Futian: ~115–140 (central ≈ 125)
- Nanshan: ~125–155 (central ≈ 140)

## (c) Which is larger?
Nanshan is more likely to have more >= 150 m completed buildings than Futian. Confidence ≈ 60–65 %. The 200 m+ gap (61 vs 54) already favours Nanshan, and every proxy for the 150–199 m band (recent completions list, Qianhai/Houhai/Shenzhen Bay build-out, the residential 180 m+ tally 9 vs 7) points the same way. The counter-scenario — Futian's dense legacy of 150–199 m office towers outnumbering Nanshan's newer stock by enough to overturn a 7-tower deficit — is plausible but not favoured. The ranges overlap, so this should be reported as "probably Nanshan, not established".
